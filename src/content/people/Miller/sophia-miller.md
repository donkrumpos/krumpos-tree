# Sophia Miller

**Lifespan:** c. 1802 – ?

**FamilySearch ID:** MCXT-PV1

## Summary
Born c. 1802 in **Canada** ("Sophia Hutcherson, 48, Canada" in the 1850 census at Lockport, Niagara Co., NY, FS MCBJ-5WR; also implied by Eliza's 1880 census report, which gives her mother as born in Canada). Her maiden name comes from only one record: her daughter's 1862 Door County marriage, which names "Thomas Hutchison and Sophia Miller" (FS XRLD-HZ6). Married Thomas Hutcheson (c. 1812, England), probably c. 1829 in Upper Canada; no marriage record has been found. Mother of Eliza (c. 1829), probably Mary (c. 1831), and Rhoda (c. 1845). Don's **4x great-grandmother**.

**Parents: unknown.** The earlier link to William Miller and Margaret Laing (Rhynd, Perthshire) was **detached 2026-10-08**. It rested on a merged FamilySearch profile (KH36-ZYD / LZF4-CX5) whose Scottish family stayed in Rhynd and Perth, and no record ties Sophia to that family. The FS tree's "1805 England" birth contradicts the census. See margaret-laing.md and krumpos-tree `reports/Scottish ancestry gap research.md`. Best guess (SPECULATIVE): a Niagara-district family in Upper Canada, whether Loyalist, late-Loyalist or British settler. Niagara Millers are commonly Pennsylvania-German or Loyalist rather than Scottish.

## Niagara register — a candidate baptism [2026-10-08, session 5; PROBABLE, NOT applied to `parents`]
- **St Mark's Anglican, Niagara, 19 Apr 1801: "Sophia Miller, of William and Margery"**, bracketed with "Louisa Miller, of William and Margery" (twins?). Louisa was buried as "Looisa Miller, infant" on 25 Sep 1801. The same couple had **Margaret** (bapt. 15 Jul 1792) and **James** (bapt. 1799) baptised there. Source: Rev. Robert Addison's register as printed in *Papers and Records*, Ontario Historical Society, vol. 3 (1901), p. 17 for the 1801 baptisms; the 1792 and 1799 baptisms and the 1801 burial are earlier and later in the same register (archive.org `papersrecordsontv3onta`). **CONFIRMED that the entry exists (transcript). The identification with our Sophia is PROBABLE.**
- **For:** the same name and the same town. Our Sophia's daughter Mary Louisa was baptised at **the same church on 21 Dec 1831** to "Thomas and Sophia" (see thomas-hutcheson.md). A birth c.1800–01 is only a year off the 1850 census age (48 → c.1802). Naming a daughter "Louisa" after a dead twin would fit, but that is speculation.
- **Against / unproven:** Miller is a common name. No record links the 1801 Sophia to Thomas Hutchinson. Her marriage has not been found.
- **This is not the Rhynd/Perth William Millar × Margaret Laing family** (children baptised in Scotland 1804–23). That detachment stands. The Niagara couple already had a child in 1792, so they are also not provably the Newcastle 1799 "William Miller and Margery Laing". **Margery's maiden name is unknown**, so no Scottish link follows from this.
- **To prove it:** the Thomas × Sophia marriage record (it would name her as a spinster "of Niagara"), or a will, deed or land petition of William Miller of Niagara naming a daughter Sophia Hutchinson. LAC land petitions need a human.

### Session 6 follow-up [2026-10-08]
- **Twins [CONFIRMED, transcript]:** two independent scans of the printed register read the bracket beside Sophia and Louisa as "twins".
- **William and Margery Miller — NEGATIVE so far:** there is no St Mark's burial for either, 1792–1829 (the printed burials end in 1829). William is **not on the Old UE List** (Crown Lands copy, 1884 UEL Centennial appendix), and is not in the Niagara censuses of 1782 or 1783 (the only Miller there is Peter). He was probably not a recognised Loyalist; a garrison soldier, discharged soldier or post-1783 British settler is more likely (SPECULATIVE). Margaret's 1792 baptism falls in Addison's first batch, so her birth year is unknown.
- **Not William Duff Miller:** the "William Miller" who held a pew at St Andrew's Presbyterian in 1802–04 and was the church's secretary in 1819 is almost certainly Lt.-Col. William Duff Miller (1786–1859). He was "born in 1786 of Scottish parentage" and came to the frontier c.1802 (NHS no. 14). He cannot be the William who was baptising children at St Mark's from 1792, and **his Scottish parentage is not evidence for this family.**
- **1848:** a "Thomas Hutchinson" household of 4 is on the St Mark's Enumerator's Roll for the Town of Niagara (see thomas-hutcheson.md; PROBABLE). The same roll lists a widow "Mrs. Miller … 1". It is SPECULATIVE whether she could be Margery, who would then be c.75–80; nothing identifies her.
- FS full text, Lincoln and Niagara counties (deeds and probate): **no "Sophia Hutchinson" in 1,998 documents.** "Sophia Miller" and "Margery Miller" find nothing relevant before 1870. This is a weak negative, because coverage before 1850 is thin.
- **Next source:** the OGS Niagara Peninsula Branch book *St. Mark's Anglican Church baptisms, Niagara-on-the-Lake, 1792–1856* (1998; FS Library book, catalog 734190, not online). It runs past the 1832 end of the OHS printing, so it should show Rhoda (c.1845) and any later Hutchinson children. FS has no image of the original register, which is at the Diocese of Niagara archives (McMaster).
