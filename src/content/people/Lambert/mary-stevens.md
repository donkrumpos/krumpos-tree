# Mary Stevens

## Summary
Born c. 1801–03 at **Yardley Gobion**, Potterspury, Northamptonshire; called Mary or Mary Ann. Wife of William Lambert of Yardley Gobion (m. 18 Oct 1825), and a pillow-lace maker (1861, 1871). Probably died in the Dec quarter of 1874, aged 75. Mother of Eliza Lambert (b. 1827), so a 4×-great-grandmother of Don Krumpos.
