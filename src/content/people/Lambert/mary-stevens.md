# Mary Stevens

## Summary
Wife of William Lambert of **Yardley Gobion**, Potterspury, Northamptonshire (m. 18 Oct 1825). Mother of Eliza Lambert (b. 1827), so a 4×-great-grandmother of Don Krumpos.
