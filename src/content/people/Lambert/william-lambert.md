# William Lambert

## Summary
Of **Yardley Gobion**, parish of Potterspury, Northamptonshire. Married Mary Stevens at Potterspury on 18 Oct 1825. Father of Eliza Lambert (b. 1827), who married William Kendall and settled in Le Sueur County, Minnesota, so a 4×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos (Eliza → John H. Kendall → Mary Jane Kendall → Schmidt side).
