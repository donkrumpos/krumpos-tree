# William Lambert

## Summary
Born c. 1794 at **Yardley Gobion**, parish of Potterspury, Northamptonshire, and an agricultural labourer there all his life. Married Mary Stevens at Potterspury on 18 Oct 1825. Father of Eliza Lambert (b. 1827), who married William Kendall and settled in Le Sueur County, Minnesota, so a 4×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos (Eliza → John H. Kendall → Mary Jane Kendall → Schmidt side). Still in Yardley Gobion with Mary in 1871; probably died in the Dec quarter of 1875, aged 82, a year after her.
