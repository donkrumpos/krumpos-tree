# Thomas Hutcheson

**Lifespan:** c. 1806/1812 – 24 Aug 1854

**FamilySearch ID:** L8RF-177

## Summary
Born c. 1806–1812 in **England** (obituary age 48 in 1854 vs. 38 in the 1850 census). The 1850 census at Lockport, Niagara Co., NY, has "Thomas Hutcherson, 38, labourer, b. England" (FS MCBJ-5WP), and Eliza's 1880 census report also gives her father as born in England. The **"24 Jul 1808, Middlesex"** on his FS profile is an **unsourced legacy NFS entry** and should not be relied on. Married Sophia Miller (c. 1802, Canada), probably c. 1829 in Upper Canada. The family was still in Canada c. 1845 (Rhoda's birth) and at Lockport by 1850. Father of Eliza (c. 1829–1884, direct line), Mary (c. 1831–1884, m. Joseph Lavassor at his house in 1854) and Rhoda (c. 1843–1857). **He died at Lockport on 24 Aug 1854, of consumption, and was buried in Cold Spring Cemetery** (Grace Church register; *Niagara Mail* obituary, "formerly of this Town", aged 48). Don's **4x great-grandfather**.

## Relationships
- **Wife:** Sophia Miller (c. 1802–?, MCXT-PV1), born in Canada; her parents are unknown
- **Daughter:** Eliza Hutchinson (c. 1829–1884, K814-2YL), **direct line**. Born in Canada. Married (1) John Walker, (2) Martin Peters, (3) Henry Martin.
- **Daughter (PROBABLE-strong, now near-certain):** Mary A. (Hutchinson) Lavassor (c. 1831–1884); "Mary Louisa Hutchinson, of Thomas and Sophia", baptised St Mark's, Niagara, 21 Dec 1831. She married Joseph Lavassor on 19 Apr 1854 at the Hutchinson house on Cottage St., Lockport, with "the Bride's father" present (session 11).
- **Daughter:** Rhoda (c. 1843/45, Canada; d. Lockport 10 Dec 1857, "in her 15th year")
- **Possible children (SPECULATIVE):** Margaret Hutchinson (m. Joseph Fify, Lockport, 3 Dec 1855) and Robert Hutchinson, who witnessed Mary's 1854 wedding together (session 11)

## Niagara, 1848 — St Mark's Enumerator's Roll [2026-10-08, session 6; PROBABLE]
- The *Enumerator's Roll, St. Mark's Church: Members of the Episcopal Church of the Town of Niagara, 1848* lists **"Thomas Hutchinson .... 4"** (heads of families and number in family). It is printed in Janet Carnochan, *Names Only But Much More* (Niagara Historical Society nos. 27–28, 1915; archive.org `namesonlybutmuch2728carn`). **CONFIRMED that the entry exists (printed transcript); PROBABLE that this is our Thomas.** It is the same church as the 1831 baptism. Four in family fits Thomas, Sophia, Rhoda (c.1845) and Mary (c.17). If he is ours, the family was still in the **town** of Niagara in 1848, and the move to Lockport falls in **1848–50**.
- The same roll has "Robert Hutchins … 2", which agrees with the 1851 census (Robert, b. c.1793 England, and Maria). It also has two William Hutchisons (3 and 4) and a widow "Mrs. Miller … 1" (unidentified).
- NEGATIVE: the St Catharines Thomas Hutchinson in FS full-text deeds (1846–66) was a minor heir of Edward and Jane Hutchinson under the 1846 will of Armstrong Hutchinson, so he is not ours. There is no other Thomas Hutch\* before 1870 in the Lincoln/Niagara full text.

## Death at Lockport, 1854, and the Lockport family [2026-10-08, session 11]
- **Burial [CONFIRMED, image read]:** Grace Church (Episcopal), Lockport, NY, burial register p. 226: "1854 August 26 — Thomas Hutchinson — [age blank] — Consumption — [died] August 24th at 5 P.M. — Cold Spring Cemetery — Chas H. Platt — Services at the house, and at the grave." FS film 008273185, image 86 (ark 3:1:3Q9M-CS5F-4D1H). Found through FS full-text search with the place filter Lockport.
- **Obituary [CONFIRMED, printed transcript image]:** *Niagara Mail* (Niagara, C.W.), Wednesday, 30 Aug 1854: "At Lockport, N.Y. on Thursday the 24th inst., after a short illness, Mr. Thomas Hutchison, formerly of this Town, aged 48 years." Source: W. Craig Burtch, *The Niagara Mail: 1843–1860* (FS Digital Library, item 879418, identifier 677803, image 60).
- **Identification: PROBABLE-strong.** The date and place match the church burial. "Formerly of this Town" (Niagara) fits the 1831 baptism and the 1848 St Mark's roll. Lockport fits the 1850 census, which has Thomas there with Eliza next door. And it explains why nobody could find him after 1850.
- **Age conflict:** 48 in 1854 (b. c.1806) against 38 in the 1850 census (b. c.1812). The FS legacy "24 Jul 1808, Middlesex" is in between and still unsourced. Birth is now recorded as c.1806–1812.
- **Mary's wedding [CONFIRMED, image read]:** Grace Church marriages p. 168: "1854 April 19 — Joseph Lavassor to Miss Mary Hutchinson — witnesses Robert & Margaret Hutchinson, Messrs. [?] Rankin, Miss Webb [?] & a number of friends — parent or friend: The Bride's father — 8 P.M., dwelling of [blank] Hutchinson, on Cottage St., S. of High St. — Chas H. Platt." Film 008273185, image 63. This is the 1854 marriage that s3 searched for in Michigan and Brown Co. Held at her father's house four months before Thomas died, it makes Mary Lavassor's place as Thomas's daughter, and so Eliza's sister, as near-certain as anything without a direct statement can be.
- **Rhoda [CONFIRMED, image read]:** Grace Church burials p. 240: "1857 Decr 12 — Rhoda Hutchinson — Typhus fever, Dec 10, P.M. — in her 15th year — [died at] Mrs. Capt. Delano's [?] — Cold Spring Cemetery — C. H. Platt." Image 93. Born c.1843; the 1850 census gave 5.
- **Margaret and Robert Hutchinson (SPECULATIVE siblings):**
  - "John Walker and Margaret Hutchinson" witnessed George Ruff × Jane Harris on 20 Jul 1853 at Joseph Dunmill's (image 61). John Walker is very probably Eliza's husband.
  - "Robert & Margaret Hutchinson" witnessed Mary's 1854 wedding.
  - "Miss Margaret Hutchinson" married **Joseph Fify** on 3 Dec 1855 at Joseph Demmill's [= Dunmill's], Van Buren St., Lockport, with Hon. E. Ransom as "parent or friend" (image 67).
  - Daughter Harriet Fify was b. 6 Jan 1858 and bapt. privately 5 May 1858; her parents are given as "Joseph & Margaret (Hutchinson) Fify" (image 39).
  - No parents are named for Margaret, and she and Robert are not in Thomas's 1850 household.
- **Sophia:** no burial in the Grace Church register under Hutchinson, 1850–60, and no other Lockport entry. Still unknown after 1854.
- NEGATIVE: Find a Grave (Thomas Hutch\*, d. 1854); NYS Historic Newspapers, Aug–Oct 1854 (no Lockport papers online for those dates).
- **Scottish:** nothing new. The "Hutchison" spelling in the Niagara obituary carries no weight. A Scottish Hutchison family is buried at **St Andrew's Presbyterian, Niagara**: Geo., 1773–1831, "a native of Berwicks., Scot.", and Hannah Barrie. Source: Robbins, *Cemeteries and Graves in the Niagara District* (FS DL 1012865), St Andrew's visited 1929. **Nothing links Thomas to that family** (he was an Anglican, and every census says born England). SPECULATIVE at most.

## Session 12 [2026-10-08]
- **Robert and Margaret Hutchinson are both Canadian-born and tied to the household of Hon. Elias Ransom of Lockport. Upgraded from SPECULATIVE to PROBABLE (circumstantial) children of Thomas and Sophia.**
  - **1860 US census, Town of Lockport (index; FS coll. 1473181):** "Robert Hutchinson", 22, **b. Canada** (so b. c.1838), in the household of **Elias Ransom**. Elias Ransom was the "Hon. E. Ransom" who stood as "parent or friend" at Margaret's 1855 wedding.
  - **1860, Town of Lockport (index; film 803823):** the Fify household is indexed as **"Phebe"**: Joseph, 29, b. England; **Margaret, b. Canada**; Harriet, b. NY (2, = the Harriet baptised 1858); William, b. NY. The image (3:1:33SQ-GBS6-9Y21) was not read: FamilySearch put up a reCAPTCHA.
  - **1850 US census, Lockport (index):** Elias Ransom's household includes **Jane Harris**. She is the bride whose 1853 wedding was witnessed by "John Walker and Margaret Hutchinson". So the Hutchinson children appear to have gone into service in, or near, the Ransom household.
  - **1855 NY state census (index):** Elias Ransom's household (E.D. 5, Lockport) includes Emily J. West, a witness at Margaret's wedding. Margaret and Robert are not in it.
  - **Naturalization, Niagara Co. court, 25 Oct 1862 (FS full text):** "Robert Hutchinson", a subject of "the Queen", declared c.1860; witnesses James H. Hamilton and George C. Greene "of the Village of Lockport". Probably the same Robert. A Canadian-born British subject needed naturalization. A second Niagara Co. Robert Hutchinson (Oct 1863) is unplaced.
  - **Why PROBABLE:** Robert (b. c.1838) fills the gap between Mary (1831) and Rhoda (1843). Margaret is Canadian-born. Both stood at Mary's wedding in their father's house, and Margaret stood beside John Walker. Thomas and Sophia were poor (a labourer, consumptive by 1854), which fits older children placed out in service before 1850. **No record names their parents.** They are still not in the frontmatter.
- **Rhoda's place of death:** "Mrs. Capt. Delano's" is Lovina W. Delano, b. Vermont, wife of B. L. Delano (1860 Town of Lockport index). Rhoda was living away from home, not with a remarried mother.
- **Probate (NEGATIVE):** FS Full-Text over every Niagara Co. NY record for Hutchinson, Hutchison, Hutcherson and Hutcheson, 1850–70, covering probate estate files, wills, deeds and guardianships, finds nothing for Thomas. The 1855–58 estate files are for Clark Hutchinson (d. c.1857) and a Helen/Susie/Harry family. The deeds are for David Hutchinson's Newfane family (Robert, 13 in 1860, b. West Indies, is a different Robert). No administration was taken out for Thomas.
- **Cold Spring Cemetery:** no Hutch* burial before 1900 on Find a Grave (cemetery 64239; 15 memorials, all later). Interment records for the early sections are reported lost.
- **1860 image read (Town of Lockport, p. 112, dwelling 905/906; image 3:1:33SQ-GBS6-9Y21):** "Joseph Phifo [or Phifer?]", 29, **boatman**, $100 personal estate, b. England; **Margaret, 24 [index: 34], b. Canada**; Harriet, 2, NY; William, 7/12, NY. Read against the "2"s in "29" and "23" on the same page, the age is more likely **24 (b. c.1836)**, which fits between Mary (1831) and Robert (1838). No Sophia or other Hutchinson on the page.
- **The Scottish Hutchisons are excluded (NEGATIVE for Thomas):** the will of **George Hutchison of Grantham**, yeoman, dated 2 Sep 1841 and proved in the Niagara District Surrogate Court on 24 Sep 1841 (FS film 008130596, images 875–878; image read), names his wife **Hannah** and his children **George, David, Jane, Ann and Hannah**. There is **no Thomas**. This is the St Andrew's family ("Hannah Barrie wife of Geo."; Robbins's "1773–1831" for George is an OCR column misalignment, since he died in Sept 1841). The 1834 Town of Niagara deed of George J. Hutchison, blacksmith, and Matthew Hutchison is the same kin group. Thomas, an Anglican born in England, was not part of it.
- **Thomas × Sophia marriage (still NEGATIVE):** no indexed marriage of Thomas Hutch\* × Sophia, 1820–35, in any FS collection. No indexed baptism of a Hutch\* child of Thomas and Sophia, 1824–46. FS Full-Text for Lincoln and Welland Cos. has no "Sophia Hutchinson", "Sophia Hutchison" or "Margery Miller" before 1880. The 1813 "Sophia Miller" hit is a married daughter in an Oxford Co. will. The 1848 St Catharines "Thomas Hutchinson" is a minor heir of Edward and Jane Hutchinson (later a saddler at Lyons, Iowa, 1861), not ours.
