# Eliza Hutchinson

**Lifespan:** c. 1829 – 1884

**FamilySearch ID:** K814-2YL

## Summary
Born c. 1829 in **Canada** (Upper Canada, probably the Niagara district). Daughter of Thomas Hutcheson (b. England) and Sophia Miller (b. Canada). Don's **3x great-grandmother**. She married three times:
1. **John Walker** (b. England c.1827), by 1850 at Lockport, NY. He died in Door County c.1859.
2. **Martin Peters** (b. Ireland c.1821), in 1859/60. He died by 1862.
3. **Henry Martin** (b. 1830 near Belfast), on 12 Nov 1862 at Sturgeon Bay.

The children enumerated as "Peters" in 1860 and as "Martin" in 1870 (Elias, Mary J, Charles, John W and Eliza A) were **her own Walker children**. With Henry she had Eliphlet "Life" Martin (1869–1956, direct line) and his siblings. She died 17 Oct 1884 in Sevastopol after a stroke, when Eliphlet was about 15.

## Research Notes

### Origin [REVISED 2026-10-08]
- Born in **Canada**, not England. The 1860 census "England" and Eliphlet's 1920 report are outliers; the 1850, 1870 and 1880 censuses all say Canada.
- Surname spellings (Hutchinson, Hutcheson, Hutcherson, Hutchison) depend on the clerk, so the "Hutcheson" form carries no weight as evidence of a Scottish origin.
- **European roots through this line:** England (father Thomas). Sophia's ancestry is unknown, beyond her birth in Canada. **No Scottish link is documented** on this line. The former "Scotland via Margaret Laing" claim was withdrawn 2026-10-08.

### Marriages — RESOLVED 2026-10-08: Walker, then Peters, then Martin
- The 1850 Lockport census shows Eliza already married to **John Walker**. The Door County marriages of Charles Walker (1880) and Eliza Walker (1878) both name their parents as **"John Walker and Eliza Martin"**. The probate files show Eliza administering John Walker's estate and later Martin Peters's.
- So the older children were **Eliza's own Walker children**, enumerated as "Peters" in 1860 and as "Martin" in 1870. They were Henry Martin's **stepchildren**. The theory below that they were "Martin Peters' children by an earlier wife" is **retired**: the married-within-the-year ticks fit Eliza's *second* marriage, to Peters, in 1859/60.
- "William Portos/Peters, 18" in the 1880 household is plausibly her one Peters child (SPECULATIVE).
