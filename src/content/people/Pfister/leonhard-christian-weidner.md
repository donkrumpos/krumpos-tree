# Leonhard Christian Weidner

**Lifespan:** 21 May 1805 – ?

## Summary
Of **Waldbach** (Oberamt Weinsberg, Württemberg). Born 21 May 1805, son of **Georg Friedrich Weidner** and **Anna Magdalena Steigmann**. He married **Margaretha Gottliebin Stiegele** at Waldbach on 29 Jan 1833. With his wife **Margaretha Gottliebin Stiegele** he had children baptised at Waldbach from 1833 to 1845: twins Christoph Christian and Catharina Christina (b. 18 Nov 1833, both died within weeks), an unnamed child (buried 5 Apr 1835), **Catharina Christina** (b. 18 Jun 1836; m. Johann Philipp Pfisterer 18 Feb 1862), Friederike Christiana (b. 10 Aug 1838), Elisabetha Carolina (b. 19 May 1841, d. 1842), Caroline Juliane Christine (b. Nov 1844, d. Dec 1844) and **Christiana Christina Carolina (b. 16 Nov 1845)**, probably Annie M. Pfister. Probable 4×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos.

## Relationships

- **Wife:** [[margaretha-gottliebin-stiegele]]
- **Children:** see Summary. Two daughters married the Zuffenhausen Pfisterer brothers Philipp and John and went to Door County.

## Research Notes
- 2026-10-16 s19: burial still not found. FS index searches for Weidner burials in Öhringen parish (Eckartsweiler) 1840–1900 and for Leonhard/Christian Weidner deaths in Württemberg 1840–95 found only other men (Leonhard Weidner, Heilbronn 1891, b. 1846). His wife's 1876 burial index (Eckartsweiler) names him but gives no marital status, so whether he was alive then is unknown. A "Christian Leonhard Heinrich Weidner" was a father at Michelbach am Wald (OA Öhringen) in 1850; probably a different man (too old to be a son b. after 1833). Still on the Archion offline list.
- **2026-10-16 s18 — parents found; the couple stayed in Württemberg.** His wife was buried at **Eckartsweiler** (Oberamt Öhringen) on 12 Dec 1876, still described as his wife, so the parents did not emigrate with their daughters. The 1869 Door Co. index's "Christian × Christiana" is a loose rendering of Leonhard Christian × Margaretha Gottliebin. His own death is not indexed under Weidner at Waldbach or Eckartsweiler (tried Leonhard, Christian, Leonhard Christian). Friederike Christiana (b. 1838) has no later record in FS indexes. No Weidner born c. 1800–10 is indexed in Wisconsin.
- 2026-10-16 s17: found from the Philipp Pfisterer × Weidner marriage (see [[david-pfisterer-1796]]). His own baptism, marriage and death were not searched; whether he emigrated (the 1869 Door Co. index names "Christian × Christiana" as Cathrine's parents) is open.
