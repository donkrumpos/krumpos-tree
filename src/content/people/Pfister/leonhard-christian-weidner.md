# Leonhard Christian Weidner

## Summary
Of **Waldbach** (Oberamt Weinsberg, Württemberg). With his wife **Margaretha Gottliebin Stiegele** he had children baptised at Waldbach from 1833 to 1845: twins Christoph Christian and Catharina Christina (b. 18 Nov 1833, both died within weeks), an unnamed child (buried 5 Apr 1835), **Catharina Christina** (b. 18 Jun 1836; m. Johann Philipp Pfisterer 18 Feb 1862), Friederike Christiana (b. 10 Aug 1838), Elisabetha Carolina (b. 19 May 1841, d. 1842), Caroline Juliane Christine (b. Nov 1844, d. Dec 1844) and **Christiana Christina Carolina (b. 16 Nov 1845)**, probably Annie M. Pfister. Probable 4×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos.

## Relationships
- **Wife:** [[margaretha-gottliebin-stiegele]]
- **Children:** see Summary. Two daughters married the Zuffenhausen Pfisterer brothers Philipp and John and went to Door County.
