# Johann Georg Schober

## Summary
Father of **Margaretha Schober** (b. Apr 1801, m. David Pfisterer 1822, bur. Zuffenhausen 1836) and of **Margaretha Magdalena Schober**, who married Johann Erhard Seitter (b. 20 Aug 1795) on 1 Sep 1824 and was buried at Ludwigsburg on 13 Mar 1834. His wife was **Magdalena Pfizer** (also written Pfetzer). Probable 5×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through John Pfister Sr.
