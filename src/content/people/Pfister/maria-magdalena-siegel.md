# Maria Magdalena Siegel

**Lifespan:** 27 Apr 1766 – Mar 1814

## Summary
Born at **Zuffenhausen** on 27 Apr 1766, daughter of **Johann Friedrich Siegel** and **Magdalena Eppler**. She married Johannes Pfisterer before 1786 and had at least six children (see [[johannes-pfisterer-1756]]), among them **David (b. 28 Oct 1796)**, father of John Pfister Sr. She was buried at Zuffenhausen on 27 Mar 1814. Probable 5×-great-grandmother of Don Krumpos.
