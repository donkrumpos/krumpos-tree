# Margaretha Gottliebin Stiegele

## Summary
Wife of **Leonhard Christian Weidner** of Waldbach (Oberamt Weinsberg, Württemberg) and mother of the Weidner children baptised there 1833–45, including **Catharina Christina** (b. 1836, m. Philipp Pfisterer) and **Christiana Christina Carolina** (b. 16 Nov 1845), probably Annie M. Pfister, wife of John Pfister Sr. ("Gottliebin" is the Swabian feminine form of the name Gottlieb.) Probable 4×-great-grandmother of Don Krumpos.
