# Melchior Stiegele

## Summary
Father of **Margaretha Gottliebin Stiegele**, who married Leonhard Christian Weidner at Waldbach in 1833 and died at Eckartsweiler in 1876. His wife was **Rosina Deininger**. The indexes spell his surname Stingel (1833 marriage) and Regel (1876 burial). Probable 5×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Annie M. Pfister.
