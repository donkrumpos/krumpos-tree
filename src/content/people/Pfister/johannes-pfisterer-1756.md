# Johannes Pfisterer

**Lifespan:** 9 Sep 1756 – May 1805 (probable)

## Summary
Of **Zuffenhausen** (Oberamt Ludwigsburg, Württemberg; now part of Stuttgart). He and his wife **Maria Magdalena Siegel** had children baptised at Zuffenhausen from about 1786 to 1803: Johannes (b. c. 1786, bur. 11 Nov 1845, aged 59), Magdalena (b. 13 Feb 1790), Jacob (b. 28 Sep 1792), Jakob (b. 23 Aug 1794), **David (b. 28 Oct 1796)** and Friderich (b. 13 Mar 1803). Probable 5×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through David Pfisterer and John Pfister Sr.

## Relationships
- **Parents (probable, index only):** Johannes Pfisterer × Maria Catharina Ruhle ("Ruhlerin"), per the baptism of 9 Sep 1756. Kept in prose.
- **Wife:** [[maria-magdalena-siegel]]
- **Children:** see Summary. Only David is listed in `children`; son Johannes (b. c. 1786) shares the name of John Pfister Sr.'s aka and is kept in prose.

## Research Notes
- **2026-10-16 s18 — found from David's baptism.** The marriage of Johannes × Maria Magdalena (before 1786) is not indexed. Two Zuffenhausen candidates fit his age: Johannes b. 9 Sep 1756, son of Johannes × Ruhle, and Johannes b. 17/18 Jun 1765, son of (Jürg) Bernhard Pfisterer × Elisabetha Catharina Sigloch. The 1796 baptism calls the father "Jung Johannes" (young Johannes), which suits a son of an older Johannes. The burial of 14 May 1805 at 48 also fits 1756 and stops the baptisms after 1803. Maria Magdalena's own burial in 1814 still calls her the wife of Johannes Pfisterer, which does not decide whether he had died. Read the Zuffenhausen Familienregister (Archion; on the offline list) to settle it.
