# David Pfisterer

**Lifespan:** 28 Oct 1796 – Mar 1866

## Summary
Of **Zuffenhausen** (Oberamt Ludwigsburg, Württemberg; now part of Stuttgart). He married **Margaretha Schober** there on 12 Feb 1822. Their children, baptised at Zuffenhausen, were **Johann Philipp** (b. 6 Feb 1824), Katharina Margaretha (b. 7 Jun 1826, d. 8 Jun 1846), **David** (b. 15 Oct 1827), **Johannes** (b. 19 Jun 1830, = John Pfister Sr. of Door County), Magdalena (b. 23 Aug 1832) and Gottlob (b. 29 Dec 1834, d. 5 Jan 1835). Three sons went to Wisconsin: John and David were in Nasewaupee by the late 1850s, and Philipp, who married in 1862, followed later. He was buried at Zuffenhausen on 13 Mar 1866. Probable 5×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through John Pfister Sr., Margaret Pfister and Hazel P. Mielke.

## Relationships
- **Parents (probable, index only):** Johannes Pfisterer × Maria Magdalena Sigel ("Siglin") of Zuffenhausen, per his baptism of 28 Oct 1796 (FS NH12-845, QPKC-911M). - **Children:** see Summary. Son David (b. 1827) shares his name and is kept out of `children`. He is David Pfisterer of Nasewaupee, d. Mobile 1865 (see [[john-pfister-sr]]).

## Research Notes
- **2026-10-16 s17 — found via John's exact birth date.** An FS index search for Johannes Pfisterer b. 1830 gave a Zuffenhausen baptism with John's exact birth date, 19 Jun 1830. The family then matches everything known in Door County: sons **David** (b. 1827; the David Pfisterer whose widow Margaret Binck was called the widow of John's brother in 1903) and **Johann Philipp** (b. 1824; the obituary's "brother Phillip"). Philipp married **Catharine Christine Weidner** at Waldbach on 18 Feb 1862; she is the "Cathrine Pfisterer, daughter of Christian Weidner", who remarried in Nasewaupee in 1869 after Philip Pfister's death at Little Sturgeon. Zuffenhausen is also next to Stuttgart, the birthplace of David's wife Margaretha Kleinknecht. PROBABLE (index only).
