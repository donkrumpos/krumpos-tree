# David Pfisterer

**Lifespan:** 28 Oct 1796 – Mar 1866

## Summary
Of **Zuffenhausen** (Oberamt Ludwigsburg, Württemberg; now part of Stuttgart). He married **Margaretha Schober** there on 12 Feb 1822. Their children, baptised at Zuffenhausen, were **Johann Philipp** (b. 6 Feb 1824), Katharina Margaretha (b. 7 Jun 1826, d. 8 Jun 1846), **David** (b. 15 Oct 1827), **Johannes** (b. 19 Jun 1830, = John Pfister Sr. of Door County), Magdalena (b. 23 Aug 1832) and Gottlob (b. 29 Dec 1834, d. 5 Jan 1835). Three sons went to Wisconsin: John and David were in Nasewaupee by the late 1850s, and Philipp, who married in 1862, followed later. Margaretha died in January 1836, when John was five. David married twice more, to two daughters of Christian Schmid × Anna Katharina Reiger: **Agnes** (b. 5 Jul 1797; m. 2 May 1837; bur. 2 Oct 1840) and **Christina** (b. 30 Oct 1809; m. 26 Apr 1842; bur. 18 Oct 1846, aged 36). He was buried at Zuffenhausen on 13 Mar 1866, aged 69. Probable 4×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through John Pfister Sr., Margaret Pfister and Hazel P. Mielke.

- **Children:** see Summary. Son David (b. 1827) shares his name and is kept out of `children`. He is David Pfisterer of Nasewaupee, d. Mobile 1865 (see [[john-pfister-sr]]).

A **Gottlieb Friederich Schwarz** marriage index (QP2K-FK16) names David Pfisterer and Schober, probably daughter Magdalena's marriage; not followed. No emigration record for John (1853) or David was found in FS indexes; the son David b. 1827 has a Find a Grave burial at Mobile National Cemetery, 1 Aug 1865 (QVVS-9JQ6).
- **2026-10-16 s17 — found via John's exact birth date.** An FS index search for Johannes Pfisterer b. 1830 gave a Zuffenhausen baptism with John's exact birth date, 19 Jun 1830. The family then matches everything known in Door County: sons **David** (b. 1827; the David Pfisterer whose widow Margaret Binck was called the widow of John's brother in 1903) and **Johann Philipp** (b. 1824; the obituary's "brother Phillip"). Philipp married **Catharine Christine Weidner** at Waldbach on 18 Feb 1862; she is the "Cathrine Pfisterer, daughter of Christian Weidner", who remarried in Nasewaupee in 1869 after Philip Pfister's death at Little Sturgeon. Zuffenhausen is also next to Stuttgart, the birthplace of David's wife Margaretha Kleinknecht. PROBABLE (index only).
