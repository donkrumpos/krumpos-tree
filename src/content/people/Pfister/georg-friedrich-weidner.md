# Georg Friedrich Weidner

## Summary
Of **Waldbach** (Oberamt Weinsberg, Württemberg). With his wife **Anna Magdalena Steigmann** he had children baptised at Waldbach from at least 1794 to 1809: Ulrich (b. 13 Feb 1794, d. 1860), Johann Friderich (b. 14 Jun 1797), **Leonhard Christian (b. 21 May 1805)**, Katharina Dorothea (b. 2 Jul 1806) and Leonhart Christoph (b. 19 Jul 1809). Probable 5×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Annie M. Pfister.
