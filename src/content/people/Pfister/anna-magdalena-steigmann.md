# Anna Magdalena Steigmann

**Lifespan:** c. 1773 – Apr 1838

## Summary
Daughter of **Johann Conrad Steigmann** and **Susanne Reuter**. Wife of Georg Friedrich Weidner of **Waldbach** and mother of Leonhard Christian Weidner (b. 1805). She was buried at Waldbach on 17 Apr 1838, aged 65. Probable 5×-great-grandmother of Don Krumpos.
