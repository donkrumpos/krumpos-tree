# Margaretha Schober

**Lifespan:** Apr 1801 – ?

## Summary
Born April 1801, daughter of **Johann Georg Schober** and a mother née **Pfizer**. She married **David Pfisterer** at Zuffenhausen (Württemberg) on 12 Feb 1822 and had six children there from 1824 to 1835, among them **Johannes (John Pfister Sr., b. 19 Jun 1830)**, who emigrated in 1853 and settled in Door County, Wisconsin. Probable 5×-great-grandmother of Don Krumpos.
