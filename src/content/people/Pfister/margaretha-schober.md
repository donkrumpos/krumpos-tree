# Margaretha Schober

**Lifespan:** Apr 1801 – Jan 1836

## Summary
Born April 1801, daughter of **Johann Georg Schober** and **Magdalena Pfizer** (Pfetzer). She married **David Pfisterer** at Zuffenhausen (Württemberg) on 12 Feb 1822 and had six children there from 1824 to 1835, among them **Johannes (John Pfister Sr., b. 19 Jun 1830)**, who emigrated in 1853 and settled in Door County, Wisconsin. She was buried at Zuffenhausen on **18 Jan 1836**, aged about 34, when John was five; David then married Agnes and Christina Schmid. Her sister Margaretha Magdalena married Johann Erhard Seitter in 1824 and died at Ludwigsburg in 1834. Probable 4×-great-grandmother of Don Krumpos.
