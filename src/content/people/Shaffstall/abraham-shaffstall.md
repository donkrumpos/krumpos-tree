# Abraham Shaffstall

**Lifespan:** February 8, 1809 – July 10, 1880

**FamilySearch ID:** 2CJQ-KRM

## Summary
A Pennsylvania German farmer. Born 8 Feb 1809 and baptised 21 Apr 1809 in Lykens Township, Dauphin County, Pennsylvania (FS). His parents, Solomon Schoffstall (1777–1849) and Catharina (Haag) Schoffstall (1783–1858), moved the family to Liberty Township, Crawford County, Ohio, where both of them died (FS). Abraham married **Sally Cobb** there on 6 Jun 1834. After her death on 4 Aug 1846 he married **Susannah Coberly** (b. c.1824, Virginia) on 7 Aug 1847. By 1850 he was in Pleasant Twp, Van Wert Co., Ohio; in 1860 and 1870 he was in Salem Twp, Steuben Co., Indiana; and he died on 10 Jul 1880 in Lincoln County, Kansas.

He is Mildred (Guernsey) Martin's great-grandfather, through his daughter Catherine.
