# Charlotte Paquet

**Lifespan:** bapt. 4 Feb 1778 — ?

## Summary
**Baptized at Saint-Joseph de Chambly on 4 Feb 1778, daughter of Joseph Paquet and Marie Vigeant dit Taupier** [CONFIRMED, FS index]. She married **Louis Lavallée** at Saint-Mathias (Rouville) on 11 Feb 1805. The act calls her "majeure, âgée de vingt-cinq ans, fille de feu Joseph Paquet… et de Marie Vigeant" [CONFIRMED 2026-10-10, register image]. Their daughter **Magdeleine** was baptized at Saint-Mathias on 9 Feb 1811 [CONFIRMED, register image], and Magdeleine's 1830 marriage at Iberville names her parents as Louis Lavallée and Charlotte Paquet [CONFIRMED, image].

Through her mother she descends from the Breton immigrant **Laurent Perrier dit Olivier**. The line is Charlotte Paquet → Marie Vigeant → Jean-Baptiste Vigeant → Marie-Anne Perrier → Laurent Perrier.

## Relationships
