# Louise Lemonde

**Lifespan:** 1813 — 1895-11-28

## Summary
Born in Beloeil, Verchères, Quebec to Louis Lemonde (~1786) and Marie Anne Christine Tanguay (~1786). Married Jacques Guilluame Beaudoin. Died November 28, 1895 in Bear Creek, Outagamie County. Buried at St Mary Cemetery, Bear Creek — same cemetery as Peter Young and Elnora Surprise.

  - The only Jacques Beaudoin × Lemonde couple in the FS Quebec Catholic index is Jacques × **Marie-Anne-Christine** Lemonde. They baptized children at Saint-Hyacinthe and Sainte-Rosalie (Bagot) from 1829 to 1850, including M.-Flavie in **Jan 1837**, and were **both buried at Sainte-Rosalie (1870, 1873)**. They cannot be this family. Little Chute's "Louise Lemonde, m. 30 Sep 1828" (with mother "Marie Anne Christine Tanguay") looks borrowed from them, and the deep Beaudoin pedigree hangs from the same record.

Her stone reads only MOTHER, so the maiden name Normandin is SPECULATIVE.
