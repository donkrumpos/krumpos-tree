# Joseph Guyette

**Lifespan:** 1811-10-19 — 1902-01-11

## Summary
Born in Canada to Joseph Goyette (1788–1876, age 87) and Marguerite Bouthillier (1788) of Saint-Mathias. Married Madeleine Mary Lavallee on February 22, 1830 at Saint-Athanase d'Iberville, Quebec [CONFIRMED 2026-10-10, register image: "Joseph Goyette fils mineur de Joseph Goyette cultivateur et de Marguerite Bouthillier de Saint-Mathias"; witnesses his father Joseph and his uncles François and Dominique Goyette]. 11 children. Emigrated to Wisconsin. Died January 11, 1902 in Fond du Lac County at age 90.

Children's birthplaces show migration: Quebec/Montreal/Iberville → Plattsburgh NY → back to Quebec → Fond du Lac, Wisconsin.
