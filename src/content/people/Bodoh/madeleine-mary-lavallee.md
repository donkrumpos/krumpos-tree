# Madeleine Mary Lavallee

**Lifespan:** 1811-02-09 — 1899-04-01

## Summary
**Baptized at Saint-Mathias (Rouville), Quebec, on 9 Feb 1811, "née de cette nuit", as Magdeleine, daughter of Louis Lavallée, laboureur, and Marie Charlotte Paquet** [CONFIRMED 2026-10-10, register image]. She married Joseph Goyette (Guyette) at Saint-Athanase d'Iberville on 22 Feb 1830, when she was 19 [CONFIRMED, register image]. She died on April 1, 1899 in Fond du Lac County, the same day her daughter Julie Guyette died in Maple Creek. She was 88.

~~Born 28 Feb 1816 at La Prairie, daughter of Alexis Lavallée × Marie Amable Langevin~~: **withdrawn 2026-10-10**. Alexis × Amable Langevin are a different family. Their daughter **Marie-Anne** was baptized at La Prairie on 26 Feb 1816, and that is where Little Chute's date comes from. They also had a later Madeleine (bapt. La Prairie 25 Aug 1825). A bride married in 1830 cannot have been born in 1816 or 1825, and her marriage act names Louis × Charlotte Paquet.
