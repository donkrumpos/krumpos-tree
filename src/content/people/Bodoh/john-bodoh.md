# John Bodoh

**Lifespan:** 1882 — 1915

## Summary
Son of Jacques Guilluame Bodoh (1836–1918) and Julia Marie Balthazar (1844–1912). Married Mary Young. Three children: Leonard Bodoh (1908–1927, died age 19), Albinus George Bodoh (1914–1982), and Marie Ellen Bodoh (1906–1998). Was ~24 when Marie was born on July 10, 1906 in Maple Creek, Outagamie County, Wisconsin.

Died in 1915 at age 32–33, when Marie was only 9 years old. Buried at Most Precious Blood Cemetery, New London, Waupaca County, Wisconsin — just west of Outagamie County where Marie was born. Mary Young, widowed at ~26, later remarried John A Siudzinski.

John was one of 10 children. His siblings: Justina B (1862), George Henry (1863), Francis (1866), Edmund (1869), Malvina Marie (1871–1880), Delia Marie (1873–1896), Mary Jane (1879), Mary Alice (1887), James (1888–1980).

## Research Notes
- Maple Creek, Outagamie County location suggests the Bodoh family was in the Appleton/western Green Bay area.
- **Birth STATE = Wisconsin — CONFIRMED via 1910 US Census (2026-07-20).** 1910 census, Mukwa Township / City of New London, Waupaca Co, WI (Supervisor's Dist. 7, ED 87, sheet 12A, dwelling 165/family 190): "Bodoh, John," head, age 29, married 5 yrs, **birthplace Wisconsin**, occupation saw-mill laborer — household includes wife Mary (21), daughter Marie (3), son Leonard (2). Unambiguously our John. This corrects the earlier "might be NY, family mid-migration" read: the Beaudoin/Bodoh family was already in WI long before his birth (brother George Henry b.1863 Fond du Lac WI; sister Mary Jane b.1879 Maple Creek WI; parents married 1860).
- **Birth TOWN = Maple Creek, Outagamie Co (still probable).** The 1910 census gives state only, not town. Maple Creek is the best estimate: sister Mary Jane born there 1879, daughter Marie born there 1906, so the family was in Maple Creek across his ~1880–81 birth. A town-level confirmation would need a baptism record or the 1880/1900 census.
- **Two conflicts flagged by the 1910 census (not yet reconciled):** (1) It lists John's **father's birthplace as "Canada (French)," not New York** — our record has Jacques b. Malone, NY (1836). Likely a census informant simplifying a French-Canadian family's origin to "Canada"; keeping Malone NY as the sourced value pending review. (2) John's **age 29 in Apr 1910 implies birth ~1880–81**, agreeing with the FamilySearch tree's "1881" over our "1882" — birth year is 1880/1881/1882-uncertain; grave indexes say 1882.
- 1900/1910 census should show the household.
- **1920 census fragment** (via Guyette family genealogy excerpt) shows the post-John household: "Bodoh – Mary 30, Marie 13, Leonard 11 and Albinus 5" — confirms Mary widowed and still using the Bodoh surname five years after John's death, before remarrying John A Siudzinski (which happened between 1920 and ~1923, since Lorraine Siudzinski was born June 1923 in New London).
- **"Bodoh" is an Americanization of "Beaudin"** — the French-Canadian "Dit" naming convention: "Beaudin Dit Bodoh" = "Beaudin, called Bodoh." Search under Beaudin, Beaudoin, Bodoh, Bodo, Bodeau.
- **Beaudin Dit Bodoh lead revised:** John's father is Jacques Guilluame Bodoh (1836–1918), NOT John Beaudin Dit Bodoh (1859). The Beaudin Dit Bodoh connection may still apply further back — Jacques could be a Beaudin Dit Bodoh — or it could be a different branch. Needs further research.
- **Birth year discrepancy for Marie:** Little Chute chart shows "Marie Eleanor Bodoh (1926–1998)" but FamilySearch says born 1906. Since John died 1915, he cannot be father to a child born 1926. The 1906 date is correct — 1926 is likely Marie's marriage year to Claude confused with birth year.
- Marie had two brothers: Leonard (1908–1927, died age 19) and Albinus George (1914–1982).
- Marie's 1998 obituary (Junction City, Oregon) would confirm details.
