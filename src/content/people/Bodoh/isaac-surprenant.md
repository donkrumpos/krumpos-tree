# Isaac Surprenant

**Lifespan:** 1825-04-02 (baptism) — 1917-08-16

## Summary
**Baptized 2 April 1825 at Iberville, Quebec**, son of Louis Surprenant (1789–1834, La Prairie/Iberville) and Marie-Marthe Lebeau (bapt. 1799, Saint-Mathias) [CONFIRMED 2026-10-10, indexed Quebec parish register]. The earlier birth date of 14 Oct 1816 (Little Chute database) is contradicted by the baptism and is withdrawn. Father Louis died young at 44 when Isaac was 17. Married Julie Guyette on September 14, 1847 in Québec. Father of Elnora Surprise/Surprenant (1850–1904). Emigrated to Wisconsin. Died August 16, 1917 in Deer Creek, Outagamie County, Wisconsin at about **92** (the earlier "age 100" came from the withdrawn 1816 date).

**Surprenant** Americanized to **Surprise**.
