# Marthe Lebeau

**Lifespan:** 1799-09-12 — ?

## Summary
**Baptized 13 Sep 1799 at Saint-Mathias (Rouville), Quebec, as "M. Marthe Lebeau", daughter of Joseph Lebeau and Marie-Louise Haché** [CONFIRMED 2026-10-10, indexed Quebec parish register]. Married Louis Surprenant (widower of Josephte Hébert) at Saint-Mathias on 17 Jun 1816 [CONFIRMED 2026-10-10]. Mother of Isaac Surprenant (bapt. 1825–1917). Her father was **Quebec-born** (bapt. Chambly 1753), not a French immigrant as previously written.
