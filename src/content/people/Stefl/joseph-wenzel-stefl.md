# Joseph Wenzel Stefl

**Lifespan:** December 4, 1818 – c. 1895

## Summary
Father of Mary Katherine (Steffel) Krumpos (1845–1920). Born in Klattau, Klattau, Bohemia. Died c. 1895 in Leopolis, Shawano, Wisconsin. Buried at Saint Marys Cemetery, Leopolis, Shawano County, WI. Married to Mary Katherine Kadletz. Naturalized in Wisconsin in 1884.

## Relationships
- **Wife:** Mary Katherine "Katie" Kadletz (c. 1822, Klatovy, Bohemia – Oct 16, 1889, Oconto, WI; buried Saint Marys Cemetery, Leopolis, Shawano Co, WI — gravestone confirmed 2026-05-03) — married in Bohemia.
- **Children:** Stephen Stefl (1843–1932, Klattau, Bohemia), Mary Katherine Steffel (Apr 16, 1845–Mar 26, 1920)

- **2026-10-17 s21 — Trnčí belonged to Dolany parish until c. 1786, but Vojtěch is not baptised there either.** Porta fontium's place search (field `search_api_views_fulltext_1`, not the main text field) lists Trnčí in the **Dolany** books (01 = 1731–60, 02 = births 1760–84, 09 = marriages 1760–84, 10 = marriages 1784–1813 with Trnčí only 1784–86, 16/17 = deaths, 23 = birth index 1731–1803) as well as in Chudenice. So the Trnčí baptisms of the 1760s are in **Dolany 02**, which explains why s20 found no Štefl children in Chudenice. Dolany 02 shows two Štefl couples at Trnčí (sources above): **Wenceslaus Steffl, lodger, × Maria** (Josef 1761; twins Adam and Josef 1764) and **Thomas Steffl, farmer, × Catharina** (née Duda?; Georg 1763, Jan 1765). The Dolany birth index (Dolany 23) has **no Vojtěch/Adalbert Štefl** in 1731–1803, so Vojtěch (b. c. 1763–67) was baptised in neither Dolany nor Chudenice. Possibilities: he was born away from Trnčí (e.g. at his mother's village), or he is one of the indexed sons under another name (the twin Adam b. 1764 would be 22 in Oct 1786 and 72 in 1837; unproven and Adam ≠ Adalbert). **Wenceslaus × Maria remain the probable parents** (a lodger family, like Vojtěch's own, and a Wenceslaus of Trnčí is the father of Thomas who m.
