# Joseph Wenzel Stefl

**Lifespan:** December 4, 1818 – c. 1895

## Summary
Father of Mary Katherine (Steffel) Krumpos (1845–1920). Born in Klattau, Klattau, Bohemia. Died c. 1895 in Leopolis, Shawano, Wisconsin. Buried at Saint Marys Cemetery, Leopolis, Shawano County, WI. Married to Mary Katherine Kadletz. Naturalized in Wisconsin in 1884.

## Relationships
- **Wife:** Mary Katherine "Katie" Kadletz (c. 1822, Klatovy, Bohemia – Oct 16, 1889, Oconto, WI; buried Saint Marys Cemetery, Leopolis, Shawano Co, WI — gravestone confirmed 2026-05-03) — married in Bohemia.
- **Children:** Stephen Stefl (1843–1932, Klattau, Bohemia), Mary Katherine Steffel (Apr 16, 1845–Mar 26, 1920)

## Research Notes
- **2026-10-16 s18 — the Steffls came from Mezihoří (Švihov parish), about 12 km north of Klatovy.** The 1832 entry's "aus Mas-herz … aus Mas-horz" is the scribe's "Meshorz", and the Švihov village book for Mezihoří (Švihov 14) is headed "Mezhoř" in the same spelling. That book has four baptisms, 1789–95, of children of **Woytěch Štefl, lodger, × Maria née Tesař** (sources above). Anna b. 1789 died at six months, so **Anna b. 8 Oct 1790** is Wenceslaus's probable mother, and Jan (1793) and Katerina (1795) are her siblings. **Maria (aged 40 in 1832, so b. c. 1792) is not among the 1784–1800 Mezihoří births**, and the family has no entries after 1795, so they probably moved toward Klatovy c. 1796. **Their marriage is not in the Mezihoří marriages 1785–97.** The 1795 groom Woytěch Kriner, a shepherd, is a different man; he is also the father of the 1784 and 1787 "Woytěch Kriner" baptisms, which could be misread as Štefl. **Next (online, Porta fontium):** Švihov 06 (Švihov town, 1784–1833, marriages c. 1786–88), Švihov 04 (births 1746–84, Vojtěch b. c. 1767) and Švihov 02 (marriages 1715–84). Also check the other village books (Švihov 07–17) for a Tesař family and for Maria's c. 1792 birth. A Klatovy 1837 burial of **Anna Czuba, widow, 70, née Steffel** (Klatovy 20 p. 125) may be Vojtěch's sister (not checked).
- **2026-10-16 s17 — Anna Steffl died unmarried in 1832; no Steffl × Kadlec marriage in Klatovy parish.** The Klatovy death index (Klatovy 62, S section imgs 144–150, years 1819–1842) led to three entries (sources above): **Anna Steffel, single, 46, d. 18 Sep 1832** (cholera), daughter of Adalbert Steffel, day-labourer, and the late Maria née Te…řů(?), probably **Tesař** (the 1818 baptism's "T…ů" is the Czech genitive "Tesařů"); her sister **Maria, 40, d. 15 Sep 1832**; and **Adalbert Steffel, widower, 70, d. 31 Jan 1837**. So Anna was b. c. 1786, Maria c. 1792, Adalbert c. 1767. The parents' origin is written "aus Ma…herz(?)" (not read; a village near Klatovy?). Anna's single status means Wenceslaus was never legitimated and kept the Steffl name, which fits Joseph Wenzel. Still PROBABLE (no record yet calls him Joseph or links him to Wisconsin), kept in prose. **Vojtěch × Maria's marriage is not in Klatovy:** the groom index (Klatovy 61, S for 1706–1818, imgs 72–74) has Steffl Anton (3/749), Joseph (3/833) and Franz (15/11) but no Adalbert, so they married elsewhere or before 1784 under another spelling. **Target 2 negative:** the 1834–45 S grooms (Klatovy 61, img 76) include only Steffel Franz (16/51) and Steffel Wenzel (17/56). The latter is Klatovy 20 marriages p. 56 (img 379): **16 Nov 1846, Wenzel Steffl, 24, son of the late Anton Steffl, × Rosalia Nowak**, i.e. the known other Wenzel, not ours. Other deaths seen: Anna Steffl, 76, widow of Anton, burgher soap-boiler, d. 13 Oct 1822 (Klatovy 19, p. 27); Wenzel Steffl, 22, son of Franz, soap-boiler, d. 12 Jun 1826 (Klatovy 19, p. 52). **Domažlice villages (Domažlice 47, births 1838–47, all 161 images, name column):** no Steffl/Štefl child in any village, so neither Stephen (Dec 1843) nor Mary Katherine (Apr 1845) was born there. ### Caveat: is this the right couple? [2026-10-13]
- The **1884 Oconto marriage index** for Joseph Krumpus × "Mary Stafal" (FS XRGR-Z7X; image restricted) gives the bride's birthplace as **"Yellow, Pellson Co."** (Plzeň district?) and her parents as **"Joseph Stafal"** and **"Mary Senkola (Bohemia)"**. It gives the groom's mother as **"Catherine Kadlec"**. The Krumpos-line study (`creative/widdershins/cert-and-krampus/14-the-krumpos-line.md` §0.1–0.2, §0.10) found that Marie *Sinkule* was the groom's own mother, so the index has probably swapped the two mothers. If so, **Catherine Kadlec would be Mary Stafal's mother**, which agrees with this profile. The father's name "Joseph" agrees too. But file 14 also notes that she arrived only in 1883, and that the Stefl compilation's details for her are muddled. Until the 1884 register image is read (FHL 1292398 / DGS 007615245, on the offline batch list), treat this couple as Mary's parents only PROBABLE.
- Immigration: **Stephan Stefl, 23, b. Bohemia, arrived New York 1867 on the ship *Bremen*** (FS QVPJ-JLRN, "New York, Passenger Lists"), with a Josef Stefl, 18 (QVPJ-JLRK). Probably the son Stephen. Bremen lists give no home village.
