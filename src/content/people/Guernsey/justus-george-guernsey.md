# Justus George Guernsey

**Lifespan:** 1835 – 1860

**FamilySearch ID:** K23V-QWG (headstone photo available)

## Summary
Born 1835 at Granby, Oswego Co., New York (FS). The family moved to Indiana in 1844, and in 1850 he was 15 in his father's household in Fairfield Twp, DeKalb Co. He married Catherine Shaffstall in Steuben Co., IN (licence 6 Mar 1857). In June 1860 he was still living, in Fairfield Twp, with Catherine, John L. (3) and an infant son George. He died later in 1860 and was buried at Hudson, Steuben Co., IN (Find a Grave index). Formerly given here as d. 1859. He died at about 25. Father of John Loami Guernsey (1857–1940). Son of George Washington Guernsey and Esther Randall. Married Catherine Shaffstall. John Loami was only 2 years old when Justus George died — part of a pattern of early parental death across three generations in this line.
