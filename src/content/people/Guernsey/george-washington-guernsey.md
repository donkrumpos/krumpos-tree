# George Washington Guernsey

**Lifespan:** December 15, 1810 – April 4, 1878

**FamilySearch ID:** K23V-Q48 (portrait photo available)

## Summary
Patriotic given name suggests the family was established American by the early 1800s. Guernsey is an English/Norman surname from the Channel Island of Guernsey. Father of Justus George Guernsey. Married Esther Randall (who died young at 30).

**Session 8 (2026-10-08):** born 15 Dec 1810, Washington Co., NY (FS). He lived at **Granby, Oswego Co., NY**, where his children were born from 1835 to 1844 and where he was a head of household in 1840 (Silas Gurnsey also there). His obituary (quoted on FS) says: "In 1844 he moved to Indiana, where his wife died, and he married again". The second marriage was to **Catherine Alexander**, licence 1847, DeKalb Co., IN (St. Johns, Butler Twp; p. 132). He was in Fairfield Twp, DeKalb Co., in 1850 and moved to Ionia, MI, by 1860. He died there on 4 Apr 1878.

## Research Notes
- **Esther Randall's parents are unknown.** Her FS life sketch names "Peter Randall and Catherine Alexander". The "Catherine Alexander" is almost certainly a mix-up with George's second wife, so the whole statement is unreliable. Randall heads of household at Granby: Silas Randell (1830); Asa, Daniel and David Randall (1840). Others nearby: Jacob, John and Rufus Randall in Volney, and Jacob Randall at Lysander (1830). Esther probably belonged to one of these families (SPECULATIVE).
- "Catherine Alexander" in 1847 points to a Mr. Alexander. A Phillip Alexander was a head of household at Granby in 1840 (SPECULATIVE).
- The pedigree beyond George (Silas Guernsey → Lewis Franklin Garnsey → Rehoboth) is New England colonial. **No Scottish line is documented.**
