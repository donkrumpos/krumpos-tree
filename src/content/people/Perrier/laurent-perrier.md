# Laurent Perrier

**Lifespan:** c.1670 (?) — buried 17 Jun 1740

## Summary
Immigrant settler at La Prairie, known as **Perrier dit Olivier**. He married **Marie Bessette**, daughter of Jean Bessette and Anne Seigneur, at La Nativité de La Prairie on 5 Aug 1691 [CONFIRMED, index]. Their daughter **Marie-Anne** was baptized there on 26 Mar 1694 [CONFIRMED, index]. Marie Bessette was buried at La Prairie on 19 May 1714, and he married **Jeanne Dumas** there on 22 Jul 1714 [index]. He was buried at La Prairie on 17 Jun 1740; the index names him as Jeanne Marie Dumas/Rencontre's husband.
