# Laurent Perrier

**Lifespan:** c.1670 (?) — buried 17 Jun 1740

## Summary
Immigrant settler at La Prairie, known as **Perrier dit Olivier**. His 1690 marriage contract calls him **Ollivier Perier, son of Loran Perier and Françoise [surname hard to read], "ses père et mère demeurant à Brest"**, and he signs it "Ollivier" [CONFIRMED 2026-10-10, original notarial act, s7]. So "Olivier" was most likely his own given name; the parish registers and Tanguay call him Laurent, his father's name. He married **Marie Bessette**, daughter of Jean Bessette and Anne Seigneur, at La Nativité de La Prairie on 5 Aug 1691 [CONFIRMED, index]. Their daughter **Marie-Anne** was baptized there on 26 Mar 1694 [CONFIRMED, index]. Marie Bessette was buried at La Prairie on 19 May 1714, and he married **Jeanne Dumas** there on 22 Jul 1714 [index]. He was buried at La Prairie on 17 Jun 1740; the index names him as Jeanne Marie Dumas/Rencontre's husband.

This is Don's second **Breton** line, after Jean Bourhis: Laurent Perrier → Marie-Anne Perrier → Jean-Baptiste Vigeant → Marie Vigeant → Charlotte Paquet → Magdeleine Lavallée → Julie Guyette → … → Don. Every join from Laurent down to Magdeleine is now CONFIRMED by register images or indexed acts (2026-10-10).
