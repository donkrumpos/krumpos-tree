# Joseph Frank Krumpos
## Update 2026-10-07/08: origin re-traced (read this first)
Full research, with every source and image reference: `creative/widdershins/cert-and-krampus/14-the-krumpos-line.md` §0 (especially §0.10 and §0.11). Short version:

- **Birthplace is most probably Trojany (German *Trojan*), Kralovice district, north of Plzeň, NOT Trhanov.** The 1884 marriage index gives "Trogan, Bohemia"; the Trhanov registers 1822–67 hold no Krumpos or Kadlec at all, while Trojany has a Josef Krumpos **born and baptised 16 Oct 1846 at Trojany no. 8** (Žebnice parish book 08, image 39, Porta fontium). [PROBABLE, not proven]
- **Parents (probable): Václav Krumpos, cottager (*domkář*) of Trojany no. 8, and Marie Sinkule**, daughter of Jan Sinkule, smallholder of Hadačka no. 7, and Anna Bouda of Černíkovice. Grandparents: **Václav Krumpos, tailor (*krejčí*), and Kateřina Bláha**, of Trojany no. 8.
- **The open mismatch:** the 1884 Oconto marriage index names his parents "Ernst Krumpus" and "Catherine Kadlec". "Mary Senkola" appears in the same index as the *bride's* mother, which is very likely Joseph's own mother (Marie Sinkule) put in the wrong column. Only the 1884 register image settles it (FHL film 1292398; online image restricted; Oconto Register of Deeds call parked).
- **Siblings at Trojany no. 8:** Josefa (22 Oct 1844), **Josef (16 Oct 1846)**, Jan (21 Nov 1848), Václav (27 Dec 1850, probably d. 3 Aug 1851), Marie (Aug 1852), Anna (23 Jun 1854), Veronika (18 Jul 1856), Vojtěch (21 Apr 1859). Aunts Kateřina, Marie and Josefa Krumpos also had children at no. 8.
- **First wife: Josephine Fenzel** (Czech probably *Fencl*), from son Joseph's marriage return, Menominee, Michigan, 8 Oct 1902 (FamilySearch Michigan Marriages 1868–1925, ark 1:1:N3D3-4MB). Lawrence's 1911 Menominee marriage gives his parents as Joseph Krumpos and "Mary Stiffel".
- **Immigration 1863**, per the 1900 census (not 1867); no passenger list found.
- **1870:** probably "Crumbers Joseph", 20, sawmill worker, boarding in Oconto East Ward. **1880:** "Joseph Krumpis", Little River. **1898:** "J Krumpos" owns parcel 233, section 32, Town of Little River (plat book), among Czech neighbours.
- **Vaclav (b. Sept 1879)** is still in the household in 1900 as "Wenzel", so he did not die young.
- **Albert Krumpus**, naturalized in Oconto 4 Nov 1884, is a possible kinsman (an 1886 marriage gives his parents as James and Mary).
- The Domažlice/Chodsko section below rested on Trhanov and no longer applies to Joseph's birthplace (Mary Katherine's own origin is also in doubt: her 1884 entry says "Yellow, Pellson Co.", i.e. Plzeň region). Kept below for the record.

## Summary
Full name **Joseph Frank Krumpos**. Born October 16, 1845 in Trhanov, Bohemia (near Domazlice). Immigrated 1867. Died February 19, 1920 in Oconto, Oconto Co, Wisconsin. Married twice. Six children from first (unknown) wife, then two children with second wife Mary Katherine (Stefl), married December 6, 1884 in Oconto County, Wisconsin.

## Connections — Domažlice / Two Rivers Sister City
Two Rivers, Wisconsin lists **Domažlice, Czech Republic** as its sister city — the same Domažlice that Joseph's birthplace of **Trhanov sits ~5 km from**, in the Chodsko region. The sister-city sign Foggy drove past in Two Rivers is pointing at the Krumpos family's own origin point: Domažlice was the market/parish/administration town for Trhanov before Joseph emigrated in 1867.

- **Same emigration wave.** The sister-city partnership (signed May 2017) grew out of **Col. Matt Konop**, who recognized Domažlice as his own ancestral hometown when his advance party helped liberate it in **May 1945**. The Konop family had emigrated from the Domažlice area in the **1860s** — the same decade and same corner of Bohemia as Joseph Krumpos (immigrated 1867). The Konops and Krumposes were near-contemporaries leaving the same place.
- **The Accidental Hero** — Matt Konop's story is told in a WWII stage show, *The Accidental Hero*, tied to the sister-city relationship. Foggy knows of it and wants to see it. → https://accidentalhero.net/sister-cities-delegation-domazlice-czech-republic/
- **Visit angle:** A trip to Domažlice (sister-city exchanges run regularly out of Two Rivers) would put Foggy 5 km from Trhanov — standing where Joseph started. Parish baptism records c. 1845–47 are digitized at **portafontium.cz**.

## Research Notes
- Born in **Trhanov, Bohemia** (near Domazlice, western Bohemia). WikiTree listed as "Trogan" — anglicized rendering of Trhanov. Village is ~5 km from Domazlice, where wife Mary Katherine Stefl was from. Same Chodsko region. They likely knew each other in the old country.
- Trhanov parish records are digitized at **portafontium.cz** — search for baptism c. 1845-1847 under Krumpos/Krumpus to find parents.
- Immigrated 1867
- Died in Oconto, just one month before second wife Mary Katherine
- Little Chute Genealogy lists birth as Oct 16, 1845; WikiTree previously had May 1847 — two-year discrepancy
- Mary Katherine's death: Little Chute says Mar 25, WikiTree said Mar 26 — one-day discrepancy
- Mary Katherine's birthplace per Little Chute: Domazlice, Okres Domazlice, Plzensky (Plzen), Czech Republic
- **First wife identified: Josephine** — per 1880 Oconto County census. Age 28, born Bohemia, both parents Bohemian. Maiden name unknown. Died between 1880 and Dec 1884 (when Joseph married Mary Katherine Stefl). Age 28 in 1880 → born c. 1852.
- **Vaclav** — baby in 1880 census, 5 months old. Czech name. Not in later records — either died young or was anglicized to James Ernest (both born c. 1880).
- **Parents both Bohemian** — 1880 census confirms Joseph's father and mother were both born in Bohemia. Ancestry.com lists father as **Ernst Krumpos** (also "Krumper") and mother as **Katherine or Mary Kadlec**. If mother's surname is Kadlec, that's the same surname as Mary Katherine Kadletz who married Joseph Wenzel Stefl — the families were intermarried in the Chodsko region.
- **1880 census, Oconto County, Wisconsin** — Joseph (33, Bohemia, Farmer), Josephine (28, Bohemia, wife), Mary (9, WI), Anna (6, WI), Joseph [Samuel] (4, WI), Josephine [daughter] (2, WI), Vaclav (5 mo, WI).
