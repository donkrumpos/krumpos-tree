# Marie Vigeant

**Lifespan:** c.1747–50 — 10 Mar 1806

## Summary
**Married Joseph Paquet at Saint-Joseph de Chambly on 7 Nov 1768.** The act calls her "Marie Vigeant fille de J.-Bte Vigeant et de Charlotte Bessette… de la Pointe Olivier". The groom was the son of Philippe Paquet and Dorothée [Viau?] [CONFIRMED 2026-10-10, register image]. She had about a dozen children, baptized at Chambly and Saint-Mathias 1769–89, among them **Charlotte** (bapt. Chambly 4 Feb 1778), Don's ancestor [index]. She was buried at Saint-Mathias on 11 Mar 1806 as "Marie Vigeant dit Taupier, veuve de Joseph Paquet… décédée hier, âgée de cinquante-neuf ans environ" [CONFIRMED, register image].
