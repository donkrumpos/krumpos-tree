# Jean-Baptiste Vigeant

**Lifespan:** c.1720–23 — 4 Jun 1799

## Summary
Cultivateur of the Pointe-Olivier mission, later Saint-Mathias, on the Chambly basin. **He married Charlotte Bessette there in Nov 1743. The act names him "Jean Baptiste Vigeant fils de Jean Vigeant et de Marie Anne Olivier"** and her as the daughter of François Bessette and Marie-Claude Dubois [CONFIRMED 2026-10-10, register image]. A dispensation was granted for 3rd-degree kinship, since both descend from the Bessettes of Chambly. His father, "Jean Vigeant père de l'époux", was a witness. He was buried at Saint-Mathias on 6 Jun 1799 as "Jean Bte Vigeant dit Taupier, cultivateur de cette paroisse, époux de Charlotte Besset, décédé avant-hier, âgé de soixante dix-neuf ans environ" [CONFIRMED, image].

## Research Notes
- **Birth: c.1720–23, VERIFY.** FS indexes "Jean(-Baptiste)" Vigeant/Larose/Taupier, bapt. Chambly 30 May 1723, son of J.-B. Vigeant × Marie-Anne Perrier/Olivier. The 1799 burial age ("79 or so") points to c.1720. The 14 Jun 1720 Chambly baptism (same parents) is indexed as Louis, and Tanguay has a separate Louis who m. Thérèse Laporte 1753, so 1723 is the likelier baptism. His parentage does not depend on it, because the 1743 act names his parents.
