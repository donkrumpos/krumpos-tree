# Fred Meikle

**Emil Mielke's father, and Don's 3x great-grandfather.** The file was created 2026-07-15 and revised 2026-10-08, when the census evidence showed Fred was **German-born**.

## Summary
Fred was a farmer in the town of Sturgeon Bay and Clay Banks area of Door County, Wisconsin. He was **born in Germany c. 1848 to German-born parents**: he appears as "Fred Michaels" in 1880 and "Fredrick Mickel" in 1900 (see Sources). The census entries themselves are confirmed. Matching them to our Fred is **probable-strong**, because the 1900 household includes Oscar and Ernest, Emil's known brothers. He married Mary (Johnsen) **c. March 1871**; their 40th anniversary was celebrated in March 1911. The 1900 census says "married 27 yrs" (→ c. 1873), so the date is not fully settled.

Known children are Emil (b. 29 Apr 1881), Ernest, Oscar and Iva, all named in Emil's 1970 obituary. Probable further children are Carl H., Susan C., Josephine and Erka (1900 household), **Ella, Bertha and Julius Meikel** (Town of Sturgeon Bay, 1911), and a married daughter, **Mrs. Kloudy**. He co-built a saloon at Tornado in 1893 and was in Sawyer (west Sturgeon Bay) in 1917.

**The Meikle line is German, not Scottish.** Fred is **unlikely** to be a son of William Meikle (b. 1813, Stirlingshire; d. 1893 Sturgeon Bay). The two families only share a surname spelling.

## Relationships
- **Wife:** Mary (Johnsen) Meikle, b. c. 1858–59. The 1880 census gives her birthplace as **Germany**; the 1900 census gives **Sweden**, which fits "Johnsen" and a Swedish boarder in the household. Her maiden name rests only on Emil's FindAGrave memorial. She was 13–15 at the 1871/1873 marriage dates, so she may be a second wife, or the age is wrong. A "Mary Meikle" appears as a Ward Three property holder in a 1918 tax-sale notice (candidate for her as a widow).
- **Children:** Emil (1881–1970, direct line), Ernest (Green Bay, 1970), Oscar (Chicago, 1970), Iva (Mrs. Linquist, California, 1970), all per Emil's 1970 obituary. The probable children above are from the 1900 census and the 1911 newspapers. The WHS index birth "Unnamed Female Meikle, Aug 30, 1890, Door County" is a candidate for Iva.
- ~~**Probable father:** William Meikle~~ and ~~**Probable brothers:** Alexander, James, John~~: **withdrawn 2026-10-08.** Alexander Meikle's 1914 obituary lists every surviving brother (James, John, Dan, Thomas) and one sister (Mrs. McBride), and **Fred is not among them**, though he was alive and living a few miles away. William's 1893 obituary calls him only "father of Alex Meikle", even though the same issue carries a Fred Meikle item. The censuses then show Fred was German-born of German parents.
