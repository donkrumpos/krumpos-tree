# Mary (Johnson) Maggle

**Lifespan:** May 4, 1859 – November 14, 1938

## Summary
Born 4 May 1859 in **Sweden**, daughter of John and Mary Johnson. She came to Door County with her mother in 1873 (1920 census). She married **Fred Meikle/Maggle** (Samuel Mägli of Oberbipp, Bern) at Sturgeon Bay on 23 Mar 1874, at fourteen. They farmed at Nasewaupee and then for 37 years at Shiloh, and retired to Sawyer. She died 14 Nov 1938 in a Sturgeon Bay hospital and is buried at Shiloh. Mother of Emil Mielke, so a 3×-great-grandmother of Don Krumpos through Hazel P. Mielke.

The full evidence is in [[fred-meikle]]. This profile exists so the pedigree links to her.
