# Sarah "Sally" Cobb

**Lifespan:** August 14, 1813 – August 4, 1846

**FamilySearch ID:** KVVM-MR6

## Summary
Born 14 Aug 1813 in Clifford Township, Susquehanna County, Pennsylvania. She was the daughter of **Asa Cobb IV** and **Catherine Woods**. The family moved by wagon to Liberty Township, Crawford County, Ohio, in 1823–24. On 6 Jun 1834 she married **Abraham Shaffstall**, a Pennsylvania German. She died on 4 Aug 1846 and is said to be buried at Bucyrus.

She is **PROBABLY** the mother of Catherine (Shaffstall) Guernsey Boyce, and so Mildred (Guernsey) Martin's great-grandmother. No record names Catherine's mother, but:
- the 1834 marriage date fits;
- Catherine (b. 1840) falls between Sally's sons John Adam (1837) and Elam (1842);
- Abraham remarried only in 1847.

Catherine's 1866 marriage instead gives "William and Mary Ann Cady" as her parents; they are unidentified, and were probably guardians after Sally's death.

## Relationships
- **Father:** Asa Cobb IV (22 Sep 1781, Orange Co., NY – 6 May 1867, Liberty Twp, Crawford Co., OH; FS K2BW-CTY). He was in Clifford Twp (then Luzerne Co.) by 1810, bought land there in 1811, and appears on the Clifford tax list in 1815 and in the 1820 census. In 1823 he moved to Liberty Twp, Ohio, where he served as **Squire** (justice of the peace) for fifteen years, 1831–46. He is buried in the Woods–Cobb Cemetery, Crawford Co. (headstone pictured in Sherwood). In 1850 he was in Liberty Twp, aged 68, with his daughter Mary (21), James (16) and Laura (4). Sherwood p. 23–26.
- **Mother:** Catherine Woods (2 Jul 1783, PA – 1844, Liberty Twp; FS LHZ3-W6P), "daughter of Mary Woods" (a query in the *Boston Transcript*, 3 Jan 1934, quoted by Sherwood). Mary Woods made the 1823 journey to Ohio with them. Her father is not known. FS gives her parents as William Woods (1738–1830) and Mary Lutz (1764–1846), and Mary Lutz's as "Davie Sheehy" (b. Ireland) and "Jane Mc Lean". That chain is unsourced and internally inconsistent, so it is **not adopted**. SPECULATIVE.
- **Grandparents (Sherwood; FS):** Asa Cobb III (1750, Quaker Hill, Pawling, Dutchess Co., NY – 1808, Mount Cobb, PA) × Sarah Stephens (1753–1816). The immigrant **Asa Cobb I** was born c.1701 in "Lands", England (FS: Land's End, Cornwall). He came to New York c.1766 with four sons, and died c.1775 in Dutchess Co. **English, not Scottish.**
- **Siblings (Sherwood's list of the children when they left PA):** Dudley (1802), Asa (1804), Henry (1806), Eliphalet (1807; d. 14 Dec 1830, a Great Lakes sailor), Elam (1810), Jedidiah (1816), William (1818), John (1820), Cyprian/Cyperion (1822), and in Ohio Alfred (1826) and Mary Ann (1829, m. John Wesley Stiles 1853). **William Cobb** (1818–1881) lived at Clear Lake (1850) and Fremont (1860), **Steuben Co., Indiana**. Steuben Co. is where Abraham moved and where Catherine married in 1857.
- **Husband:** Abraham Shaffstall (1809–1880), m. 6 Jun 1834, Crawford Co., OH.
- **Children (FS; Find a Grave 100211542):** John Adam (1837–1918), William (c.1838–1850), **Catherine (1840–1902)**, Elam (c.1842–1861/65); probably also Martha J. (c.1845–46).
