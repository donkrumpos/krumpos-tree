# Mary (Daum) Schindler

**Lifespan:** c. 1846 – May 28, 1929

## Summary
Born about 1846–47 in Bavaria, daughter of a man surnamed **Daum**. Married **George Schindler** at St. Peter, Nicollet County, Minnesota, on 16 Jul 1870. Lived in Lexington Township, Le Sueur County. Died 28 May 1929 at Cleveland, Le Sueur County. Mother of Margaret "Maggie" (Schindler) Kendall.
