# Mary (Daum) Schindler

**Lifespan:** c. 1846 – May 28, 1929

## Summary
Born about 1846–47 in Bavaria, daughter of a man surnamed **Daum**. Married **George Schindler** at St. Peter, Nicollet County, Minnesota, on 16 Jul 1870. Lived in Lexington Township, Le Sueur County. Died 28 May 1929 at Cleveland, Le Sueur County. Mother of Margaret "Maggie" (Schindler) Kendall.

## Relationships
- **Husband:** George Schindler (1846–1924)
- **Children:** see [[george-schindler]]; **Margaret (1873–1937) — direct line**

## Research Notes
- **2026-10-13 s4:** her 1929 obituary's "came to America when a very young girl" conflicts with the 1920 golden-wedding notice and the 1870 manifest (aged 23). The manifest and the 1920 notice agree, so she probably came in 1870 as a young woman. Neither obituary names a village or her parents. The John Daum (Tyrone Twp) lead is still untested: nothing in the Hub connects him to her.
- **2026-10-13 s3:** Find a Grave index (FS QVGL-SH4P, "Maria Daum Schindler"): b. **Sep 1847**, d. 28 May 1929, buried **Le Center**. **Lead, possible brother:** a **John Daum** (b. 1835 Germany) farmed in **Tyrone Twp, Le Sueur Co.**, in 1880 (FS index), with wife Catherine (b. 1838 Germany) and children August (1864), John (1867) and Mary (1869), all b. Wisconsin, and Henry (1875), b. Minnesota. No record links him to Mary. His death or naturalization record, if it gives a Bavarian village, would be worth a look.
- Her parents' given names are not in the index. The death certificate (MNHS, $9 copy) may give them. Next: a Daum household in Nicollet/Le Sueur Co. in 1870, and her obituary (Le Center Leader, June 1929).
