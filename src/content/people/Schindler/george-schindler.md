# George Schindler

**Lifespan:** February 1846 – November 5, 1924

## Summary
Born February 1846 in Bavaria. Married **Mary Daum** at St. Peter, Nicollet County, Minnesota, on 16 Jul 1870. Farmed in Lexington Township, Le Sueur County, from at least 1875 to 1920. Died 5 Nov 1924 in Mankato Township, Blue Earth County. Father of Margaret "Maggie" (Schindler) Kendall, so a 3×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Mary J. Kendall.

## Relationships
- **Wife:** Mary Daum (c. 1846–1929)
- **Children:** Anna M. (1872, m. Charles W. Sycks), **Margaret (1873–1937, m. John H. Kendall) — direct line**, Augusta (1875), Heinrich/Henry (1877), Mary (1879), George Jr. (1885), John (1892)

## Research Notes
- **2026-10-14 s8 — emigration-file and record-detail negatives.**
  - The full Germans-to-America records add only the ship and port: Georg Schindler (KDW4-Z77), farmer, b. 1846 "Bavaria", ship ***Bremen***, departure port given as **Havre**, arrived New York 7 Jul 1870, manifest 00031622. Marie Daum (KD3H-B74) is the only Daum on that date. No village.
  - **Bavarian emigration files (Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek / Archivportal-D, which index the Staatsarchive's Auswanderungsakten by name):** none for a Schindler or Daum in 1869–71. The Bavarian Schindler files are Gunzendorf (Lkr. Bamberg; Andreas 1859, **Georg 1860**, Margarethe 1856; Staatsarchiv Bamberg, Landgericht ä.O. Bamberg I 37–39), Trabelsdorf (1858–60), Gerolzhofen (1856), Kulmbach (1854) and Erlach (1883–92). The Gunzendorf Georg of 1860 is ten years too early for our George, who came in 1870; he could in theory be Anna Mary's father "G. Schindler", but nothing links them. The Franconian Daum files are Protestant Muggendorf/Buckenreuth (1840, 1844, 1868). Bavaria largely dropped emigration permits after 1868, so a 1870 couple may have left no file.
  - His 1924 death record index (FDQM-J4D) gives age 78 y 10 m and wife Mary, but no parents; the certificate is on the offline list.
- **2026-10-14 s7 — the Dahlgren "G. Schindler" ruled out (PROBABLE negative).** George Schindler of Dahlgren, Carver Co. (b. 1819/20, Germany) was already in Minnesota in **1865**. That year's state census (FS SP91-T96) has him with wife **(Maria) Susanna** (b. 1840) and sons George and Henry. He is also in the 1880 census (MZ9M-1LV; parents' birthplace "Germany", not Bavaria) and the 1885 one (MQFN-M3Y). Children's births are indexed at Carver: Emma Louisa 1871 (FDCB-JV8), Harold 1877 (FDWR-PQ9). Three things count against him: (1) he had **his own son George, b. in Minnesota c. 1863**, while our George (b. 1846) was alive in Germany, and a living son is not usually given his brother's name; (2) his wife was b. 1840, too young to be the mother of children born 1846 and 1852, so he would need an unrecorded first marriage; (3) he came years before 1870. The 1860 Chaska census has a **John** Schindler, b. 1820, "Saxonia" (M4L5-MZ5), possibly the same man, which would make him Saxon, not Bavarian. No death or probate for him is indexed on FS 1880–1915. Anna Mary (Schindler) Latour Scharf's "G." father stays unidentified; her Dec 1922 certificate (MNHS) is still the best source (offline list).
- **2026-10-14 s6 — Latour × Schindler marriage image and 1880 household.** The Scott Co. marriage certificate (FS Q2M5-86H8, image 3:1:3QS7-L9CL-1988, register p. 316; **image read**): license 30 Jun 1871 to John Henry Latour and **Anne Mary Schindler**, both of Scott Co.; married **11 Jul 1871 at Shakopee** by Gregory Koering, Catholic priest; **witnesses Joseph Latour and Mary Vierling**. Neither witness is a Schindler, and no parents are named. In the 1880 census (FS MZ9G-L6F, Jackson Twp, Scott Co.) "Wm. Henry Latur" (b. 1844, Germany) lives with Maria, Anna Mary, William and John, with no Schindler in the house. The Latour children's entries in "Minnesota, Births and Christenings" (Maria 1873 F81D-CJ3, Wilhelm 1875 F81D-P38, Anna 1882 FDZG-QZN, Maria C./E. 1885, Margaretha 1887 F81X-56G) are civil birth registrations with no sponsors (the 1875 one gives mother b. Bavaria). Negative for parents.
  - **Unproven candidate for "G. Schindler":** the 1880 census has a **George Schindler, b. 1820 Germany**, farmer at **Dahlgren, Carver Co.** (across the river from Shakopee), wife Susanna (b. 1840), with children b. MN 1863–1877 (George, Henry, Ernst, Anna, Ida, Emma, Robert, Harold, Moritz) (FS MZ9M-JQS area). His age fits a father of children born 1846 and 1852, and he lived near Anna Mary. Against: he was in Minnesota by 1863, while George and Anna Mary came in 1870; his children's names sound Protestant; and Anna Mary was Catholic. Nothing links them. Test with his death or probate record (Carver Co.) or an 1870 census entry; he was not found in the 1870 index.
- **2026-10-13 s5 — the probable sister traced: Anna Mary (Schindler) Latour Scharf, 1852–1922.** Henry Latour died **2 Feb 1890** (b. 10 Nov 1843; Find a Grave index, Shakopee), and his widow **Mary Latour married John Scharf** at St. Paul on **28 Sep 1891** (FS "Minnesota, County Marriages", Q2M5-HYGN; "Minnesota, Marriages" FD8M-75V, "John Scharf of Scott Co."). The 1895 MN census (FS MQD3-8CC, Jackson Twp, Scott Co.) has Mary Scharf, b. 1852 Germany, with Scharf stepchildren and her Latour children Katharena, Anna and Magareth. In the 1900 census (FS M93P-9YX, Jackson & Louisville Twps) she is **b. Mar 1852, Germany, immigrated 1870**, mother of 9; Maggie, Josephine and Anna "Lathur" are in the house. She **died 3 Dec 1922, Jackson Twp, Scott Co., aged 70**: "Minnesota, Death Records and Certificates, 1900-1955" (FS FD3L-DDF; cert. ref. 13321, DGS 004226820 img 39, **image unavailable** on FS) indexes her as "Anna Mary D. Scharf" with **father "G. Schindler"**, spouse John Scharf (the mother is not indexed). Find a Grave **204364930** ("Anna Mary B. 'Schindler' Scharf"): b. **23 Mar 1852, Bavaria**, buried Shakopee Catholic Cemetery; it links her eight Latour children: Mary Anna Dunham (1873–1958), William Henry (1875–1964), John (1878–1890), **Katherine Rosner** (1880–1963), Anna Felten (1883–1919), Elisabeth (1885–1890), **Margaret Crescencia Block** (1887–1961) and **Josephine Johanna Pribyl** (1889–1981; the "Mrs. Ed Trybil" of 1924, an OCR slip for Pribyl). FS tree L5K4-5GM (others' work; read only) gives the same dates, "Bavaria" and immigration 1869/1870.
  - **Why she is probably George's sister:** Mrs. Rosner, Mrs. Block and Mrs. Pribyl called him "uncle" at his 1924 funeral; she was born in **Bavaria** (Find a Grave; the 1875 census's "Prussia" is the odd one out); she **came in 1870**, George's year; and her father was a **"G." Schindler**. Still PROBABLE: no record names both of them, and she was **Catholic** (Shakopee Catholic Cemetery) where George's family was Lutheran. She is not on the 7 Jul 1870 manifest index with George and Marie Daum (the "Germans to America" Schindler entries for 1869–71 have no Anna/Maria b. c. 1852).
  - Her obituary (Shakopee *Argus-Tribune* or *Jordan Independent*, Dec 1922) is not in the MNHS Hub, which has no Scott Co. papers. The St. Peter, Le Sueur and Mankato papers for Dec 1922 have nothing under Scharf/Sharf/Latour. Offline list: her certificate (MNHS, the full form may give the mother and birthplace), the obituary on microfilm, and the 1871 Latour × Schindler Catholic marriage entry (St. Mark's, Shakopee, or the Jackson Twp parish), which may give her parents.
- **2026-10-13 s4 — arrival and a probable sister.** See the golden-wedding notice and the 1870 manifest under Sources. His funeral was at Le Sueur Center on Sat. 8 Nov 1924; the *Saint Peter Herald* (14 Nov 1924, Cleveland column) and *Le Sueur News-Herald* (12 Nov 1924) report that **Mrs. Ed Trybil of Shakopee** attended "the funeral of her uncle, Geo. Schindler" and stayed with her sisters **Mrs. Nick Rosner** and **Mrs. Lewis Block** of Cleveland. Mrs. Block was **Margaret Crescencia Latour** (b. 23 Mar 1887, Jackson Twp, Scott Co., to Henry and Maria; m. Louis Block, Le Sueur Co., 28 Nov 1907; FS F81X-56G, FDDR-9LW). Her parents were **John Henry Latour × (Anne) Mary Schindler**, m. Scott Co. 30 Jun / 11 Jul 1871 (FS Q2M5-ZR43, Q2M5-86H8). So **Mary (Schindler) Latour was probably George's sister**; she married in Scott Co. a year after his arrival. Caveat: the 1875 MN census (FS MKNT-6DQ, Jackson, Scott Co.) gives her birthplace as **Prussia**, b. c. 1851, where George's records say Bavaria; "uncle" could also be looser kinship. Her death record or obituary may name their parents and village. No obituary for George himself was found in the Hub (the *Le Center Leader* is not digitized before 1931).
