# Eugénie Josèphe Goes

**Lifespan:** July 8, 1827 – ?

## Summary
Born at **Beauvechain, Brabant, Belgium**, 8 Jul 1827, daughter of **Ferdinand Joseph Goes, potter (*potier*)**, and **Albertine Schoensetters**, both living at Beauvechain in 1854. Banns of marriage with Guillaume Joseph Barette were published at Mélin on 2 July 1854. She emigrated to Red River, Kewaunee County, Wisconsin, about 1855–56. Probable mother of Joseph Barrette (1855–1933).

## Sources
- Marriage banns, Mélin, no. 20, 2 Jul 1854 — **CONFIRMED** (image read) (FS 6VSW-VB82, image 421/555). The index reads her mother as "Albertine Sehoenoessers"; the image reads Schoensetters.
- 1870 census, Red River: "Eugenia", 44, b. Belgium. 1880 census, Red River: "Virginie", 53, b. Belgium.
- A probable sister, Marie Thérèse Goes (b. c. 1829, d. L'Écluse 1893; FS 6VSH-N9NZ, index), is the daughter of Ferdinand Joseph Goes × "Catherine Schoensetters". The mother's given name differs between the two records.
