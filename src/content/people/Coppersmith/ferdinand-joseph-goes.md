# Ferdinand Joseph Goes

## Summary
Potter (*potier*) of Beauvechain, Brabant, living there in July 1854 when his daughter Eugénie's banns were published at Mélin. Probably the source of his grandson Joseph Barrette's aka "Ferdinand Joseph".

## Sources
- Marriage banns, Mélin (Brabant), no. 20, 2 Jul 1854 — image read (FS 6VSW-VB82, image 421/555). See [[guillaume-joseph-barette]] and [[eugenie-josephe-goes]].
- Grandson's birth act, Beauvechain, no. 10, 5 Mar 1855 — image read (DGS 004341930, image 165): "Ferdinand-Joseph Goes, aged 58, potter, living at Beauvechain", first witness. So he was born c. 1796–97. The child was named Ferdinand-Joseph after him. See [[joseph-barrette]].

## Research Notes
- Brabant civil registers (FamilySearch, "Belgique, Brabant, registres paroissiaux et état civil") are online and browsable. Look for the couple's marriage and deaths in Mélin and Beauvechain.
