# Hortence Meuron

**Lifespan:** 1818 — ca 1880

## Summary
Younger sister of **Désirée Juliana Meuron** (1815–1891) — the only known sibling of our direct-line ancestor. Born 1818 in Mélin to Pierre Joseph Meuron (1789–1818) and Anne Marie Barbe Logis (1791–ca 1858). **May have been born posthumously** — Pierre died February 22, 1818; if Hortence was born after that date, she never met her father. Raised by their widowed mother alongside Désirée.

**Married Eugène Joseph Baclaine, a mason, and emigrated to Wisconsin in 1856** — the same spring as her sister. The Belgian Researchers transcription of 1856 Port of New York Belgian arrivals lists "Bacleine (Meurens) Horthense," 36, destination Wisconsin, on the **Chimborazo from Antwerp, arrived New York May 3, 1856** — five weeks after the Copesmettes landed (March 29). Traveling with her: Eugène Bacleine, 40, mason, and children. Geneanet's PenBelGen tree (Door / Kewaunee / NE Brown Belgians before 1901) independently names **Marie Therese Baclaine (1848–1934)** as daughter of Eugene Joseph Baclaine and Hortence Meuron, m. Gustav Mertens 1874. Died around 1880, eleven years before Désirée — most likely in Wisconsin, not Mélin.

So the two Meuron sisters crossed together in one emigration season and settled in the same Walloon Belgian country. Whether their widowed mother Anne Marie Barbe Logis came with one of them is still open.
