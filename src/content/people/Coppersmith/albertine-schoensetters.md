# Albertine Schoensetters

## Summary
Wife of Ferdinand Joseph Goes of Beauvechain; *ménagère* (housewife) in July 1854. Given as "Albertine" in the 1854 banns (index: "Sehoenoessers"), but as "Catherine Schoensetters" in the 1893 death index of a probable daughter, Marie Thérèse Goes (FS 6VSH-N9NZ).

## Sources
- Marriage banns, Mélin (Brabant), no. 20, 2 Jul 1854 — image read (FS 6VSW-VB82, image 421/555). See [[guillaume-joseph-barette]] and [[eugenie-josephe-goes]].

## Research Notes
- Brabant civil registers (FamilySearch, "Belgique, Brabant, registres paroissiaux et état civil") are online and browsable. Look for the couple's marriage and deaths in Mélin and Beauvechain.
