# Charles Joseph Potier

**Lifespan:** 1820 — 1894

## Summary
Father of Antonette (Antoinette Marie) Potier (1859–1914). Married Clémence Josèphe Liégeois (1821–1902) in 1848. At least 8 children known from Geneanet: Victoire (1847), Félicienne Joseph (1850), Louis (1855), Pélagie (1857), Antonette/Séraphine (~1859), Louis (1862), Clémence (1865), and others.

## Research Notes
- **1860 census FOUND (2026-10-06):** 1860 US Federal Census, Town of Green Bay, Brown County, Wisconsin, post office Robinsonville. Enumerated 15 Aug 1860 by Fred S. Ellis. NARA M653 roll 1400, page 240, lines 13–20, dwelling 1813. Charles Pottier 40, farmer ($200 / $40); Clemina 33; Victorie 12; Felicie 9; Pelagie 2 (b. Wisconsin); **Antoinett 2/12** (b. Wisconsin, ~spring 1860) — Antonette Potier, Odile Barrette's mother. Also in the dwelling: Marie Henri 36 and **Seraphine Henri 1** — which likely resolves the Antonette/Séraphine question below: Séraphine was a different child, not Antonette's alternate name.
- **Probable parents, unconfirmed:** dwelling 1814, next door in 1860, is Ferdinand Pottier 72 (b. ~1788) and Victoire 66, farmers, with Jacques 32, Xavier 26 and Ferdinand 20. Charles's parents are unknown; this household is the strongest candidate. Confirm via Geneanet/PenBelGen or a Belgian baptism.
- **Two direct lines as neighbors:** dwelling 1811, two doors away, is Alexis Copesmette's family, with 17-year-old Isidore. Isidore's son Frank married Antonette's daughter Odile. See [[alexis-copesmette]].
- Antonette may be listed as Séraphine Potier (ca 1859) on Geneanet — same birth year, possibly a baptismal vs. civil name difference.
- Potier and Liégeois are French/Belgian surnames — likely emigrated from Belgium to Wisconsin.
