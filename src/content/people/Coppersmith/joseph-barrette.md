# Joseph Barrette

**Lifespan:** March 4, 1855 — February 18, 1933

## Summary
Born **Ferdinand-Joseph Barette** at **Beauvechain, Brabant, Belgium**, on 4 Mar 1855, son of Guillaume-Joseph Barette and Eugénie-Joseph Goes (civil birth act no. 10, image read). He came to Wisconsin with his parents as an infant (1855–56) and was known as Joseph. Father of Odile Clemens (Barrette) Coppersmith (1883–1972). Married Antonette Potier (1859–1914).

Ten children per Geneanet: Clemence Josephine (1880), August (1882), Odile C. (1883), Oliver J. (1885), Jules J. (1887), Clemence (1889 — name reused, first likely died young), Gustave William (1890), Louis Joseph (1893), Emma (1894), Joseph (1897).

## Research Notes

### Birth act, Beauvechain, 5 Mar 1855 [2026-10-13 s3] — parents CONFIRMED
- **Act no. 10, registered 5 March 1855 at 4 p.m.** before Erasme-Joseph Duchateau, alderman and civil registrar of Beauvechain (arr. Nivelles). **Guillaume-Joseph Barette**, aged 27, farmer (*cultivateur*), born at **Mélin**, living at Beauvechain, presented a boy born "yesterday" (**4 March 1855**) at 11 p.m., "of him, the declarant, and of **Eugénie-Joseph Goes**, his wife, aged 28, housewife (*ménagère*), living at the same place", and named him **Ferdinand-Joseph**. Witnesses: **Ferdinand-Joseph Goes**, 58, potter (*potier*), of Beauvechain (the maternal grandfather), and Jean-Joseph Frix, 67, rural policeman (*garde champêtre*). Signed "Guillaume-J Barette", the witnesses and Duchateau.
- Every detail fits the 1854 banns: the father's age and Mélin birth, the mother's age, and the grandfather Ferdinand Joseph Goes, potter of Beauvechain. It also explains Joseph's aka "Ferdinand Joseph". He was named for his maternal grandfather.
- **Date conflict:** the act gives birth on **4 Mar 1855**. The Find a Grave / BillionGraves index (QKD2-5448, QVLL-VN9L) gives 22 Jan 1855. The civil act was made the next day and is the better source, so the birth date is now 4 Mar 1855. The gravestone date may come from family memory.
- The 1855 table has one other Barette, act no. 33 (Jules-Joseph, son of the carpenter Jean-Joseph Barette × Marie-Thérèse De…), a different family.
- Next (optional): the **Beauvechain population register 1847–1866** (FS DGS 008189290, 432 images, online) should show the household and its departure for America in 1855–56.
