# Joseph Barrette

**Lifespan:** January 22, 1855 — February 18, 1933

## Summary
Father of Odile Clemens (Barrette) Coppersmith (1883–1972). Also listed as Ferdinand Joseph Barrette on some records. Married Antonette Potier (1859–1914).

Ten children per Geneanet: Clemence Josephine (1880), August (1882), Odile C. (1883), Oliver J. (1885), Jules J. (1887), Clemence (1889 — name reused, first likely died young), Gustave William (1890), Louis Joseph (1893), Emma (1894), Joseph (1897).

## Research Notes

### Parents — Guillaume Joseph Barette × Eugénie Goes [2026-10-13]
- **1870 US census, Red River, Kewaunee Co.** (FS household of William Barrette): William 43 and Eugenia 44, both b. Belgium; **Joseph 15, b. Belgium**; then Mary 10, Augustina 8, Prosper 6, Octavia 4 and William 2, all b. Wisconsin. Joseph is the only child born in Belgium.
- **1880 census, Red River** (FS): Guillaume Barette 52 and "Virginie" 53, both b. Belgium, with Marie, Augustin, Prosper, Octavie and Guillaume. Same family; Joseph had left home.
- **1910 census** (Green Bay Ward 4): Joseph b. 1855 Belgium, **immigrated 1856**. William Barrette Sr. (1910, Red River) immigrated 1855.
- **Find a Grave / BillionGraves index** (FS QKD2-5448, QVLL-VN9L): Joseph b. **22 Jan 1855**, d. **18 Feb 1933**. Guillaume Barrette (FS QVVH-BR23): b. **18 Feb 1829**, d. 4 Jun 1916, buried Duvall.
- **Marriage banns, Mélin (Brabant), no. 20, Sunday 2 July 1854 — IMAGE READ** (FS 6VSW-VB82; "Mélin. Marriage Banns 1805–1870", image 421 of 555): "Guillaume Joseph Barette, né à Mélin le dix-huit février mil huit cent vingt-neuf, … fils majeur de feu Jean François Barette et de feue Marie Josèphe Meulema[n]s" and "Eugénie Josèphe Goes, née à Beauvechain le huit juillet mil huit cent vingt-sept, sans profession, y domiciliée, fille majeure de Ferdinand Joseph Goes, potier, et de Albertine Schoensetters, ménagère, conjoints domiciliés audit Beauvechain." The groom's birth date matches the Duvall gravestone date exactly. His 1829 Mélin birth is indexed as FS 6VSQ-4YTD (20 Feb 1829; parents Jean François Barette × Marie Joseph Meulemens). The FamilySearch tree already joins this man to the Wisconsin Guillaume (KN7J-D6Q, 1829–1916).
- **Why PROBABLE, not CONFIRMED:** the couple is CONFIRMED as the Red River William × Eugenia (birth date, birthplace, spouse's name and age). Joseph's place in the family rests on the 1870 household, born about 6½ months after the banns, plus his aka **"Ferdinand Joseph"**, his maternal grandfather's name. His own Jan 1855 birth or baptism (probably Beauvechain or Mélin) is **not indexed** on FamilySearch, so the next step is to browse the 1855 Beauvechain and Mélin birth registers.
- **Mother's name variants:** Eugénie (banns), Eugenia (1870), Virginie (1880). Brother Prosper's 1902 Wisconsin death record (FS XLHZ-FJP) gives his parents as "Wm. Barette" and "A. Mannwell", both b. Belgium. The "Mannwell" looks like a garble (cf. the grandmother Meulemans).
- **Not the same woman:** an Eugénie Goes × Jean Joseph Leurquin had children at Saint-Jean-Geest in 1855–56 (FS 6VSQ-QN4T, 6VSQ-QGKP). That is a different Eugénie Goes.
- **Supersedes the open lead below:** Joseph is not a son of Clement Joseph Barrette. Both families came from the same Beauvechain–Mélin cluster, so they may well be kin.
