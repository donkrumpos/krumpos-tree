# Asa W. Linson

**Lifespan:** February 1814 – December 20, 1889

**FamilySearch ID:** L7JK-338 (16 sources; no parents)

## Summary
Born February 1814 in New York. He married Hannah Walker in 1842, in New York according to the FS tree. Their children were born in **Indiana** from 1843 to about 1854, then in **Michigan** from 1856. He was in Bushnell Township, Montcalm Co., in 1870 and in Ionia, Ionia Co., in 1880, the same town as his daughter Almeda and son-in-law John Loami Guernsey. He died at Fair Plain Township, Montcalm Co., on 20 Dec 1889. Almeda died at his house in 1881.
