# Asa W. Linson

**Lifespan:** February 1814 – December 20, 1889

**FamilySearch ID:** L7JK-338 (16 sources; no parents)

## Summary
Born February 1814 in New York. **His father was born in England and his mother in New York** (1880 census). He married Hannah Walker about 1842. Their first two daughters were born in New York (c.1843–45). By 1847 the family was in **Fairfield Township, DeKalb County, Indiana**, where they lived next door to Ezra Guernsey in 1850. Their children were born in Indiana until about 1854 and in **Michigan** from 1856. He was in Bushnell Twp, Montcalm Co., in 1870 and in Ionia in 1880, the same town as his daughter Almeda and son-in-law John Loami Guernsey. He died at Fair Plain Twp, Montcalm Co., on 20 Dec 1889 of paralysis. The register gives his parents as "not known". Almeda had died at his house in 1881.

## Research Notes
- **Origin:** the father was English-born, probably an immigrant of c.1790–1813, and the mother New York-born. That makes Asa first-generation American on the paternal side. **No Scottish evidence.** Linson is an English surname (Lincolnshire, Northamptonshire).
- **Susan A. (Linson) Horton**, the possible sister: her 1900 Michigan death certificate (Bangor, Van Buren Co.; image read) gives her as born 1 Mar 1817, married at 18, mother of 12 children with 7 living. It gives her father as "Linson", **born Vermont**, and her mother's name as unknown, **born England**. Her son N. C. Horton was the informant. Asa's 1880 census has the reverse: father England, mother New York. So the sibling link is still **SPECULATIVE**. The two may be cousins, or one informant swapped the parents. Geographically it fits: Susan lived at Lysander, Onondaga Co., in 1850, next to Granby, Oswego Co., where the Guernseys lived before Indiana.
- **NEGATIVE:** no Linson, Lindson, Linsen, Lenson, Linsom or Lynson head of household in the 1830 or 1840 NY censuses (FS index).
- The "1860 residence, New Britain, Hartford Co., CT" on his FS profile belongs to a **different man**, Asa Linerson (b. 1811 CT).
- **1860 census:** still not found.
