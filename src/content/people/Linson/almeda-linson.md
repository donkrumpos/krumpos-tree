# Almeda Linson

**Lifespan:** c. 1859 – February 4, 1881

**FamilySearch ID:** L2SJ-TYB (filed there as "Almeda Hinson"); duplicate GYKC-JT2 ("Almon Grumsey", wrongly made a son of Asa and Hannah)

## Summary
Born about 1859–61 in Michigan, daughter of **Asa W. Linson** and **Hannah Walker**, both New York-born. She married John Loami Guernsey about 1879, and their daughter Mildred was born at Ionia on 21 March 1880. Almeda died on **4 February 1881**, aged 22, at her parents' home in Fair Plain Township, Montcalm County. The cause recorded was "heart disease". Three days earlier her newborn daughter had died unnamed, aged 3 days. Mildred was 10 months old.

**Corrected 2026-10-08 (Scottish-ancestry research, session 7):** the surname was formerly given as "Hinson" and the death as 1883. Both were wrong. See krumpos-tree `research_notes/Scottish ancestry gap research/followup_2026-10-08_s7.md`.

## Research Notes
- **Scottish question:** nothing Scottish documented. Linson is a rare name found in New York, Ohio and Indiana, and Walker is common in both England and Scotland. Asa's father was English-born (1880 census). Hannah's parents were John Walker and Catherine Finkle, both New York-born (1907 death certificate). See their files.
- Two Guernsey men married Linson sisters: John Loami (Almeda) and **his uncle** Dennis (Esther Ann). Dennis (1837–1919) was a son of George Washington Guernsey and a brother of Justus George (1850 census, Fairfield Twp, DeKalb Co., IN). **PROBABLE (s8):** Almeda's grandmother Hannah Walker was a daughter of Catherine Finkle, G. W. Guernsey's second wife, so the Linsons were Guernsey step-kin, which explains both marriages (see hannah-walker.md).
