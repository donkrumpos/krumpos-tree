# Hannah Walker

**Lifespan:** c. 1816 – January 9, 1907

**FamilySearch ID:** L7JK-3SC (16 sources; no parents in the FS tree)

## Summary
Born about 1816 in New York State, the daughter of **John Walker** and **Catherine Finkle**, both also New York-born (her 1907 death certificate). She married Asa W. Linson about 1842. The family was in New York until about 1845, then in **Fairfield Township, DeKalb County, Indiana** (1850), and in Michigan from about 1856. She was widowed in 1889 and died on 9 Jan 1907 in Ronald Township, Ionia Co., aged 91, of paralysis. She was buried in Tuttle Cemetery the next day.

## Relationships
- **Father:** John Walker (b. New York; nothing more known)
- **Mother:** Catherine Finkle (b. New York). **PROBABLE:** she is the Catherine Finkle (c.1797–1892) who later married Welden Flagg, then a Mr. Alexander, and in 1847 George Washington Guernsey, which would make Hannah the stepdaughter of G. W. Guernsey (see Research Notes).
- **Husband:** Asa W. Linson (1814–1889, L7JK-338)

## Research Notes
- **The Catherine Finkle link (PROBABLE, 2026-10-08 s8).** George Washington Guernsey's second wife was Catherine (Finkle) Flagg Alexander. Her 1892 death register entry names her parents as John and Dorcas Finkle, and she died "aged 95", so she was born about 1797. That makes her old enough to be Hannah's mother. Several things tie the two families together:
  - In 1850 the Linsons lived **next door to** Ezra Guernsey and in the same township as G. W. Guernsey (Fairfield, DeKalb Co., IN).
  - Both families came from the Granby/Lysander area of Oswego and Onondaga counties, NY.
  - Two of G. W. Guernsey's family married two of Hannah's daughters. His son Dennis married Esther Ann Linson; his grandson John Loami married Almeda.
  - Hannah named a daughter **Dorcas**, the name of Catherine Finkle's mother.
  - Hannah and Catherine are both buried in **Tuttle Cemetery**.
  - Catherine died in Orange Twp, where Hannah's daughter Dorcas Wiggins also lived.
  
  Not yet proven: no record names Hannah as Catherine's child, and no marriage of Catherine to a John Walker has been found.
- **Finkle** (Finckel, Vinckel) is a Palatine German name of the Hudson and Mohawk valleys, and Schoharie County had Finkles in 1840. Walker is common in England, Scotland and Ulster alike. **Scottish: nothing documented.**
