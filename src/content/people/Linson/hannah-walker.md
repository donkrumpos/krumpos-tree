# Hannah Walker

**Lifespan:** c. 1816 – January 9, 1907

**FamilySearch ID:** L7JK-3SC (16 sources; no parents)

## Summary
Born about 1816–18 in New York. She married Asa W. Linson in 1842 and raised at least eleven children in Indiana and Michigan. She died in Ionia Township on 9 Jan 1907, aged 90.

## Relationships
- **Husband:** Asa W. Linson (1814–1889, L7JK-338)
- **Daughter:** Almeda Linson (c.1859–1881), **direct line**

## Sources
- *Ionia Standard*, 11 Jan 1907 (quoted in an FS collaborate note): "Mrs. Hannah Linson, aged 90, of Ionia township, died Wednesday. Funeral Thursday p.m. at the home, and interment in Tuttle cemetery."
- 1870 census, Bushnell Twp (1818, NY); 1880 census, Ionia (1818, NY).
