# Joseph Lebeau

**Lifespan:** bapt. 5 Jul 1753 — ?

## Summary
**Baptized 5 Jul 1753 at Saint-Joseph de Chambly, Quebec, son of Charles Lebeau and Amable Viau** [CONFIRMED 2026-10-10, indexed Quebec parish register]. He was a *laboureur* at Saint-Mathias (Rouville). He married **Marie-Louise Haché** (dit Gallant, of an Acadian family) by 1787; their children were baptized at Chambly (1787) and Saint-Mathias (1794, 1797, 1799). Father of Marthe Lebeau, and so great-great-grandfather of Isaac Surprenant's children. He is the link from the Surprenants to the **Bourhis (Breton) line**: his mother Amable Viau was the granddaughter of Jean Bourhis.
