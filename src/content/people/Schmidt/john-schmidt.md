# John Schmidt

**Lifespan:** March 24, 1822 – February 1, 1895

## Summary
Father of Charles Frederick Schmidt (1863/64–1920). Born 24 Mar 1822 in Germany. Married Elizabeth Rabe (1824–1912). He was in Joliet, Will Co., Illinois, by 1856, and farmed in Cleveland, Le Sueur County, Minnesota, by 1866 (1880 census). A mason by trade at his death. Died 1 Feb 1895 at St. Peter, Nicollet County, aged 72 years 10 months 7 days, and is buried in Kasota Township, Le Sueur County.
