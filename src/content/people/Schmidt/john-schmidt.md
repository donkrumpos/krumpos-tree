# John Schmidt

**Lifespan:** March 24, 1822 – February 1, 1895

## Summary
Father of Charles Frederick Schmidt (1863/64–1920). Born 24 Mar 1822 in Germany. Married Elizabeth Rabe (1824–1912). He was in Joliet, Will Co., Illinois, by 1856, and farmed in Cleveland, Le Sueur County, Minnesota, by 1866 (1880 census). Died 1 Feb 1895 at St. Peter, Nicollet County, and is buried in Kasota Township, Le Sueur County.

## Relationships
- **Wife:** Elizabeth Rabe (31 Aug 1824 – 25 May 1912), daughter of William Rabe
- **Children:**
  - Elizabeth Schmidt (9 Apr 1856, Joliet – 4 Jun 1918; m. Fred Hart)
  - Mary Schmidt (Dec 15, 1859–Mar 28, 1909, born Illinois)
  - Charles Frederick Schmidt (Dec 13, 1863/64–May 1920, born Illinois) — **direct line**
  - Amelia Ann Schmidt (c. 1866–?, born Minnesota; age 14 in 1880 census)
  - Emilie Schmidt (1882–1966) — *doubtful (see Elizabeth Rabe's notes)*
