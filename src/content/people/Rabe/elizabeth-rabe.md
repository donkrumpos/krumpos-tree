# Elizabeth Rabe

**Lifespan:** August 31, 1824 – May 25, 1912

## Summary
Born 31 Aug 1824 in **Hanover**, Germany (her 1912 obituary; "Prussia" in the 1880 census, as Hanover was Prussian after 1866), daughter of **William Rabe**. She and her husband crossed to America in 1852. Married John C. Schmidt (b. 1822). Lived in Illinois (Joliet, 1856–64), then in Cleveland, Le Sueur County, Minnesota (1880). Widowed in 1895. Died 25 May 1912 at St. Peter, Nicollet County, Minnesota, aged 87, and is buried in Kasota Township, Le Sueur County, beside her husband.

## Research Notes
- **Hanover CONFIRMED by obituary [2026-10-13 s4]:** the 1912 *Free Press* obituary names Hanover. That fits the Will Co. Rabe lead below (Anna Rabe at Monee and Eli Rabe at Frankfort, both b. Hanover), though nothing yet links her to them; Rabe is a common Hanoverian name. The 1850 Will Co. census index (USGenWeb) has no Rabe. Her parish in Hanover is still unknown; the next step would be a Milwaukee or Joliet church record of 1852–62 (a child's baptism might give the parents' home parishes).
- **Children:** the 1912 obituary says seven children, four surviving: Henry T., Charles, Elizabeth (Mrs. Fred Hart) and **Mrs. Adolph Hermel** (Waterville; presumably Amelia Ann). Mary died 1909. **Emilie Schmidt (1882–1966) is not among the survivors although she was alive in 1912, so she was not Elizabeth's daughter**; remove her when convenient.
- **Burial:** the obituary says Woodlawn cemetery; Find a Grave places her in Kasota Township, Le Sueur Co., beside John. They may be the same cemetery under two names; not checked.
- **Corrected 2026-10-13: the earlier "b. 12 Dec 1846, d. 1918 Greenlawn, Butler Co., Ohio, buried Columbus" belonged to someone else.** It came from the FamilySearch tree, and it could not be right: a woman born in 1846 cannot be the mother of a daughter born in 1856. The 1912 death record (b. 1824, aged 87) agrees with the 1880 census age of **56**, so the original census reading was right after all. The 1918 death year was probably borrowed from daughter Elizabeth (Schmidt) Hart, who died 4 Jun 1918.
- Her mother's name and her German home town are still open. Next: her obituary (St. Peter Herald or Free Press, late May 1912); St. Peter Lutheran or Catholic burial register; Rabe households in Will Co., IL, 1850–60.
- **Lead [2026-10-13 s3]: Rabe families from Hanover in Will Co., Illinois, 1860.** The 1860 census (FS index) has an Anna Rabe (b. 1816, Hanover) household at **Monee** (with Frederick, William and Eliza, the last b. 1850 Hanover) and Eli Rabe (b. 1836, Hanover) at **Frankfort**. Both are near Joliet, where Elizabeth and John lived 1856–64. The Kingdom of Hanover was annexed by Prussia in 1866, which would fit her "Prussia" in 1880. **No record links her to them yet.** Next: these households' images and the 1850 census of Will/Cook Co. for a William Rabe b. c. 1790–1800.
- **Source hierarchy:** census images and register images first, then indexes, then FamilySearch tree data. The tree has several confirmed errors on this family.
