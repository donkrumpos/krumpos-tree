# Elizabeth Rabe

**Lifespan:** August 31, 1824 – May 25, 1912

## Summary
Born 31 Aug 1824 in **Hanover**, Germany (her 1912 obituary; "Prussia" in the 1880 census, as Hanover was Prussian after 1866), daughter of **William Rabe**. She and her husband crossed to America in 1852. Married John C. Schmidt (b. 1822). Lived in Illinois (Joliet, 1856–64), then in Cleveland, Le Sueur County, Minnesota (1880). Widowed in 1895. Died 25 May 1912 at St. Peter, Nicollet County, Minnesota, aged 87, and is buried in Kasota Township, Le Sueur County, beside her husband.

## Research Notes
- **2026-10-14 s6 — Henry T. b. Mecklenburg; Charles's obituary.** The 1920 census (FS MWBV-GZ6, St. Peter) gives **Henry Schmidt b. Mecklenburg**, immigrated 1852, with Amelia, Evelyn and Carl. So the eldest son was born in **John's** home state, and the couple probably married in Mecklenburg. The Hanover side remains her birthplace only. **The Henry Schmidt d. 13 Jul 1938, Nicollet (QPHR-XWCN) is NOT Henry T.** He was b. 1869, Hennepin Co., son of Gottlieb Schmidt × Johanna Radintz (CG86-T56Z). The Nicollet Henry Schmidts d. 1920 (b. 1869 NY) and 1946 (Henry J., husband of Emma) are also others. Henry T. was alive in Sept 1924 (*Saint Peter Herald* 5 Sep 1924); his death and obituary are not found yet (offline list). Charles's obituary (*Saint Peter Herald* 21 May 1920) is summarized in [[charles-frederick-schmidt]].
- **2026-10-14 s5 — second obituary; eldest son b. Germany 1852.** The *Saint Peter Tribune*, 29 May 1912, p. 4 (MNHS Hub, OCR read) has a second notice: she died at her son H. T. Schmidt's, aged **87 y 8 m 26 d**; "born in Hanover, Germany, on the last day of August in the year 1824, and came to America with her husband in 1852", a sailing voyage of **seven weeks and four days**, to Milwaukee and "during the same year" to Joliet, "where they remained until **1864**" (the *Free Press* says 1862), then Cleveland Twp until 1881. "**Eight** children were born, four of whom survive" (the *Free Press* says seven). No village or parents. **Henry T. Schmidt**, the eldest son, was **born in Germany in 1852** and immigrated 1852 (1910 census, St. Peter Ward 2, FS M2GY-7YQ; 1905 MN census SP3J-VP6). So the couple probably **married in Germany** in 1851–52, and Henry's baptism would be in Elizabeth's or John's home parish. Henry m. Emelia Moring (FS Q2M5-34VC). A Henry Schmidt d. 13 Jul 1938, Nicollet Co. (QPHR-XWCN) may be him; not checked.
