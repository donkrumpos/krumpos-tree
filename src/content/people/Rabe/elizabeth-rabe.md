# Elizabeth Rabe

**Lifespan:** August 31, 1824 – May 25, 1912

## Summary
Born 31 Aug 1824 in Germany (Prussia per the 1880 census), daughter of **William Rabe**. Married John C. Schmidt (b. 1822). Lived in Illinois (Joliet, 1856–64), then in Cleveland, Le Sueur County, Minnesota (1880). Widowed in 1895. Died 25 May 1912 at St. Peter, Nicollet County, Minnesota, aged 87, and is buried in Kasota Township, Le Sueur County, beside her husband.

## Research Notes
- **Corrected 2026-10-13: the earlier "b. 12 Dec 1846, d. 1918 Greenlawn, Butler Co., Ohio, buried Columbus" belonged to someone else.** It came from the FamilySearch tree, and it could not be right: a woman born in 1846 cannot be the mother of a daughter born in 1856. The 1912 death record (b. 1824, aged 87) agrees with the 1880 census age of **56**, so the original census reading was right after all. The 1918 death year was probably borrowed from daughter Elizabeth (Schmidt) Hart, who died 4 Jun 1918.
- Her mother's name and her German home town are still open. Next: her obituary (St. Peter Herald or Free Press, late May 1912); St. Peter Lutheran or Catholic burial register; Rabe households in Will Co., IL, 1850–60.
- **Lead [2026-10-13 s3]: Rabe families from Hanover in Will Co., Illinois, 1860.** The 1860 census (FS index) has an Anna Rabe (b. 1816, Hanover) household at **Monee** (with Frederick, William and Eliza, the last b. 1850 Hanover) and Eli Rabe (b. 1836, Hanover) at **Frankfort**. Both are near Joliet, where Elizabeth and John lived 1856–64. The Kingdom of Hanover was annexed by Prussia in 1866, which would fit her "Prussia" in 1880. **No record links her to them yet.** Next: these households' images and the 1850 census of Will/Cook Co. for a William Rabe b. c. 1790–1800.
