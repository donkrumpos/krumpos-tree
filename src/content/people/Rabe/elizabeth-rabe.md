# Elizabeth Rabe

**Lifespan:** August 31, 1824 – May 25, 1912

## Summary
Born 31 Aug 1824 in **Hanover**, Germany (her 1912 obituary; "Prussia" in the 1880 census, as Hanover was Prussian after 1866), daughter of **William Rabe**. She and her husband crossed to America in 1852. Married John C. Schmidt (b. 1822). Lived in Illinois (Joliet, 1856–64), then in Cleveland, Le Sueur County, Minnesota (1880). Widowed in 1895. Died 25 May 1912 at St. Peter, Nicollet County, Minnesota, aged 87, and is buried in Kasota Township, Le Sueur County, beside her husband.

  - Henry T. Schmidt left St. Peter after 1927. He wintered in California from January 1928 with his daughters Evelyn (Mrs. Bernard Hennessey) and Lottie (Mrs. Arthur Maxson).   - **Ruled out: the Kyritz couple.** FS indexes a Brandenburg marriage that looks like this one at first sight. **Johann Joachim Friedrich Schmidt**, colonist at Sechzehneichen(?), parish Kyritz, son of Johann Christian Schmidt, late sexton and schoolmaster at Zempow, was **b. 24 Mar 1821** at Schweinrich. On 2 Aug 1850 at Kyritz (Ostprignitz) he married the widow **Katharina Elisabeth Butter née Rabe**, daughter of Christoph Rabe, Büdner. The Zempow (FS J464-T5W, image 3:1:S3HY-63TS-FSL) and Kyritz (JQ6H-LQC, image 3:1:S3HT-XK53-XMR) duplicates were both **image read**.
