# Suzanne Bourhis

**Lifespan:** c.1704 — ?

## Summary
Daughter of the Breton immigrant **Jean Bourhis** and **Marie Demers**. She married **Jean-Baptiste Viau** at Saint-Antoine de Longueuil on 9 Feb 1722; the marriage index names her parents as "Jean Bourhice × Marie Demers" [CONFIRMED 2026-10-10, index]. Mother of Marie-Amable Viau (bapt. Longueuil 1731). She married (2) Antoine Valière at Longueuil in 1733 [index]. Her descendants use the forms Bourhis, Bourhice, Boulerice, and the nickname *dit Lebreton* ("the Breton").
