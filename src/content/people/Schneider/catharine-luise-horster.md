# Catharine Luise Hörster

**Lifespan:** February 14, 1828 – ?

## Summary
Born 14 Feb 1828 at **Lautzenbrücken** (parish of Kirburg, Westerwald), daughter of Johann Heinrich Hörster and Elisabethe Margarethe Krumm, farmers there. Her father died when she was six (1834); her mother remarried Justus Hüne in 1837. Married **Heinrich Ludwig Schneider**, a miner, at Lautzenbrücken on 28 Nov 1858. Mother of Mathilde Henriette Schneider (b. 25 Apr 1870), who emigrated to Kenosha in 1892. Called "Louise Horster" in her daughter's 1892 marriage index.

Two other Schneider couples were having children in Lautzenbrücken at the same time: Johannes Daniel Schneider × Christine Wilhelmine Müller, and Johannes Ludwig Schneider × Elisabetha Catharina Gerhäuser. Don't confuse them; the latter had a **Henriette Mathilde** b. 24 Feb 1864 (img 342), not ours. Siblings are kept in prose only: a children-list entry like "Ludwig Paul Schneider" would collide with Heinrich's aka "Ludwig Schneider" and with Mathilde's son Paul.
