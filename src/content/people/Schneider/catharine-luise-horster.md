# Catharine Luise Hörster

**Lifespan:** February 14, 1828 – ?

## Summary
Born 14 Feb 1828 at **Lautzenbrücken** (parish of Kirburg, Westerwald), daughter of Johann Heinrich Hörster and Elisabethe Margarethe Krämer, farmers there. Married **Heinrich Ludwig Schneider**, a miner, at Lautzenbrücken on 28 Nov 1858. Mother of Mathilde Henriette Schneider (b. 25 Apr 1870), who emigrated to Kenosha in 1892. Called "Louise Horster" in her daughter's 1892 marriage index.
