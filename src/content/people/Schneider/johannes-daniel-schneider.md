# Johannes Daniel Schneider

## Summary
Farmer at **Lautzenbrücken**, parish of Kirburg, Westerwald; living in 1826. Father of Johann Friedrich Schneider (b. 1797), so a 5×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Mathilde (Schneider) Schneider.
