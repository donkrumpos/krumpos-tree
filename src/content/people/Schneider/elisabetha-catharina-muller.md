# Elisabetha Catharina Müller

## Summary
Wife of Johann Peter Glöckner, farmer at **Norken**, parish of Kirburg, Westerwald; living in 1826. Mother of Marie Catharine Christine Glöckner (b. 1803).

## Sources
- Marriage of Johannes Friederich Schneider × Maria Catharina Christina Glöckner, Lautzenbrücken, 3 Sep 1826, no. 1 — **IMAGE READ 2026-10-14 s10** (FS catalog 760015, "Kirchenbuchduplikat, 1817-1874", Ev. Kirche Kirburg; film 2003138 items 2–3, DGS 102101265, image 167, ark 3:1:3Q9M-CSWK-8S7T-P). Groom b. Lautzenbrücken 27 Aug 1797, evangelical, single, farmer (Ackersmann) there; son of Johannes Daniel Schneider and his late (verstorbene) wife Anna Maria née Benner, farmers at Lautzenbrücken. Bride b. **Norken** 20 Jun 1803, evangelical, single, living at Norken; daughter of Joh. Peter Glöckner and his wife Elisabetha Catharina née Müller, farmers at Norken.
