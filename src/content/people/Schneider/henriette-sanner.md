# Henriette Sanner

## Summary
Wife of Friedrich Schneider and mother of William ("Von Wilhem") Schneider (b. Mar 1872) and Henry (Heinrich) Schneider (b. 5 Jul 1874), both of Kenosha, Wisconsin. The family came from **Daaden**, Westerwald. A 3×-great-grandmother of Don Krumpos through Matilda (Schneider) Krumpos. Matilda's middle name, Henrietta, was probably given for her.

**PROBABLE:** three independent indexes name her (1892, 1898, NUMIDENT), but no image has been read.

## Sources
- **Henry's marriage, Kenosha, 24 Nov 1898** (FS "Wisconsin, Marriages, 1836-1930", XRV6-N7S; index only, found 2026-10-13 s4): "Henri Schneider" × Rosine Naegeli (parents Andreas Naegeli × Kath. Fuhrer); groom's parents **Fried Schneider** and **Henriette "Tanner"**. A third independent index naming the couple, with a third reading of her surname (Sanner / Ganner / Tanner).
- William's marriage, Kenosha, 19 Oct 1892 (FS XR5J-7PC; index only): "Henriette Sanner or Ganner".
- Henry Schneider's Social Security NUMIDENT (FS 6KQN-1163; index): "Henrietta Sanner". This settles Sanner over Ganner.
- See [[william-schneider]] and [[friedrich-schneider]].

## Research Notes
- **Surname [2026-10-13 s4]:** three indexes, three readings: Sanner (NUMIDENT), "Sanner or Ganner" (1892) and Tanner (1898). Two of the three, and the 1892 indexer's first choice, read Sanner, so Sanner stays. **Sanner is a Kreis Altenkirchen name**: FS indexes Sanner burials at Hamm (Sieg) in the 1790s and at Niederfischbach in 1849, both within about 15–25 km of Daaden. No Henriette Sanner baptism c. 1840–55 is indexed there.
- Look for her baptism and marriage in the Daaden (or neighbouring) Evangelical church books on Archion.
