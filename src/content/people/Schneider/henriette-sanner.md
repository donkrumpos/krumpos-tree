# Henriette Sanner

## Summary
Wife of Friedrich Schneider and mother of William ("Von Wilhem") Schneider (b. Mar 1872) and Henry (Heinrich) Schneider (b. 5 Jul 1874), both of Kenosha, Wisconsin. The family came from **Daaden**, Westerwald. A 3×-great-grandmother of Don Krumpos through Matilda (Schneider) Krumpos. Matilda's middle name, Henrietta, was probably given for her.

**PROBABLE:** two independent indexes name her, but no image has been read.

## Sources
- William's marriage, Kenosha, 19 Oct 1892 (FS XR5J-7PC; index only): "Henriette Sanner or Ganner".
- Henry Schneider's Social Security NUMIDENT (FS 6KQN-1163; index): "Henrietta Sanner". This settles Sanner over Ganner.
- See [[william-schneider]] and [[friedrich-schneider]].
