# Johann Friedrich Schneider

## Summary
Farmer (Bauersmann) at **Lautzenbrücken**, parish of Kirburg, Westerwald, and husband of Marie Catharine Christine Glöckner. Father of Heinrich Ludwig Schneider (b. 1830), so a 4×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Mathilde (Schneider) Schneider. Not the same man as [[friedrich-schneider]] of Daaden, William Schneider's father.

## Sources
- Marriage of Heinrich Ludwig Schneider × Catharine Luise Hörster, Lautzenbrücken, 28 Nov 1858, no. 5 — **IMAGE READ 2026-10-14 s9** (FS catalog 760015, "Kirchenbuchduplikat, 1817-1874", Ev. Kirche Kirburg; film 2003138 items 2–3, DGS 102101265, image 361, ark 3:1:3Q9M-CSWK-8SW6-N). Names him and his wife as the groom's parents, "Ehe- und Bauersleute zu Lautzenbrücken"; both apparently living in 1858.
