# Johann Friedrich Schneider

**Lifespan:** August 27, 1797 – ?

## Summary
Born 27 Aug 1797 at **Lautzenbrücken**, parish of Kirburg, Westerwald, son of Johannes Daniel Schneider and Anna Maria Benner (d. before 1826). A farmer (Ackersmann, Bauersmann) there, he married Marie Catharine Christine Glöckner of Norken on 3 Sep 1826. Their first child, Friederich August, was born 20 Mar 1827 and died 1833. Father of Heinrich Ludwig Schneider (b. 1830), so a 4×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Mathilde (Schneider) Schneider. Not the same man as [[friedrich-schneider]] of Daaden, William Schneider's father.
