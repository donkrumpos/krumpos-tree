# Friedrich Schneider

## Summary
Father of William ("Von Wilhem") Schneider (b. Mar 1872) and of Henry (Heinrich) Schneider (b. 5 Jul 1874), who both emigrated to Kenosha, Wisconsin, in 1891–92. The family's home was **Daaden** (Kreis Altenkirchen, Westerwald), the last residence Henry gave at Baltimore in April 1892. Husband of Henriette Sanner. A 3×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Matilda (Schneider) Krumpos.

**PROBABLE:** three independent indexes name him (1892, 1898, NUMIDENT), but no image has been read.
