# Elisabethe Margarethe Krumm

**Lifespan:** March 20, 1806 – ?

## Summary
Born 20 Mar 1806 at **Lautzenbrücken** (parish of Kirburg, Westerwald), daughter of Johann Christian Krumm and Maria Margaretha Weller, farmers there. Married (1) **Johann Heinrich Hörster** at Lautzenbrücken on 9 Dec 1827; mother of Catharine Luise Hörster (b. 1828) and Friedrich Carl Hörster (b. 1830). Widowed 8 Mar 1834, she married (2) **Justus Hüne** on 25 Jun 1837. A 4×-great-grandmother of Don Krumpos through Mathilde (Schneider) Schneider.
