# Johann Christian Krumm

## Summary
Farmer at **Lautzenbrücken**, parish of Kirburg, Westerwald; living in 1827. Father of Elisabethe Margarethe Krumm (b. 1806), so a 5×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Mathilde (Schneider) Schneider.

## Research Notes
- **2026-10-16 s12:** a second daughter found: Johannette Catharine, b. 30 Mar 1803, m. 6 Feb 1820 Johann Peter Althäuser, d. 15 Jan 1871 (img 433). So he was married to Maria Margaretha Weller by 1802. **Lead (not linked):** Anne Catharine Denker née Krumm (b. 1786, d. 1856, img 376) was a daughter of **Johann Wilhelm Krumm × Anna Maria Weller** of Lautzenbrücken, and her son Johann Anton Krumm (b. 1806) was mayor of Lautzenbrücken in 1856. Johann Christian may be a son of that couple (i.e. Anne Catharine's brother) or a nephew; the Kirburg church books (restricted / Archion) would show his baptism.
- 2026-10-15 s11: alive and churchwarden (Kirchenvorsteher) of Lautzenbrücken in June 1836; named with his wife as "Bauers- und Eheleute zu Lautzenbrücken" in June 1837.
- Born before the Nassau civil transcripts begin (1817); earlier records are in the Kirburg church books (FS catalog 146509, restricted) or on Archion. See [[mathilda-unknown-schneider]].
