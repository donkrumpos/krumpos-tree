# Anna Maria Schneider

## Summary
Wife of Johann Gerlach Hörster, farmer at **Lautzenbrücken**; living in 1827. Mother of Johann Heinrich Hörster (b. 1803). Her relationship, if any, to the Schneider family of Lautzenbrücken (Johannes Daniel Schneider) is unknown.
