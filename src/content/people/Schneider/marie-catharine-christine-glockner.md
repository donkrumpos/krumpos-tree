# Marie Catharine Christine Glöckner

**Lifespan:** June 20, 1803 – ?

## Summary
Born 20 Jun 1803 at **Norken** (parish of Kirburg, Westerwald), daughter of Johann Peter Glöckner and Elisabetha Catharina Müller, farmers there. Married Johann Friedrich Schneider of Lautzenbrücken on 3 Sep 1826; mother of Friederich August (1827–1833) and Heinrich Ludwig Schneider (b. 1830). A 4×-great-grandmother of Don Krumpos through Mathilde (Schneider) Schneider.

## Research Notes
- Her given names vary: Maria Catharina Christina (1826 marriage), Marie Catharine Christine (1827 birth, 1858 marriage), Anna Catharina Christina (1830 birth). Surname Glöckner is clear in all four entries. Norken has its own section in the Kirburg Kirchenbuchduplikat (catalog 760015), but her 1803 birth predates it.
