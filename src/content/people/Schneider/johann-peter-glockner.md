# Johann Peter Glöckner

## Summary
Farmer at **Norken**, parish of Kirburg, Westerwald; living in 1826. Father of Marie Catharine Christine Glöckner (b. 1803), so a 5×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Mathilde (Schneider) Schneider.

## Sources
- Marriage of Johannes Friederich Schneider × Maria Catharina Christina Glöckner, Lautzenbrücken, 3 Sep 1826, no. 1 — **CONFIRMED** (image read 2026-10-14 s10) (FS catalog 760015, "Kirchenbuchduplikat, 1817-1874", Ev. Kirche Kirburg; film 2003138 items 2–3, DGS 102101265, image 167, ark 3:1:3Q9M-CSWK-8S7T-P). Groom b. Lautzenbrücken 27 Aug 1797, evangelical, single, farmer (Ackersmann) there; son of Johannes Daniel Schneider and his late (verstorbene) wife Anna Maria née Benner, farmers at Lautzenbrücken. Bride b. **Norken** 20 Jun 1803, evangelical, single, living at Norken; daughter of Joh. Peter Glöckner and his wife Elisabetha Catharina née Müller, farmers at Norken.
- Death of Elisabetha Catharina Pfeiffer née Glöckner, Lautzenbrücken, 23 Nov 1859, no. 8 — **CONFIRMED** (image read 2026-10-16 s12) (FS catalog 760015, "Kirchenbuchduplikat, 1817-1874", Ev. Kirche Kirburg; film 2003138 items 2–3, DGS 102101265, image 382, ark 3:1:3Q9M-CSWK-8S7W-X). b. 3 Nov 1788 (Norken); parents **Johann Peter Glöckner and Elisabetha Catharina née Müller**, farmers at Norken; aged 71 yrs 20 days. Married 10 Jan 1812 Johann Friedrich Pfeiffer of Lautzenbrücken, who died 30 Mar 1842 (his death: img 294, ark 3:1:3Q9M-CSWK-8S7F-B); one son and one daughter. An elder sister of Marie Catharine Christine Glöckner.
