# Maria Margaretha Weller

## Summary
Wife of Johann Christian Krumm, farmer at **Lautzenbrücken**; living in 1827. Her place of origin is written after "von" in the 1827 marriage but was not read. Mother of Elisabethe Margarethe Krumm (b. 1806).

## Research Notes
- **2026-10-16 s12:** a second daughter found: Johannette Catharine Krumm, b. 30 Mar 1803, m. 1820 Johann Peter Althäuser, d. 1871 (img 433). An earlier Krumm × Weller marriage (Johann Wilhelm Krumm × Anna Maria Weller; daughter Anne Catharine b. 1786, img 376) suggests the Krumm and Weller families intermarried more than once; whether Anna Maria was a relative of Maria Margaretha is unknown. Her own birthplace word in the 1827 marriage (img 168) is still unread.
- 2026-10-15 s11: the 1837 marriage (img 222) names her as Maria Margaretha née Weller, farmer's wife at Lautzenbrücken, apparently living. The word after "Weller," in the 1827 entry (img 168) was re-read at full resolution: it looks like "[aus?] …enrod" (perhaps Stangenrod, a village near Kirburg), but the reading is not secure.
- Born before the Nassau civil transcripts begin (1817); earlier records are in the Kirburg church books (FS catalog 146509, restricted) or on Archion. See [[mathilda-unknown-schneider]].
