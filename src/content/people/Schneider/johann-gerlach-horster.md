# Johann Gerlach Hörster

## Summary
Farmer at **Lautzenbrücken**, parish of Kirburg, Westerwald; dead by Dec 1827 ("weiland"). Father of Johann Heinrich Hörster (b. 1803), so a 5×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Mathilde (Schneider) Schneider.

## Research Notes
- **2026-10-16 s12:** a daughter found: Anna Maria Elisabeth, b. 27 Jan 1799, m. 1821 Johannes Daniel Schmidt of Norken, d. Lautzenbrücken 27 Nov 1864 (img 393). Her death entry calls her father **a miner (Bergmann)** at Lautzenbrücken. Since she was born in 1799, he was married to Anna Maria Schneider by 1798.
- 2026-10-15 s11: his son Johann Heinrich's 1834 death entry (img 235) names him and Anna Marie née Schneider as farmers at Lautzenbrücken; a second son, **Johann Gerlach** (b. 1 Apr 1806, m. 1830 Elisabethe Marie Schell), died 26 Mar 1834. His own death (before Dec 1827) was not seen in a name-column sweep of the 1817–29 deaths (imgs 169–185), so it may predate 1817.
- Born before the Nassau civil transcripts begin (1817); earlier records are in the Kirburg church books (FS catalog 146509, restricted) or on Archion. See [[mathilda-unknown-schneider]].
