# Johann Gerlach Hörster

## Summary
Farmer at **Lautzenbrücken**, parish of Kirburg, Westerwald; dead by Dec 1827 ("weiland"). Father of Johann Heinrich Hörster (b. 1803), so a 5×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Mathilde (Schneider) Schneider.

## Sources
- Marriage of Johannes Heinrich Hörster × Elisabetha Margaretha Krumm, Lautzenbrücken, 9 Dec 1827, no. 1 — **CONFIRMED** (image read 2026-10-14 s10) (FS catalog 760015, "Kirchenbuchduplikat, 1817-1874", Ev. Kirche Kirburg; film 2003138 items 2–3, DGS 102101265, image 168, ark 3:1:3Q9M-CSWK-8SWP-H). Groom b. Lautzenbrücken 11 May 1803, evangelical, single, farmer there; son of Johann Gerlach Hörster, late (weiland) farmer at Lautzenbrücken, and his wife Anna Maria née Schneider. Bride b. Lautzenbrücken 20 Mar 1806, evangelical, single; daughter of Johann Christian Krumm and his wife Maria Margaretha née Weller (her place of origin after "von" not read), farmers at Lautzenbrücken. Margin: "Joh. Heinr. Hörster ist gestorben 1834 8 März. Die Wittwe verheirathete sich zum 2. mal 1837 den 25 Juny mit Justus Hüne."
