# Mathilde (Schneider) Schneider

**Lifespan:** April 1870 – July 1912

## Summary
Mother of Matilda Henrietta (Schneider) Krumpos (1897–1973). Born April 1870 in Germany. Died c. 1912 in Kenosha, Wisconsin (age ~42). Buried at Green Ridge Cemetery, Kenosha, Kenosha County, Wisconsin. Her maiden name was apparently also **Schneider** (spelled Schnieder for her father). Two independent sources give it, though no image has been seen yet: her 1892 marriage index and her daughter Anna's Social Security application. She arrived at Baltimore in April 1892, single, aged 22, bound for Wisconsin, and married William Schneider at Kenosha six months later, on 19 Oct 1892.

## Research Notes
- **2026-10-14 s6 — fellow passengers read; "Lengerke" reading.** The *Karlsruhe* list (28 Apr 1892) runs over FS 2CSH-Z9X images 772–774 (pp. 261–263; DGS 005576969). Mathilde is on p. 262. Her neighbours are no help: Emilie Merkly (Stuttgart → Ohio), Anna Hoyer (Heinbrechts = Helmbrechts → Md.) and the Henk family ("Plate" → Nebr.). Only four other parties were bound for Wisconsin: Carl Graf, 24 ("Lukko", p. 262 line 3); the Süss family of 8 (Mellendorf, p. 261); August Malk's family of 7 ("Marienhytte", p. 263); and Alois Treuma? ("Rimpach", p. 263). None is from a place resembling hers. The FS index of the list gives no residence. **Clue to the clerk's spelling:** he wrote "Marienhytte" for Marienhütte (y = ü), "Heinbrechts" for Helmbrechts and "Gluckstad" for Glückstadt. So "Lanzerykke" is a phonetic rendering, and **Lengerich, Westphalia, is "Lengerke" in the local Low German.** That fits the sound well and makes Lengerich (Kreis Tecklenburg) the best guess, but it is still unproven. FS indexes have no Schneider × Hörster baptism or marriage at Lengerich or anywhere in Kreis Tecklenburg. The Lengerich (evangelisch) church books are on Archion (offline).
- Born in Germany (Deutschland) — not Bohemian/Czech like the other Krumpos-line families
- Died young (~42) in Kenosha County — buried July 5, 1912 at Green Ridge Cemetery (SW, Block 63, Lot 1)
- Family may have moved south from Kewaunee to Kenosha
- Maiden name: Schneider/Schnieder per 1892 marriage index and Anna's NUMIDENT (2026-10-13). To confirm, read the Kenosha County marriage register image (1892) or her 1912 death certificate (WI Vital Records / Kenosha Co. register of deeds).
- Her German home town is unknown. The Baltimore list's "Langerykk" is unidentified. Her father's "Luda" is probably Ludwig or a mis-reading.
- **"Langerykk" re-read [2026-10-13 s3]** (same image, 2CSH-Z9X, image 773 of 822): the clerk wrote something like "Lanzerykk" / "Langerykk", a phonetic spelling, not a real place name. Two guesses, both unproven: (1) **Lengerich** (Westphalia, Kreis Tecklenburg), whose final *-ich* an English clerk could hear as "-ykk"; (2) **Langerfeld** (by Barmen/Schwelm, now Wuppertal), next to Barmen, where the surname **Hörster** is common in the FS indexes. Horster/Hörster is a Westphalian and Bergisch name, so both fit her mother's surname. No Mathilde Schneider born c. 1868–72 to Ludwig × Louise Horster/Hörster is indexed under either place.
- Note: her ship (*Karlsruhe*) and William's brother Henry's (*Oldenburg*, 14 Apr 1892, from Daaden) both landed at Baltimore in April 1892 and are on the same film. They were not on the same ship.
- Referred to as "the late Mathilda" in Mabel's 1992 obituary — no maiden name given
- Name appears as "Matilda Snider" in 1905 Wisconsin State Census
- Five of her six 1900-census children confirmed alive into adulthood: Anna (Mider, Cincinnati), Fred (Kenosha), Matilda (Krumpos, De Pere), Paul (Kenosha), Mabel (Reinke, Green Bay) — established by Matilda's 1973 obituary listing surviving siblings. Clara (b. 1893) and the unnamed daughter (b. May 1900) remain unaccounted for after 1900.
- **2026-10-13 s4 negatives:** FS searches found no Mathilde Schneider b. 1868–72 with a Horster/Hörster mother, and no Ludwig/Louise Schneider couple with a Hörster wife, in any indexed German collection. The nearest Hörster-Schneider pairs are on the Lower Rhine/Bergisch side (Johann Schneider × Wilhelmine Hörster, Hilden, child b. 1878; Wilhelm Schneider × Johannette Hörster, child b. 1853), which is at least consistent with the Langerfeld guess, but they are not her parents. Her 1912 death is not indexed on FS (only Find a Grave QK1R-HD7L). The Kenosha register of deeds holds the 1912 death certificate (offline list).
- **2026-10-14 s5 — second index, same spelling:** the WPA-era Baltimore index card ("Maryland, Baltimore Passenger Lists Index", FS QV9F-RPZV; card image 3:1:3QS7-898X-N9HP-P, read) gives **"LANZERYKKE"**, Germ., single, 22, F, destination Wis., ship **Karlsruhe**, **28 Apr 1892**, list ref. 118-23-26, line **66**.
