# Von Wilhem Schneider

**Lifespan:** March 1872 – c. 1925

## Summary
Father of Matilda Henrietta (Schneider) Krumpos (1897–1973), Mabel Ruth (Schneider) Reinke (1908–1992), and Paul Schneider. Born March 1872 in **Daaden** (Westerwald, now Rhineland-Palatinate); arrived at New York aged 19 on the SS *Noordland* (Red Star Line, Antwerp), a baker (see mathilda-schneider.md). Immigrated 1891 (1900 census). Married **Mathilde Schneider** at Kenosha on 19 Oct 1892. His parents were Fried[rich] Schneider and Henriette **Sanner**, per the marriage index and, independently, his brother Henry's Social Security record. Henry, a miner, landed at Baltimore in April 1892 with last residence **Daaden**, which corroborates the family's Daaden origin. Referred to as "the late" in Mabel's 1992 obituary. Both parents born in Germany (per 1905 census).

## Research Notes
- Full name "Von Wilhem" per Mabel's obituary — Americanized to "William" in census/other records
- Born in Germany — not Bohemian/Czech like the other Krumpos-line families
- Both parents born in Germany (1905 census)
- Name appears as "Snider" in 1905 Wisconsin State Census
- Occupation: hod carrier (1905 census) — a laborer who carried bricks, mortar, and materials up ladders to masons on construction sites. One of the hardest physical jobs in construction, common work for recent German immigrants.
- **Daaden corroborated [2026-10-13 s3]:** the Daaden birthplace had no cited source in these notes (it came with the *Noordland* detail from earlier research). Brother Henry's own 1892 passenger entry, read from the image, gives last residence "Daaden". Daaden (Kreis Altenkirchen, Rheinprovinz) was an iron-ore mining parish in the Siegerland–Westerwald border country, which fits Henry's "miner". William's March 1872 and Henry's July 1874 births fall before Prussian civil registration (Oct 1874), so the source is the **Evangelical church book of Daaden**. It is not indexed on FamilySearch (searches for Schneider births at Daaden, and for Friedrich × Henriette Sanner, return nothing). The Daaden registers are on **Archion** (paid); see the offline list.
- "Ganner" in the 1892 index is a misreading: Henry's NUMIDENT has Sanner.
- Death c. 1925 per WikiTree — Mabel would have been ~17. Outlived wife Mathilda (d. 1912) by about 13 years
