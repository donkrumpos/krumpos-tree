# Johann Heinrich Hörster

## Summary
Farmer at **Lautzenbrücken**, parish of Kirburg, Westerwald, husband of Elisabethe Margarethe Krämer and father of Catharine Luise Hörster (b. 1828), so a 4×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Mathilde (Schneider) Schneider.
