# Johann Heinrich Hörster

**Lifespan:** May 11, 1803 – March 8, 1834

## Summary
Born 11 May 1803 at **Lautzenbrücken**, parish of Kirburg, Westerwald, son of Johann Gerlach Hörster (d. before 1827) and Anna Maria Schneider. A farmer there, he married Elisabethe Margarethe Krumm on 9 Dec 1827 and died 8 Mar 1834, aged 30. Father of Catharine Luise Hörster (b. 1828) and Friedrich Carl Hörster (b. 1830), so a 4×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Mathilde (Schneider) Schneider.
