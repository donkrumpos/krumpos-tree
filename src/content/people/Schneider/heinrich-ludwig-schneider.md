# Heinrich Ludwig Schneider

**Lifespan:** March 28, 1830 – ?

## Summary
Born 28 Mar 1830 at **Lautzenbrücken** in the Westerwald (parish of Kirburg, Amt Hachenburg, Duchy of Nassau), son of Johann Friedrich Schneider and Marie Catharine Christine Glöckner (of Norken), farmers there; an elder brother, Friederich August (1827–1833), died in childhood. A miner (Bergmann) when he married **Catharine Luise Hörster** at Lautzenbrücken on 28 Nov 1858. Father of Mathilde Henriette (b. 1870), who emigrated in 1892 and married William Schneider at Kenosha; so a 3×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Matilda (Schneider) Krumpos. Called "Luda Schnieder" in his daughter's 1892 Wisconsin marriage index. Children (birth register, all at Lautzenbrücken): Sophie Caroline 1859, Johannetta Emilie 1861, Minna(?) Pauline 1863, Ludwig Albert 1865 (d. same year), Ludwig Paul(?) 1866 and Mathilde Henriette 1870.

## Research Notes
- **2026-10-16 s12:** his father Johann Friedrich Schneider was killed in the iron-ore mine near Lautzenbrücken on 5 Jul 1859 (img 382), the year Heinrich Ludwig's first child was born. Heinrich Ludwig's own death is not in the Lautzenbrücken deaths through 1874; he was alive in 1870 and named ("Luda") in his daughter's 1892 Wisconsin marriage index.
- **2026-10-15 s11 — Mathilde's siblings.** Swept every Lautzenbrücken birth 1856–69 (imgs 320–354 and 410–414; 1856–67 births = imgs 320–354, one register page of about four entries per image; 1868–69 = imgs 410–414). Children of Heinrich Ludwig Schneider × Catharine Luise Hörster: **Sophie Caroline** (28 Jun 1859, img 327), **Johannetta Emilie** (11 Mar 1861, img 333), **Minna(?) Pauline** (21 Apr 1863, img 339), **Ludwig Albert** (27 Mar 1865, d. 21 Jul 1865, img 347), **Ludwig Paul(?)** (1 Nov 1866, img 351), then Mathilde Henriette (25 Apr 1870, img 415). No child 1867–69. Two other Schneider couples were having children in Lautzenbrücken at the same time: Johannes Daniel Schneider × Christine Wilhelmine Müller, and Johannes Ludwig Schneider × Elisabetha Catharina Gerhäuser. Don't confuse them; the latter had a **Henriette Mathilde** b. 24 Feb 1864 (img 342), not ours. Siblings are kept in prose only: a children-list entry like "Ludwig Paul Schneider" would collide with Heinrich's aka "Ludwig Schneider" and with Mathilde's son Paul.
- 2026-10-14 s10: his 1830 birth entry (img 190) and his parents' 1826 marriage (img 167) read. His death (after 1870) would be in the Lautzenbrücken civil registers from 1874 (Standesamt, Prussian) or the Kirburg church book. See [[mathilda-unknown-schneider]].
