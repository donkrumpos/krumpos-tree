# Heinrich Ludwig Schneider

**Lifespan:** March 28, 1830 – ?

## Summary
Born 28 Mar 1830 at **Lautzenbrücken** in the Westerwald (parish of Kirburg, Amt Hachenburg, Duchy of Nassau), son of Johann Friedrich Schneider and Marie Catharine Christine Glöckner (of Norken), farmers there; an elder brother, Friederich August (1827–1833), died in childhood. A miner (Bergmann) when he married **Catharine Luise Hörster** at Lautzenbrücken on 28 Nov 1858. Father of Mathilde Henriette (b. 1870), who emigrated in 1892 and married William Schneider at Kenosha; so a 3×-great-grandfather of Don Krumpos through Matilda (Schneider) Krumpos. Called "Luda Schnieder" in his daughter's 1892 Wisconsin marriage index.
