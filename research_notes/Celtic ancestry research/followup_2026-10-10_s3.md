## 1. Lamont / Normandin — a new competing candidate

The s2 hypothesis was: Bodoh's mother Louise = Louise Normandin, bapt. Beloeil 5 Jul 1817, dau. of Claude Normandin dit Lamonde × Louise Guyon/Dion.

New in s3: a **Louise Normandin married Calixte Desnoyers at Saint-Jean-Baptiste de Rouville on 24 Oct 1837** and raised a family in Quebec until at least 1853. She cannot be Bodoh's Louise, who had children in Clinton Co., NY, from 1836. **But she may be Claude's Louise**: Claude's family was at Saint-Hilaire (Rouville), next to Saint-Jean-Baptiste, in 1830. The index shows only one Louise among Claude's children.

- **Decisive test:** the 1837 marriage image (DGS 005471099 img 175). If it names Claude Normandin × Louise Guyon, the s2 identification fails, and so does the unsourced FS link (GYQY-M2X → GBW9-VY1). The *Normandin dit Lamonde* reading of the surname could still be right through another branch of the family.
- If it names other parents, the s2 hypothesis survives, and the Saint-Cyprien marriages 1833–36 (DGS 005469563, after img 60) are the next read.
- louise-lemonde.md: note added (VERIFY). Tree unchanged.
