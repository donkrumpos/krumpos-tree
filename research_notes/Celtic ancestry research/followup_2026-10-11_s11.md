## 1. Lamont / Normandin dit LaMonday

- The **1860** household was hiding under "Monty". A given-name search (Louisa, b. 1793–1803, Canada, Plattsburgh, collection 1473181) found it in one step. Use this pattern for French-Canadian families with shifting surnames.
- The FS **Full-Text** search reaches Clinton Co. deeds, mortgages, lis pendens and tax lists. Query syntax that worked: `(Lamonday OR Lamunday OR Lamonda OR Lamandy OR Lamunda) AND (Plattsburgh OR Clinton)`. The results list only shows snippets after scrolling (lazy-loaded); open a hit as `/ark:/61903/3:1:<id>?view=fullText&keywords=<word>` and pull the transcript from `document.body.innerText`.
- Other hits not followed up: Deeds 1867–68 ("12 June James Lamonday", an index), Deeds 1878–79 (Joseph Lamonday of Plattsburgh and wife), Deeds 1880–81 (land "occupied by Benjamin Lamonday"), Grantor index 1886–1905 ("Glode Lamonday"). None is likely to name Mrs Bodoh.
- **No Clinton Co. probate turned up in full text.** A surrogate's file for the 1872 estate would list heirs; that is now on the offline batch.

## 2. Bodoh heirs' deed (Wisconsin)

- Found while testing Bodoh × Lamont full-text pairs (the "Lamont" hit was elsewhere in the film). It is the first document to list the whole sibling set of Jacques Jr. and confirms "James M. Bodoh & Julie" = Jacques Jr. × Julia Balthazar. Recorded in `jacques-guilluame-beaudoin.md`. Not Celtic, but useful.

## 3. Woods (Catherine Woods's parents)

- Sherwood said Mary Woods held land next to Asa. GLO shows she took the **patent in her own name**, issued the same day as Asa's. By 1830 she was most likely in **Henry Woods's** household (the only Woods in Liberty Twp), and in 1835 she sold out to Asa while in **Huron Co.**
- Huron Co. Woods households 1830: Andrew (Norwalk), Daniel and "Hrato" (Florence); 1840 index: Ira, J. C., P. C., Sylvester, Gilbert, David, Benjamin Wood(s). Nothing decisive; no 1840 Henry Woods matched.
- Interpretation (PROBABLE): Mr Woods died before mid-1830 (perhaps before 1824), Mary was b. c.1750–60, and Henry (b. 1790–1800) was probably Catherine's brother. The FS "William Woods 1738–1830" remains unsourced; the death year 1830 fits, the name does not yet have any support.
