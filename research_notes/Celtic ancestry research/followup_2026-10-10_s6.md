## 1. Which Marie: the index sweep

Searches in FS coll. 2531984 (Quebec Catholic parish registers index):

- **J.-B. Vigeant × Charlotte Bessette (Besset) as parents:** baptisms of Marie-Charlotte 1744, Marie-Françoise 1745, Marie-Françoise 1747, Marie-Ursule 1750, J.-B. 1753, Alexandre 1755, Marie-Élisabeth 1758 and Marie-Catherine 1760. Burials: Alexandre 1756, Élisabeth and Charlotte 1760, Catherine 1761. **Nothing after 1761.** The 1745 Marie-Françoise's burial is not indexed, so burial coverage is incomplete.
- **Mothers named Ursule/Françoise Vigeant, 1764–1800:** only Ursule × Amable Boileau (children to 1775) and Françoise × Pierre Robert-Lafontaine (child bur. 1794). Both are older women. Marie-Ursule × Boileau married at Chambly on 23 Feb 1756 (record 005468704_00664_00013). Françoise × Robert married 5 Feb 1748 and is J.-B.'s sister (b. 1725, per Tanguay).
- **Every indexed marriage with a Marie… Vigeant/Taupier bride (40 entries; date and film read from each persona):** before 1800 these are only Boileau 1756, **Paquet 1768**, **Cochon 1774**, Michel Choinière × Marie-Louise 1781, Charles Viens × Marie-Amable 1797 and Antoine Barrière × Marie-Victoire 1798. Among brides named Ursule or Françoise Vigeant there is nothing apart from Robert 1748.
- **Cochon 1774** was the only open candidate. The image shows she was Louis Vigeant's daughter, so she is ruled out.

Conclusion: the FS index can't decide this. The 1806 burial age ("about 59") still points to Marie-Françoise (b. 26 Aug 1747), but that is not proof. Next step: the PRDH (paid; it links each person's baptism, marriage and burial), or Jetté's Vigeant entry. Both are on the offline batch.

## 2. Perrier origin by free routes

- **Tanguay vol. 1** (archive.org `dictionnaireg01tang`; the `dictionnairegn01tang` id is an empty stub): the entry is quoted above. It lists Laurent's children by Marie Besset (Jean 27 Apr 1692; Marie-Anne 26 Mar 1694, m. 7 Jan 1713 Jean Vigeant; Marie-Jeanne 10 Apr 1696, m. 4 Dec 1721 Jean Bouis; Angélique 24 May 1698), then his 1714 marriage to Jeanne Dumas, widow of François Dumont. No birthplace.
- **1691 marriage image (DGS 005470509).** The act isn't there (see table). The volume structure is complicated. **Film map:** 22–23 = 1867 certified extract of damaged 1682–83 leaves ("Extrait de plusieurs feuilles") · 26 = 1676 baptisms · 28 = Dec 1690 – Jan 1691 baptisms · 29 = Mar 1691 baptisms (left) + Latin marriage register from 1673 (right) · 30 = damaged Latin marriages · 32 = marriages 1674–75, then blank · 34 = Latin burials 1670–80 · 36 = burials 1674–76 · 50 = new register opened Dec 1688 ("L'an mil six cent quatre-vingt-huit") · 57 = "Baptêmes… depuis le 29 novembre 1690" · **80–81 = "Mariages… depuis l'année 1690 et suivantes"** (p. 64–65). The index's image numbers (22 for the 1691 marriage, 25 for the 1694 baptism, 57 for the 1714 marriage) don't match the viewer.
- Fichier Origine: no Perrier/Olivier entry was found in s1–s2 (unchanged; not re-run).
- **WikiTree Perrier-40** (public API; it must be called over **https**, because plain http returns a 301 that looks like a rate-limit failure). The profile has a **marriage contract of 26 Nov 1690 before notary Antoine Adhémar**, drawn up at Chambly (source "Marchi généalogie"), with the note that 5 Aug 1691 is when the contract was filed and that there is "no record of the actual marriage". That fits the gap in the register. Parents per WikiTree: Laurent Perrier (Perrier-41, "b. 1639 Brest") × Françoise Chauvin, against Tanguay's Marie Guiltard. Brest and the parents come from Ancestry/FTW imports (SPECULATIVE). The 1714 census age of 49 gives b. c.1665. **The Adhémar contract is now the best route to an origin** (BAnQ Advitam / Parchemin; offline batch).
