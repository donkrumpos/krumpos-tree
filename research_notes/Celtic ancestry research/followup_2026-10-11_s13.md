## 1. Lamont / Normandin dit LaMonday

- The s11 question ("James" vs "Glode", which one d. 1872?) is answered by the censuses. Claude Sr. used "James" with census-takers (1850 Lamandy, 1860 and 1870 Monty) and was 89 in 1870. Claude Jr. ("Gload/Glode/Glade") is at Schuyler Falls in 1850, 1870 and 1880. So the Find a Grave "Glode LaMonday d. 15 Sep 1872, aged 98" is Sr. (bapt. 1791, so about 81). His age was overstated in the census and on the stone. Louise is not found in 1880 (Find a Grave gives 1878).
- Claude Jr.'s ages wander (b. 1820 / 1814 / 1826), but the place, the occupation and the 1820 baptism fit one man.
- This settles the family's shape. It does not prove that Mrs Bodoh belongs to it. The proof still needs the Plattsburgh registers (FS Center) or the Clinton surrogate.

## 2. Woods

- The s12 follow-up dismissed the Illinois Henry × Eunice couples as "unrelated". The 1837 Pike Co. deed reopens that: "Henry Woods & Eunice his wife", both marksmen as in Huron 1832, in Fulton Co. by 1837. His son Henry's death record gives an **Ohio birth in Dec 1832**, ten months after the Greenwich sale.
- For identity: same couple's names; both sign by mark; Henry b. 1794–97 (fits 30–39 in 1830 and 40–49 in 1840); not in Illinois in 1830; a child b. Ohio late 1832; the FS tree also puts him on an Ohio tax list in 1830 (unsourced).
- Against: Henry "b. Canada" in 1850; the tree has Mary Ann b. 1829 "Virginia" and John b. 1831 "Illinois" (census-derived, probably loose). If Henry was Catherine Cobb's brother (Catherine b. 1783 PA), a Canada birth in the 1790s is possible but unexplained.
- So: **the Greenwich couple = the Fulton Co. family is PROBABLE.** Henry = Mary's son is still unproven. Mary Woods is not in Henry's 1840 household, so she probably died c.1835–40 in Ohio.
- Celtic relevance: none yet. "Woods" could be English, Ulster-Scots or Irish, and the origin is still unknown.

## 4. Tool notes

- FS record search accepts wildcards: `q.surname=Lam*` with `q.residencePlace=Clinton, New York` + `f.collectionId` lists every LaMonday variant in a county census (1850 = 1401638, 1860 = 1473181, 1870 = 1438024, 1880 = 1417683, 1840 = 1786457, 1830 = 1803958). Results are ranked, so variants appear on page 1 and later pages drift to other surnames. `offset=100` pages.
- Searching with no name, only `birthLikeDate` + `birthLikePlace=Canada` + town, finds households indexed under an unexpected surname ("James Monty").
- `q.fatherGivenName` + `q.motherSurname` (e.g. Claude + Guyon) pulls a whole sibship from the Quebec index.
- 1840 census records have no age data in the index. "View Original Document" → the viewer; zoom the header and the row with the same x-range to read the columns.
- Full-text deed hits: `view=fullText&keywords=<w>`; the viewer's image box takes a number + Return.
- Tree "Sources" tab: `/tree/person/sources/<PID>` lists titles but no record links. Search the record instead.
