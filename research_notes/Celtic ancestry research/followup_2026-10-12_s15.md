## 1. Woods — what the children said

- The two daughters who stayed near Astoria (Mary Ann Tate, Idress Edgar) consistently said their father was born in **Canada**; Idress's 1900 entry specifies **English Canada**. Henry Jr. (who left for Missouri and Iowa) and Elizabeth Rebman (St. Louis) consistently said **New York**. Henry Sr. himself said Canada in 1850 and New York in 1860.
- Reading: the family was probably on the **New York–Upper Canada border** c.1794 (Niagara/St. Lawrence frontier), so both answers could be "true" depending on the informant. Nothing yet names a town, and nothing yet ties Henry to Mary Woods of Crawford Co. or to the Cobbs. No Celtic signal in any of it: "Canada Eng" argues for an English-speaking (Loyalist-era) family rather than a French-Canadian or Scottish one, but that is inference.
- New chronology: Greenwich Twp, Huron Co., sale 8 Feb 1832 → Henry Jr. b. 5 Dec 1832 "Dayton, Ohio" (one informant, a grandson; 1830 Montgomery Co. census has no Henry Woods, so treat as PROBABLE at best) → Elizabeth b. 29 Jun 1835 **Astoria, Fulton Co., Ill.** → Pike Co. deed 4 Mar 1837. Mary Ann b. c.1824 Ohio puts the Woods–Whitehead marriage by c.1823 in Ohio, which fits Mary Woods's 1824 Crawford Co. patent.
- Henry Jr.'s 1900 "married 36 years" (c.1864) and the Hill step-daughters correct Find a Grave's "m. Mary M. Hedge 1860": she was a widow Hill when he married her.

## 3. Tool notes

- **Find a Grave pages fetch fine from outside the browser** (memorial pages and `/memorial/search?firstname=&lastname=`); the location filter is still ignored, so filter the results by hand.
- **Missouri death certificates 1910–75** (`s1.sos.mo.gov/records/Archives/ArchivesMvc/DeathCertificates`): the form POSTs to `/Results`; set `LastName`, `FirstName`, `BeginYear`, `EndYear` by JS and click the Search input (Return does nothing). The PDF link is `www.sos.mo.gov/images/archives/deathcerts/<year>/<year>_<id>.PDF`; curl needs a browser User-Agent and a Referer (otherwise a Cloudflare "Just a moment" page); Chrome's PDF viewer screenshots black, so curl + `pdftoppm`. Spelling in the index is as written on the certificate ("Elizebeth").
- **Illinois death indexes** (`apps.ilsos.gov/isavital/deathsrch.jsp` pre-1916, `idphdeathsrch.jsp` 1916–72): the form has an input named `submit`, so call `HTMLFormElement.prototype.submit.call(form)`; fields `name` ("LAST, FIRST") + `county`, or `lastName`/`firstName`/`county`.
- FS: `browser_batch` with navigate + `javascript_tool` (5 s wait, `document.body.innerText`) reads 3–4 record pages per call. The persona API (`/platform/records/personas/<id>`) now returns 401 even when signed in. Collection IDs: 1880 = 1417683, 1900 = 1325221, 1820 = 1803955, 1830 = 1803958, 1890 veterans = 1877095.
- The FS image viewer opens with the Image Index panel over the image; close it (X, top right) before screenshotting; `zoom` on a region reads typed certificates easily.
