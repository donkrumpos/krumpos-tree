## 1. Chambly film DGS 005468704: corrected map

The film's catalog notes (film 1018130) list eight items: 1 baptism index 1706–1876 · 2 burial and marriage index 1707–1876 · 3 register 1706–27 · 4 duplicate 1706–27 · **5 copy of the first registers 1706–1748** · 6–8 registers 1727–1765.

Viewer calibration (s3 figures corrected):
- Baptism index: image 150 = the 1843 page (year-by-year lists). Image 210 = 1846–49.
- **Copy (item 5), legible 19th-century hand:** 597–600 = Oct–Dec 1743 · 608 = Sep–Oct 1744 · 609 = Nov–Dec 1744 · 610 = Dec 1744 · 650 = Sep 1748 · 655 = end of the copy.
- **Original 1727–65:** 664 = Sep 1727 · 720 = 1737 · 742 = 1739 · 754 = Sep–Oct 1741 · 760 = Aug 1742 · **762 = start of 1743** (763 is a second exposure of the same pages) · 764–765 = spring to Sep 1743 · **766 = 21 Oct – 28 Nov 1743** · 767 = Dec 1743, then the "1744" heading · **786 = Jul–Aug 1754** (about 3 images/yr after 1739, not 11). The s3 estimate of "785–790" was wrong.
- The Chambly copy also carries acts "de la paroisse de l'Immaculée Conception de la Ste Vierge à la Pointe Olivier" (e.g. Nov–Dec 1744). So the mission's acts were sometimes entered at Chambly, but not this marriage.

1743 marriages at Saint-Joseph de Chambly (both registers): Charles Robert × Madeleine … (Jan) · a Legrain dit Lavallée marriage (spring; signatures Carmel Levasseur, Jean Maillet, Charles Legrain dit Lavallée) · Laurent Pereau × Marie-Louise Boileau (late summer) · Jean-Marie Gilbert × Julie Lefort (21 Oct; signed by Commandant de Lépervanche, Hertel de Beaulac, Niverville) · Jacques Gautier dit St-Germain × Marie-Josephe Livernois, widow of Pierre Quenneville (indexed 21 Oct). The "Laurent Péro" who signs a witness line is Laurent Pereau/Perrault, not a Perrier.
