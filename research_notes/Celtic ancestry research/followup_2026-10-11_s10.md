## 2. Perrier's mother: two originals disagree

- 1690 contract (private act, Chambly fort, the groom present): "Françoise [Ch?]auvi[n/t]e". 1714 register (La Prairie priest): "Magd. Guillard".
- Possible explanations: (a) she was Madeleine-Françoise and one act uses each given name, while the surname differs because of a dit name or a remarriage; (b) the 1714 priest wrote what a witness told him; (c) a stepmother. WikiTree's "Madeleine Françoise Chauvin Guyard" (unsourced) already combines both.
- Either way the surname is French, and Brest is unaffected. The Barette contract (no. 97, same day) is a third, independent statement.

## 3. Lamont / Normandin dit LaMonday

- Timeline: Claude b. 1791 Saint-Denis; m. Beloeil 19 Feb 1816 Louise Guyon (b. 31 Aug 1800); **Louise bapt. Beloeil 5 Jul 1817** (their first child); Saint-Hyacinthe c.1819; Saint-Cyprien 1823–33; USA in the 1830s (Sally b. NY c.1836); **Malone 1840**; **Plattsburgh 1850**; Claude d. Plattsburgh 15 Sep 1872, Louise 28 May 1878.
- Bodoh timeline: Jacques Jr. b. 1836; children "b. Canada East" 1837–44 (census; loose); Louisa b. NY 1846; Abraham b. Plattsburgh 1847; **Chateaugay 1850**. So the two families overlap at Malone/Chateaugay and at Plattsburgh.
- Claude's daughters used **both surnames**: DeMarce's Salomé "Normandin" in the St. Peter's registers is "Sally Lamandy" in the census. That matches Mrs Bodoh, who is "Normandin" on Find a Grave and "Lamount/La Mont" in the 1908/1921 records.
- Proof is still missing. Best routes: (1) St. John the Baptist DGS 7857892 (FS Center); (2) **St. Peter's, Plattsburgh from 1853**, for Bodoh baptisms with LaMonday sponsors; (3) Drouin/PRDH for a Beaudin × Normandin marriage, if it took place in Quebec.
