## 1. Brest municipal archives

- **Technique (much faster than s7):** open `…/4DCGI/Web_RegistreArt<COTE>/<session>`. The viewer form (`Web_RegistreChangePage`, POST; hidden fields Table/Left/Right/Afficher, `select` = view no., `wafficher` = "Voir la vue") can be submitted from the page with `fetch(form.action, {method:'POST', body:…, credentials:'include'})` in a loop. Each call returns in under a second and creates `Images/tempimages/<COTE>_<NNN>.jpg`, which curl then fetches in seconds. A bare curl POST is refused (503); it needs the browser session. Run long loops as a fire-and-forget async function, because a blocking loop times out the JS tool after 45 s.
- **GG470 (Saint-Sauveur/Recouvrance, contemporary table 1669–1715, 230 views):** baptisms A→Z (view 1 = A; G ≈ 40, M ≈ 60, O = 69, **P = 70–79**), marriages (pages headed "letter + M"; **P = views 115–118**), burials (**P = views 204–208**). Two pages per view; entries are by year, then folio + r/v + given name + surname.
  - Baptisms P: 1685 f. 3r Marie M. Perier; 1686 f. 11v Estienne Perrier; 1688 f. 39r Françoise M. Perrier; 1688 f. 65v Catherine Perrier; 1691 f. 52r Guillaume Perier; 1692 f. 78v Thomas Perie; 1694 f. 21v Guillaume Perie; 1695 f. 73r Marie L. Perrier; 1696 f. 79r [smudged] Perier. (Also "Anne Pereur" 1682, a different name.)
  - **Act read, GG303 (1685) view 2, f. 3r:** "Marie Marguerite fille naturelle et légitime de noble homme Estienne Perrier lieutenant de la marine, et de damoiselle Marie Marguerite Launay sa compagne, demeurante à Brest du côté de Recouvrance", born 15 Jan, baptized next day; godparents noble homme Nicolas Marion, notaire royal, and damoiselle Marguerite Le Run, wife of Michel de Launay, sieur de Salvert. That's a naval-officer family, not our ship's carpenter.
  - Marriages P 1669–1702: no Perier. Burials P 1669–1707: Françoise M. Perrier 1689 (f. 54v), Catherine and Louis Perier 1691, Marie-Thérèse Perrier 1697 (f. 71r); no Laurent/Loran.
- **GG468 (Sept-Saints annual tables 1686–98, 136 views; clean 19th-c. hand; B/M/S columns):** 1686 P = view 8, 1687 = 15, 1688 = 20–21, 1689 = 27–28, 1690 = 39–40. All NEGATIVE for Perier. **1690 L section: "le Bourhis, Yves", burial July 1690**, so "Le Bourhis" can be filed under L. 1691–98 not done (≈ views 42–136, about ten per year).
- **GG482 (19th-c. tables):** pp. 1–38 Sept-Saints deaths 1711–21 (P = view 15–16, G = 6–7): NEGATIVE. p. 102 = view 52 (Lambézellec births B): NEGATIVE for Jean son of Jean. pp. 129–141 = views 65–71 (Lambézellec marriages, men) and 142–154 = 71–77 (women): NEGATIVE.
- So Laurent and Françoise are not visible in any Brest table 1669–1721 checked so far. Possible reasons: (a) they were Sept-Saints parishioners who died 1691–1710 (GG468 1691–98 and GG469 still to do); (b) the mother is indexed under her maiden name; (c) they lived in a rural trève; (d) Brest in the 1690 contract means the Brest area.
