## 1. Why s4 missed the act

The catalog's "registers 1727–1765" for Saint-Joseph de Chambly include a **third volume (1739–65) that opens with the Pointe-Olivier mission's own register**. It is filmed after the 1766–67 fragment (viewer 799–811) and the item target at 812–815. s4 read the Chambly volume proper (762–767 for 1743) and the 19th-century copy (597–600). The volume-3 register starts at viewer 816. The alphabetical index entry (005468704_00664_00002) presumably indexes this volume, which is why it has the right date.

## 2. Tanguay (free secondary source)

Tanguay, *Dictionnaire généalogique des familles canadiennes*, vol. 7 (archive.org `dictionnaireg07tang`), s.v. VIGEANT, gives:
- Jean I, b. 1672, son of François × Jeanne Basin of **Montlieu, diocese of Saintes**; m. 7 Jan 1713 La Prairie, Marie-Anne Perier-Olivier; d. Chambly 1756.
- Their son "Jean-Baptiste, b. 14 juin 1720; m. 1746 à Marie-Charlotte Besset"; daughter "Marie… m. 7 nov. 1768 à Joseph-Marie Paquet".

The dates are muddled, as the index and the images show, but the family structure agrees. The full-text OCR can be grepped locally; it is useful for other pre-1765 Quebec questions.
