## 1. Woods

- Huron Co. in 1830 included Greenwich Twp (Firelands, settled from Fairfield Co., Conn.). The sellers were Greenwich, Conn., proprietors. Henry bought in Nov 1830, so he left Liberty Twp soon after the June 1830 census. He sold again in Feb 1832, by then "of Greenwich".
- Henry and Eunice signed by mark. Mary signed alone in 1835 (by mark? not re-checked).
- Henry is not in the 1840 Ohio index under Huron or Crawford, so he may have left Ohio or died. Mary's death is still unfound (c.1835–45?).
- Next (online): Greenwich Twp cemetery and church records; Henry Woods × Eunice in the 1840–50 censuses outside Ohio (the given-name trick: Henry + spouse Eunice, b. 1790–1800). Offline: Huron Co. probate court.

## 2. Lamont / Normandin dit LaMonday

- Full text reaches Clinton Co. deeds but not its surrogate records, so an estate file for the 1872 death can only come from the court or the County Clerk (already on the offline batch).
- Sally Lamondy (1872) is a small new lead on who died in 1872. In 1850 "Gload Lamando" (b. 1820) was at Schuyler Falls, and in 1853 Claude Lamonda "& Stellie his wife" sold land. If Stellie = Sallie, then Sally selling alone in Jul 1872 suggests Claude Jr. was dead or absent. Compare with Find a Grave's "Glode LaMonday d. 15 Sep 1872, aged 98". The September death comes *after* the July sale, so that doesn't fit Claude Jr. Unresolved.

## 5. Tool notes

- FS Full-Text URL parameters: `q.text`, `q.anyPlace`, `q.anyDate.from`, `q.anyDate.to`, `count`. `q.anyPlace` behaves like extra keywords (it matched "Franklin" in a Tennessee newspaper), so check each hit's county. A `(A OR B) AND (x OR y OR z)` query with common words in the second group can explode (338k hits).
- `get_page_text` was refused on familysearch.org this session; `javascript_tool` reading `document.body.innerText` worked. Output is capped at ~1,000 characters, so read in slices.
- Film viewer: `/search/film/<DGS>?i=<n-1>` opens the thumbnail grid at that image; double-click the thumbnail, then use the image box.
