## 1. The Adhémar contract (how it was found)

- **Advitam** keyword "Adhemar" (no accent), facet *CN – Greffes de notaires*, gives **Antoine Adhémar dit Saint-Martin, 1668–1714, CN601,S2**; "Voir les documents" opens BAnQ numérique (`52327/4026468`). The whole greffe is digitized: four chronological repertories, two name indexes, and the acts in volumes of 200 numbers. Volume links follow the pattern `collections.banq.qc.ca/notice/redirect/06M,CN601,S2enf03NN` (0310 = nos. 1601–1800, 13 Apr 1690 – 9 Jan 1691; 0311 = nos. 1801–2000, 9 Jan – 7 Dec 1691).
- **Each act has a cover sheet** (date + summary + "no NNNN"). Acts are filed by the date they entered the étude, so a private contract (*sous seing privé*) deposited later is filed under the deposit date. That is why no. 1905 sits among the August 1691 acts (no. 1903 = image 450, no. 1904 = 452–456, **no. 1905 = 457–459**, no. 1906 = 460).
- **Technique (works without the slow viewer):** in the volume page, `drupalSettings.bitstreams_liste` holds every image's IIIF id (flatten it and keep the entries with `.id`). The images are on `img.banq.qc.ca/iiif/2/<id>/full/<w>,/0/default.jpg`, which curl can fetch directly with no cookie. Pull 40-image ranges at 500–700 px, make a montage with ImageMagick, find the cover sheets, then fetch the covers at high resolution (`pct:` regions work). Contract image ids: 457 `DrEfikXiAIGbhvRmmb7OEQ` (cover), 458 `dGqvMWlbcvBnncZGw5NIdg` (text), 459 `6ooMvTHdie1uyiiDI6WCJQ` (end + signatures).
- **Text (image 458, opening):** "Sont comparus en leurs personnes Ollivier Perier fils de Loran Perier [~~charpantier de na[vire]~~] et de Françoise [?alvite] [~~struck word~~] ses père et mère demeurant à Brest d'une part, et Marie Bessep[t] fille de Jean Bessept et de Anne Saigneur habitants de Chambly ses père et mère, garçon et fille à marier…", followed by the usual clauses (marriage in the Catholic Church, community of goods, *préciput*, *douaire*). **End (image 459):** "Fait et passé au fort St-Louis le vingt-sixième novembre mil six cent quatre-vingt-dix, en présence de monsieur Desbergères, capitaine commandant audit fort", the sieurs Devarlet and Charles …, Jean Dupuy and others, inhabitants of Chambly. Marks for those who could not sign; signatures include "Ollivier", Desbergères, Devarlet, Anne Richard and Nicolas Ravise(?).
- **Cover (image 457):** old docket "Contract de mariage d'Olivier Perrier & Marie Bessette du 26 9bre 1690 … remis en l'estude le 5e aoust 1691 … sous seing privé". Archivist's pencil: "No 1905 — contrat de mariage entre Olivier Perrier et Marie Bessede du 26 nov. 1690 remis en l'étude d'Adhémar le 5 août 1691".
- The deposit date (5 Aug 1691) is also the FS-indexed "marriage" date, which explains why the La Prairie register has no act for it (s6).

## 3. Brest registers (new venue)

- **The Archives départementales du Finistère** (reachable this time) hold only Brest's civil registers from 1793 (3 E 29); a search for baptisms 1640–1700 found nothing.
- **The Archives municipales et métropolitaines de Brest** (`archives.mairie-brest.fr` → "Dossiers et registres numérisés" → Registres paroissiaux-État civil → **GG Registres paroissiaux de Brest 1608–1793**) have, free online: Château 1608–1741, **Sept-Saints 1620–1721** (53 registers), Saint-Louis 1710–92, **Recouvrance 1632–1792** (178), and **24 volumes of 19th-c. alphabetical tables**. Name search covers only c.1790+.
- Viewer: `…/4DCGI/Web_RegistreArt<COTE>/…`; choosing a view number in the `select` and clicking "Voir la vue" creates `…/Images/tempimages/<COTE>_<NNN>.jpg` (≈2 MB, 5500 px). curl can fetch it **only after** the viewer has created it, and the server is slow (2–8 min per image; re-fetch if the JPEG is truncated). Two table pages per view (view ≈ page/2 + 1).
- **Table map:** GG481 = Sept-Saints births 1620–85 (pp. 21–141), deaths 1620–85 (142–227), marriages 1669–85 (228–258). GG482 = Sept-Saints deaths/marriages 1711–21 (1–59), **Recouvrance births 1632–63 (60–98)**, Lambézellec births 1626–68 (99–128) and marriages (129–154), **Château births 1608–1731 (155–159)**, Quilbignon births 1606–84 (163–181). GG470 = Saint-Sauveur (Recouvrance) BMS 1669–1715 (230 views). GG468/469 = Sept-Saints annual tables 1686–1710.
- **Results:** Bourhis: GG481 p. 34 has only "Bourhis (le) Françoise fille de François, 1666". GG482 pp. 62–63 have only "Bourch (le)" children of Martin (1657–63). Château p. 155 and Quilbignon pp. 164–165 have none. **Perier**: GG481 pp. 110–113 run Penhors → Pérignon → Perrault → Perrot (no Perier; "Perrot Olivier fils de Mathurin 1664" is another family). GG482 pp. 88–89 (Recouvrance 1632–63) and pp. 158–159 (Château) have none.
- **Not done:** GG470 (Recouvrance 1669–1715, the best remaining place for a ship's carpenter's son born c.1665–70), Recouvrance 1664–68 (no table), Lambézellec p. 102. All are on the offline batch.
