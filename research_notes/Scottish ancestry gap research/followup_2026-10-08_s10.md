## Headline

1. **Sally Cobb's death date is resolved:** she died **4 Aug 1846**, not 8 Aug 1847. The source is Fred M. Sherwood, *Descendants of Asa Cobb* (1993), p. 30, which is **online and full-text searchable in the FS Digital Library** (item 131993). Abraham's remarriage on 7 Aug 1847 therefore no longer conflicts. Sally stays **PROBABLE** as Catherine's mother; nothing names her directly.
2. **Sally's parents:** Asa Cobb IV (1781 Orange Co., NY – 1867 Liberty Twp, Crawford Co., OH) and Catherine Woods (1783 PA – 1844), daughter of Mary Woods.
   - Asa was Squire (JP) of Liberty Twp for 15 years, 1831–46.
   - The immigrant Asa Cobb I was born c.1701 in "Lands", England, and came to New York c.1766.
   - **English, not Scottish.**
3. **Abraham Shaffstall's parents** (FS tree): Solomon Schoffstall (1777–1849) × Catharina (Haag) (1783–1858). Both died in **Liberty Twp, Crawford Co.**, the township where Catherine was living with the Petermans in 1850. The pedigree is wholly PA German.
4. **Why Catherine went to Door County:** her full brother **John Adam Shaffstall married at Sturgeon Bay on 1 Jan 1862** and was still there in 1870. That was four years before her own 1866 marriage there.
5. **Mildred Guernsey in 1900:** the census has "Mildred G. Boyce", b. Mar 1880, Michigan, in Charles and Catherine Boyce's household at Sebastopol. PROBABLE (index).
6. **John Loami Guernsey was born in Michigan (probably Ionia), not Hudson, IN.** His 1940 Texas death record, his 1891 marriage record and four censuses all say Michigan. birth_place changed. PROBABLE.
7. **Cadys:** still unidentified. NEGATIVE.
8. **Hannah Walker × Catherine Finkle:** no new evidence; still PROBABLE.
9. **Scottish: still NEGATIVE.** The Cobb genealogy mentions Scottish origins only for collateral in-law families (Lowry, Buchanan, McLean), none of them ancestors.

## 1. Sally Cobb line (priority 1)

### (a) *Descendants of Asa Cobb* (Sherwood 1993)
FS catalog (koha:638104), FHL 929.273 C633sf, film 1750736 item 12, DGS 7540132. The "click here" digital link opens FS Digital Library item 131993, which is viewable online and has "Search inside".

**Tip:** in-page `fetch('/library/books/api/altopages?item_id=131993&limit=20&q=<term>')` returns full-page OCR text as JSON. It works only sequentially; parallel calls get an HTML challenge page.

- **p. 30 (image 100), no. 142** (text read via OCR):
  - "Sally. Born, 14 Aug 1813, in Clifford Twp, Susquehanna Co, PA. Died, 4 Aug 1846. She married Abraham Shaffstall."
  - "Very little is known of her other than she died 4 Aug 1846. Records show she married Abrahan Shaffstall 5 Jun 1834." [The index says 6 Jun.]
  - The Shaffstall family "still live on the Mt Zion Rd north of Bucyrus. She is buried in Bucyrus."
- **pp. 23–26 (images 93–96), no. 24, Asa Cobb IV** (Asa III, Asa II, Asa I):
  - b. 22 Sep 1781, Orange Co., NY; d. 6 May 1867, Liberty Twp, Crawford Co., OH
  - migrated to Liberty Twp 1823; buried May 1867, Bucyrus (Woods–Cobb Cemetery)
  - 1860 census, Liberty Twp, with son Henry; Clifford Twp 1810, on the 1815 tax list and in the 1820 census
  - occupation: Squire. Named in his father's will (1808) and his mother's will (1816).
  - **Wife Catherine Woods**, b. 2 Jul 1783. The book quotes a *Boston Transcript* query of 3 Jan 1934 calling her the "daughter of Mary Woods"; Mary Woods travelled to Ohio with the family.
  - Children as listed when they left PA: Dudley (1802), Asa (1804), Henry (1806), Eliphalet (1807), Elam, **Sally (14 Aug 1813)**, Jedidiah (1816), William (1818), John (1820), Cyperion (1822)
  - Asa was elected Squire of Liberty Twp in 1831 and served "fifteen years serving five terms" until April 1846
  - p. 57 (image 57): photo of the headstone, "Asa Cobb IV 1781–1867, son of Asa Cobb III 1750–1808 [and] Sarah Stephens Cobb 1753–1816, Woods–Cobb Cemetery, Crawford County Ohio"
- **Catalog note:** "descendants of Asa Cobb who was born ca. 1701 in Lands, England. He immigrated to America with his four sons and landed in New York ca. 1766." Asa Cobb I died c.1775 in Dutchess Co. and was buried in Orange Co.
- **Scottish search inside the book:** "Scotland" (1 hit), "Scotch" (2), "Scottish" (1), "McLean" (5). All are collateral:
  - the source list (Scottville, MI; McLean, VA)
  - p. 22: John Lowry's antecedents "may have come from the north of Ireland from a family of Scottish origin" (Lowrys married Cobbs on the Zipron line)
  - p. 190: the Buchanan family
  - p. 84: Noah Cobb m. Hannah McLean, daughter of James McLean and Catherine Shaffer
  - **None is in Sally's ancestry. NEGATIVE.**
- "Peterman" appears once, p. 150: a nephew of Wesley Stiles (husband of Sally's sister Mary Ann). Unrelated to the 1850 household. "Cady": 0 hits.

### (b) The death-date conflict
- Find a Grave 100211542, "Sarah Louisa Cobb Shaffstall": no photo; "Burial Details Unknown"; death given as 8 Aug 1847 (aged 33); the bio guesses Ohio. This memorial is the source of the FS date.
- Ohio marriages index, Abraham Shoffstall × Susannah Coberly: **7 Aug 1847**, Crawford Co. (marriage date, Role Groom).
- **Conclusion:** Sherwood's 4 Aug 1846 is a year before the remarriage. Find a Grave's 8 Aug 1847 looks garbled. **PROBABLE (compiled genealogy).**
- Abraham's 1850 household has **Martha J., 4** (b. c.1845–46). She was born before the 1847 marriage, so she is probably Sally's last child; perhaps Sally died after the birth. SPECULATIVE.
