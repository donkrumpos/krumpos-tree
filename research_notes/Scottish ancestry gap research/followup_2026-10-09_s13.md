## 1. Henry Martin's arrival, NARA M237 July 1851 (priority 1)

**Method.** The FS "New York Passenger Lists" index (coll. 1849782) carries `FilmNbr` per persona. Known anchors fix the roll-to-film mapping: Marmion (roll 103) = FHL **175459**; A H Schultz (roll 108) = **175464**. So roll N = 175356 + N:

| Roll | FHL film | DGS | FS image-group title |
|---|---|---|---|
| 101 | 175457 | 004678756 | "101 – 1 Jul 1851–16 Jul 1851" (639 images) |
| 102 | 175458 | 004678757 | "102 – 17 Jul 1851–4 Aug 1851" (644 images) |

Adding `q.filmNumber=<film>` to an hr query ranks that film's entries first. Paging until the film changes therefore lists every matching indexed entry on the roll.

**Index sweeps (both rolls):**
- Given name Henry (fuzzy: Henry, Henery, Hy, Harry, Heinrich…), all ages: 289 entries on roll 101 and 305 on roll 102. Filtered to ages 14–28 and non-German origins: no Martin-like surname.
  - The only "Henry Martin" is 8, England, *Queen of the West* (roll 101).
  - **"Henry Mafin", 20, Ireland, *Empire State*** (roll 101, image 314) was checked on the image: the name reads **"Henry Magin"** (or Mafin), not Martin. Ruled out.
- Surname Martin (and Marten, Martyn, Mertin, Morton, which FS standardizes to the same group): 49 entries on roll 101 and 41 on roll 102. **No Henry.** Notables:
  - Thos Martin 21 Ireland (*Waterloo*); Stephen Martin 20 England (*Robt Kelly*); Robert Martin 21 GB (*Medal*); Jno Martin 20 Ireland (*Constantine*); Patrick Martin 20 Ireland (*Alice Wilson*).
  - **Owen Marten 20 and Dennis Marten 15, *E. Buckley***, roll 102.
- **The *E. Buckley*, read page by page on the images:** "List or Manifest of all the Passengers taken on board the E. Buckley whereof David Brown is Master, from Liverpool", roll 102, **images 34–42** (~380 passengers; the 18 Jul 1851 arrival matches the Famine Irish index). Every name was read.
  - Dennis Marten and Owen Marten are on image 38, about five lines apart.
  - **No Henry Martin**, and no Henry of any surname near them. An infant "E. Buckley" (named for the ship) is on image 37.
  - **NEGATIVE.** This Owen Marten (20) is a different man from the Marmion's "Owen Martin, 20" (with "Henry Martin, 16", Aug 1851).
- **Conclusion:** on rolls 101–102 Henry is unindexed, or garbled in both names, or not there. Alternatives:
  - "July 1851" was approximate: the Marmion (15 Aug 1851, roll 103) stays SPECULATIVE.
  - He landed at Quebec or Saint John and entered New York overland, which is common for Belfast emigrants.
  - A blind read of the remaining ~1,270 images (≈40 names each) was judged too low-yield to do.

## 2. Martin Peters's "wife and four children" (priority 2)

- **FS Full-Text, Door Co.:** "Martin Peters" (25 hits), "Eliza Peters" (10), "William Peters" (15, all 20th-century), "heirs of Martin Peters" (0), "Elias Walker" (1), "Mary Jane Walker" (1980s only).
  - **The Peters estate papers (1861–63) never name the children.** Only Henry Martin's 1863 petition says "wife and four children".
  - No guardianship for Walker or Peters minors turns up. **NEGATIVE.**
- **Martin Peters was Catholic:** a certificate in Door Co. "Religious Documents" (FS full text; recorded later, dated **10 Mar 1860**). Martin Peters and Marris Reardon presided at the meeting of "the first Catholic Society" at the house of Joseph Senator [?], town of Sturgeon Bay. **Martin Peters, Patrick Ryan and "Joseph Senassor" [very probably Joseph Lavassor]** were elected trustees of "the first Catholic society of Door County". Witness David Houle. So Peters knew the Lavassors before marrying Eliza.
- **Other Peters full-text items:** deed from George Hodgkins (1860, $130); articles of agreement with Denis A. Reed of Sturgeon Bay (10 Nov 1860; witnesses Henry Harris, Joseph Harris); a deed involving Gilbert Morrow (recorded 1889).
- **Analysis:** in Oct 1861 the possible children were Elias (8), Mary Jane (7), Charles (5), John W. (3), Eliza A. (2), plus William (b. c.1861; perhaps born after Peters's death). The card file's "she – 11 ch." (below) matches 5 Walker + 1 Peters + 5 Martin and supports William as Eliza's Peters child. "Four children" is unexplained: Mary Jane may have been placed out (she is absent in 1870), or the petition counted only part of the family. **Unresolved.**

### New Door County finds

  - *Door County Advocate*, Thu 20 Oct 1870, p. 3, "Local Matters" (image read): "The oldest son of Mr. Henry Martin, of Sevastopol, died last Saturday night, of heart disease." Saturday = 15 Oct 1870.

### Robert Hutchinson to 1910
- **1900 US census, Lockport city Ward 4, ED 58, sheet 14A, enumerated 12 Jun 1900 (coll. 1325221; image 27 of 31; read):**
  - "Hutchison, Robert", Head, W M, b. **Mar 1840**, 60, married 9 years; b. **Canada Eng**; **father b. England; mother b. Canada E[ng]**; immigrated **1850**, 50 years in US, **Na**[turalized]; day labourer; rents house.
  - Rebecca, wife, b. Nov 1859, 41, married 9 years, 1 child born / 1 living, b. NY (parents NY).
- **1892 NY state census (coll. 1529100; index):** Robert Hutchinson, 51, b. Canada, Lockport; Rebecca Hutchinson, 51, b. Canada (index or enumerator discrepancy).
- **1910 US census, Lockport Ward 4 (coll. 1727033; index):** Robert Hutchinson, 70, b. Canada, immigrated 1845, married; Rebecca E., b. NY (index age 70).
- **Fit with the 1860–62 Robert:**
  - Canada-born (b. 1838–40).
  - Arrived 1850, matching the family's move from the Town of Niagara (1848 roll) to Lockport (1850 census).
  - Naturalized, matching the 25 Oct 1862 Lockport naturalization.
  - Parents' birthplaces England/Canada, matching Thomas (England) and Sophia (Niagara, Canada).
  - **Strengthens the PROBABLE.** It is not a proof: no document names his parents.
- **Not found:**
  - Robert in 1865, 1870 or 1880 at Lockport (index; possibly mis-indexed).
  - His death: Find a Grave has no Robert Hutchinson or Hutchison b. 1838–41 in Niagara Co.
  - The 1875 Lockport Robert (b. 1847, wife Catherine) and the 1865 Newfane Robert (b. 1851, David's son) are other men.
