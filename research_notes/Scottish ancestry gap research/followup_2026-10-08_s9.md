## 2. Catherine (Shaffstall) Boyce (priority 2)

### (a) Death record
- **FS "United States, Wisconsin, Deaths"** (derived from Wisconsin Death Records 1867–1907, film 1310174, DGS 004207423, image 65; **image restricted online**). Index:
  - Catherine Schafstall Boyce, d. 1902, Green Bay, Brown Co.
  - Buried Bayside Cemetery, Sturgeon Bay; resided Sturgeon Bay
  - Born 1840, Ohio; married; aged 62; female, white
  - **Father: Abram Schafstall**; spouse Charles Boyce
- **Wisconsin Death Index:** Catherine Boyce, 28 Sep 1902, Brown Co. (vol. 04, p. 0068).

### (b) Obituary
***Door County Democrat*, Sturgeon Bay, 4 Oct 1902, p. 8, "Death of Mrs. Chas. Boyce."** (Door County Library archive; search "Mrs. Chas. Boyce"). **CONFIRMED (image).** Summary:
- Died "early Monday morning": that is 29 Sep 1902, against the indexed 28 Sep, a Sunday. A death in the small hours may explain it; the reliquary keeps 28 Sep at **St. Vincent's hospital, Green Bay**; of Sevastopol; her husband and a son at her bedside. Aged 62, in good health until stomach trouble "baffled all medical skill".
- "Born in **Hudson, Indiana**, on Jan. 10, 1840". This is wrong: the 1850, 1860, 1870 and 1900 censuses all say Ohio. Hudson, Steuben Co., IN is where her Guernsey sons were born.
- "Married to Chas. Boyce in 1866. Seven children … six of whom are now living":
  - Thos. Boyce, of Milwaukee
  - "Herbert A." [= Albert H.] Boyce, of Minneapolis
  - Mrs. John McCauley, of Milwaukee
  - Mrs. H. A. Wagener, of Sturgeon Bay
  - Mrs. Richard Gilbert, of Sturgeon Bay town
  - Miss Ruth, at home
- "There are also several children by a former husband, **Mr. Guernsey**." The obituary does not mention a Gordon.
- Buried the following Wednesday at **Bayside cemetery**. The service was at her home in Sevastopol, conducted by Rev. S. Groenfeldt.
- Same issue, p. 1, Personal column (image):
  - "Mr. and Mrs. Thos. Boyce, of Milwaukee were here Wednesday in attendance at the funeral of Mr. Boyce's mother."
  - "A. H. Boyce and wife arrived here Wednesday, having been called here by the death of his mother Mrs. Chas. Boyce."

### (c) Her father, Abraham Shaffstall/Schoffstall
- FS tree (read only): Abraham Schoffstall, b. 8 Feb 1809, bapt. 21 Apr 1809, **Lykens Twp, Dauphin Co., PA**; d. 10 Jul 1880, Lincoln Co., Kansas (Find a Grave: buried Lincoln, KS; residence 1880 Marion Twp, Lincoln Co.).
- **Marriages (Ohio marriage indexes):**
  - **Sally Cobb**, 6 Jun 1834, Crawford Co., OH
  - **Susannah Coberly**, 7 Aug 1847, Crawford Co., OH
- **Censuses (FS index):**
  - 1850, Pleasant Twp, Van Wert Co., OH: Abm Shofstall 41 PA; Susana 26 VA; John 14 OH; Elame 8 OH; Martha J 4; Sarah E 2; Evans D 1
  - 1860 and 1870, **Salem Twp, Steuben Co., IN**, with Susanna; Catherine married Justus in Steuben Co. in 1857
- The Bucyrus Shoffstalls (Peter, b. 1810 PA) are probably kin; Shoffstalls were also still in Lykens Twp, Dauphin Co. in 1850–60. SPECULATIVE. Peter's 1850 household has a Catharine aged 1, a different girl.

### (d) Her mother, probably Sally Cobb
- FS tree: Sarah Louisa "Sally" Cobb, b. 14 Aug 1813, Clifford, Susquehanna Co., PA; d. 8 Aug 1847, Crawford Co., OH; buried Bucyrus. Children: John Adam (1837–1918), William (1838–1850), **Catherine (1840–1902)**, Elam (1842–1865).
- FS sources for Sally: the 1834 marriage, Find a Grave, and "Descendants of Asa Cobb".
- **PROBABLE**, because:
  - the marriage date fits
  - the 1850 household still had the older children (John, "Elame"), with Catherine's age between them
  - Abraham remarried in Aug 1847
- **Conflict:** FS has Sally dying 8 Aug 1847, the day after Abraham's second marriage. One of those dates is wrong (the marriage may be a licence date, or her death date estimated).
- **Consequences:**
  - The s8 guess that the Petermans (her 1850 household) were her grandparents, through a "Mary Ann Peterman", is weakened. They were more likely foster carers after 1847.
  - "William and Mary Ann Cady" (1866 marriage) were probably also guardians or foster parents. They are unidentified; William Cady × Mary Ann searches in OH, IN, MI and WI, 1840–60, were NEGATIVE.

### (e) The Gordon husband
**NEGATIVE.** No Catherine Guernsey/Gurnsey × Gordon marriage in FS indexes, and no Catherine Gordon in Door Co. in 1860. The 1902 obituary names only "a former husband, Mr. Guernsey". "Gorden" on the 1866 record stays unexplained.

### (f) Albert H. Boyce
**CONFIRMED** as Catherine's son:
- 1902 newspaper (above)
- FS tree: b. 24 Jan 1876, Sevastopol (NUMIDENT: Sturgeon Bay); d. 17 Apr 1944, Los Angeles
- California County Birth & Death Records: d. 17 Apr 1944, **Hollywood**; parents **Charles Boyce × Catherine Shafstall**
- California Death Index: mother's maiden name Shafstall
- Wisconsin marriage to Grace E. Denby (parents Charles Boyer × Catherine Thaffstall)

He was in Minneapolis in 1902 (a daughter, Nell Catherine, was born there in 1903) and in Los Angeles from 1920 to 1940. So he is the "Albert H. Boyce of Hollywood" at Gerald Martin's 1931 funeral, and Mildred's half-uncle.
