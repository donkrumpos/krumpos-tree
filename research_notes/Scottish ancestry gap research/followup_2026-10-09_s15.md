## Method notes
- FS full persona records: `/platform/records/personas/<entry.id>` (the hr *entry* id, not the person id inside gedcomx) gives FatherBirthPlace, MotherBirthPlace, CntYearsMarried and CntChildren for the 1900 and 1910 censuses, without opening the image.
- Find a Grave: `/cemetery/<id>/memorial-search?lastname=X` (in-page fetch) lists one cemetery; cemetery and national searches work with DOMParser. A background loop must run on a page of the site it fetches from (one loop was started on Find a Grave by mistake and its FS calls failed).
- The NYS Death Index CSV is reliable to about 1940; the 1941–50 rows often have no certificate number or place.
