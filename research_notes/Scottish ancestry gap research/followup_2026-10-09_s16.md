## Method notes
- **Archives of Michigan Preservica:** the search results page lists items by surname, and each item page gives the index (parents' names, death date, county) in a "Family Name: … Given Name: …" text block. Fetch item pages in-page and regex the fields. Loops over more than ~10 items go in a background IIFE (the 45 s tool timeout otherwise).
- **Image viewer:** typing an image number into the box and pressing Return works; `?i=` in the URL does not. The arrow at the right edge steps one image. Taking a 0.6-scale zoom of the spread per image makes the margin names legible, about 6 images per batch call.
- **Door archive viewer:** set `#rc-scale-select` to 0.5 and dispatch `change`, then scroll `#rc-tiled-image-container`, to read a column. The archive's date label can differ from the masthead: this issue is filed as 1923-08-23 but printed 16 Aug 1923.
- A page navigation wipes the in-page helpers (`hr`, `withPar`), and a background loop then fails silently. Re-define the helpers after every navigation and wrap loops in try/catch.
