## Headline

1. **St Mark's Anglican, Niagara: "Mary Louisa Hutchinson, of Thomas and Sophia", baptised 21 Dec 1831** (Rev. Robert Addison's register, printed in OHS *Papers and Records* vol. 3). **CONFIRMED (published transcript of the register).** This is the first record that names Thomas and Sophia as parents of a Mary born in Niagara c.1831. It matches Mary (Hutchinson) Lavassor, "born in Niagara in the year 1831". **Mary Lavassor as Eliza's sister: PROBABLE → PROBABLE (strong).** The only gap is the middle name: "Mary A." on the Find a Grave memorial against "Mary Louisa" here.
2. **The same register: "Sophia Miller, of William and Margery", baptised 19 Apr 1801 at Niagara**, listed with "Louisa Miller" of the same parents (twins?). Louisa was buried as an infant on 25 Sep 1801. William and Margery Miller also had Margaret (bapt. 1792) and James (bapt. 1799) baptised at Niagara. **This is a PROBABLE candidate for Eliza's grandparents**: same name, same town, and a birth year one year off the 1850 census age (48 → b. c.1802). The Hutchinsons were having children baptised at the same church thirty years later. Naming Mary's daughter "Louisa" after Sophia's dead twin would also fit, though that is SPECULATIVE. **This Niagara couple is not the Rhynd/Perth William Millar × Margaret Laing**, who were baptising children in Scotland in 1804–23. They are also not provably the Newcastle 1799 "William Miller and Margery Laing", because the Niagara couple already had a child in 1792. Nothing yet shows whether Margery was a Laing.
3. **"Mrs John Hutchison" of Youngstown (d. 29 May 1882) is NOT our family.** Her obituary, read from the page image, says she was born in Co. Fermanagh, Ireland, in 1804 and was the daughter of Wm. Quade.
4. **Mary (Johnson) Maggle had a sister, "Mrs. Anton Meri"** (Door County Democrat, 6 Nov 1909). This is a new lead on the Johnson family. The mother is still unidentified.

## Lead 1 — NYS Historic Newspapers

### (a) Niagara County News, 2 Jun 1882: Mrs John Hutchison — CONFIRMED (image read), NEGATIVE for our line
- The card index said p. 3, but the obituary is on **p. 2, col. 3, under "OBITUARY"** (https://www.nyshistoricnewspapers.org/?a=d&d=ncn18820602-01.1.2). OCR renders the name as "flutchiiK m", which is why the search missed it. Read from the image: "We are called upon this week to record the death of Mrs. John Hutchison, one of the oldest residents of this town, who died of dropsy Monday evening May 29th at half past nine o'clock … She was **born in County Fermonah [Fermanagh], Ireland, in 1804**, and at the age of 19 she came to this country landing in Kingston, where she remained about a year, when she removed to Buffalo and was a resident there eight years, removing from that city to Ransomville, and has lived in this town ever since … The deceased was a **daughter of the late Wm. Quade** … She leaves a husband, a son, William, and a sister — Mrs. Robert McKim … Rev. Wm. Glover, of Lockport, officiating. The remains were taken to North Ridge cemetery."
- Card of thanks, p. 5 (https://www.nyshistoricnewspapers.org/?a=d&d=ncn18820602-01.1.5): "JOHN HUTCHISON. W. A. HUTCHISON. Youngstown, June 1st, 1882."
- **Assessment:** this is the Youngstown storekeeping family (W. A. Hutchison). It is Irish and Methodist, with no Thomas or Sophia. **Not kin as far as the evidence goes.** Close this lead.

### (b) Sweep of the Niagara County News, 1878–92 (OCR, then page text)

- **SPECULATIVE:** the Niagara (Ont.) agricultural fair prize lists in the "NIAGARA, Ont." column (21 Oct 1881 p. 2; 5 Oct 1883 p. 1; 10 Oct 1884 p. 4) name **"J. Hutchinson" / "J. - No item in the column mentions Miller, Martin or Sturgeon Bay in connection with Hutchinson.

## Lead 2 — Niagara-on-the-Lake registers and the Canada West censuses

### St Mark's Anglican, Niagara (Rev. Robert Addison), 1792–1832 — read in full text
Source: "Baptisms in Niagara, by Rev. Robert Addison", "Weddings at Niagara" and "Burials, Niagara", *Papers and Records*, Ontario Historical Society, vol. 3 (Toronto, 1901), archive.org `papersrecordsontv3onta` (https://archive.org/details/papersrecordsontv3onta). This is a printed transcript of the register, so check it against the original register (or the FS film) before citing it as final.
- **1831, Dec. 21: "Mary Louisa Hutchinson, of Thomas and Sophia."** (p. 52) **CONFIRMED (transcript).**
- **1801, April 19: "Sophia Miller, of William and Margery" / "Louisa Miller, of William and Margery"** (bracketed together; p. 17). **CONFIRMED (transcript).**
- 1792, July 15: "Margaret Miller, of William and Margery". 1799: "James Miller, of William and Margery". 1801, Sept. 25 (burials): "Looisa Miller, infant".
- **No baptism of Eliza (c.1829) or Rhoda (c.1845)** appears under any Hutch\* spelling. The printed register ends in 1832, and Rhoda's baptism would fall later. **No Thomas Hutch\* × Sophia Miller wedding** appears in the Weddings for 1825–32. The only Hutchinson wedding is James Murphet Hutchinson × Ellen Quinn in 1807. So the marriage was probably at another church (St Andrew's, Methodist) or by a magistrate, or elsewhere. Eliza may have been baptised elsewhere or not at all.
- **St Andrew's Presbyterian, Niagara**: the same volume prints births/baptisms and marriages from c.1830–32 only. No Hutch\* entries appear, though the OCR of the baptism table is poor. **NEGATIVE (for 1830–32).**
