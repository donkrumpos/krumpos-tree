## Lead 1 — Thomas & Sophia Hutch*, after 1850; Rhoda

**Thomas/Sophia after 1850: NEGATIVE (indexes).**
- 1855 NY state census (FS coll. 1937366), surname Hutch*, Niagara Co.: only one Royalton family (wife b. 1814, children Bruce, Caroline and a son; K67Y-9XH). It is **not ours**. No Hutch* entry is indexed at Lockport.
- 1860 US census (coll. 1473181) and 1870 (coll. 1438024): no Sophia Hutch* born 1797–1807 in Canada, and no Thomas Hutch* born c.1812 in England in NY, WI or MI who fits. The only Canada-born Sophia Hutch* indexed anywhere is the 1850 Lockport entry (MCBJ-5WR).
- Door County, any Hutch* record before 1900: no Thomas, Sophia or Rhoda. The 1870 Sturgeon Bay Hutchinsons (Earl, Olive, Elbridge) were born in New York, so they are a different family.
- Records search with father Hutch* and mother Sophia: one hit, Hannah M. Bingley (d. Toronto 1923, b. 1838; JNG3-HLG). Her mother is **Sophia Smith**, so she is not ours.
- Reading: Thomas and Sophia probably died between 1850 and 1855, or they are indexed under a badly garbled name. No death or burial was found.

**NEW, PROBABLE: Mary A. (Hutchinson) Lavassor was Eliza's sister.**
- FindAGrave #129004940 (indexed on FS as QVP9-LLZ9): Mary A. Hutchinson Lavassor, b. May 1833, Canada; d. 19 Jan 1884, Sturgeon Bay; buried **Bayside Cemetery**, Sec. 6, Ward B, the same cemetery as Eliza. Husband Joseph Lavassor Sr (1822–1883).
- **Door County Advocate, 24 Jan 1884, p.3, "A Deserted Fireside"** (doc `ea91eb62-96e3-4ad5-b0c2-0fc095b362be/wsbd0000/20120910/00001129`). This is Rev. G. W. Prescott's funeral address. It says **"The maiden name of Mrs. Lavassor was Mary Hutchinson. She was born in Niagara West, in Canada, about the year 1831"**, that she married Joseph Lavassor in 1854 and came with him to Sturgeon Bay in May of that year, and that the funeral was held at the M.E. church. Her son Charles is mentioned. **No parents or siblings are named.**
- Why PROBABLE and not CONFIRMED: no record names her parents. The fit is strong, though. She has the right surname and was born in Canada c.1831–33, between Eliza (c.1829) and Rhoda (c.1845). She was in Sturgeon Bay from 1854, and the Walkers came to Door Co. soon after (John Walker died there c.1859). Both women are buried at Bayside, and both funerals were at the M.E. church. She was not in Thomas's household in 1850, so she may have been in service or living nearby.
- **Significance:** "Niagara West" (probably Niagara Township, Lincoln Co., Upper Canada) is the first **sourced** Niagara birthplace for this family. If Mary is Eliza's sister, it gives some support to the unsourced "Niagara-on-the-Lake" on Eliza's FS profile. It also narrows Thomas and Sophia's marriage and the older children's baptisms to the **Niagara/St. Mark's/St. Andrew's** registers that were already flagged.
- Next: Mary's 1854 marriage to Joseph Lavassor (probably Michigan or Wisconsin; place not known) may name her parents. Also check the 1860 and 1870 Lavassor households in Sturgeon Bay, where Sophia might be living as a widow (the index searches on the spouse name were inconclusive).
- Side note, SPECULATIVE and probably unrelated: Ruth Whitter Hutchinson Kilgore (1830–1886, Baileys Harbor; FS QVKR-95HM), wife of Moses Kilgore. "Whitter" suggests a New England family. Not pursued.

**Rhoda: NEGATIVE.** Only the 1850 Lockport entry (MCBJ-5WT). The Madoc/Hastings Co. Ontario Rhoda Hutchison is still unlinked, and no Door Co. or NY Rhoda was found.

## Lead 2 — Sarah Ann (Martin) Kehoe obituary (March 1915)

Archive search for "Kehoe" between 1915-03-05 and 1915-04-10 found 3 hits. All three were read from the image.

1. **Door County News, issue dated 10 Mar 1915, p.1, "Deaths"** (doc `88b0dabc-478c-4621-a4a1-0ae7125b6234/wsbd0000/20120718/00001372`). This is the full text: "We received information just as we go to press that Mrs. John Kehoe of North Bay passed away at her home at that place on Monday. Mrs. Kehoe was a lady of 60 years and leaves a husband to mourn her sudden death." Nothing more. (Caution: the same page carries a St. Patrick feature and a death whose age arithmetic points to mid-March, so the archive's issue date may be a week early. That would move "Monday" from 8 to 15 Mar. The masthead date is not legible at the zoom used.)
2. **Sturgeon Bay Advocate, 18 Mar 1915, p.10, Baileys Harbor column** (doc `b906834c-d092-4fa8-a27e-15e1b2759076/wsbd0000/20130111/00001004`): "Mrs. John Kehoe, one of our former residents and one of the oldest settlers, passed away at her home at North Bay Tuesday morning. **She was born in Ireland, is about 86 years old** and has been a resident of this town about 25 years." **CONFIRMED (text).**
3. **Sturgeon Bay Advocate, 25 Mar 1915, p.5, Card of Thanks** (doc `…/20130111/00001005`): "I take this method of thanking the people of North Bay … during the illness and after the death of my beloved wife; also … Rev. Erick Anderson for his services. JOHN KEHOE. Dated at North Bay, March 22, 1915." Names no parents.

**Assessment**
- Birthplace: **Ireland, PROBABLE** (one newspaper, from local informants). It agrees with Henry. The 1900 census "England, parents England" is now the outlier, likely an enumerator error or a family that had lived in England. Calling her a half-sister is no longer needed to explain a different country.
- Age: the sources disagree widely. "About 86" gives c.1829, 1900 census gives Oct 1840, and the DCN's "60" gives c.1855. At 86 she would be close to Henry's age (b. 1830). The "24 years younger" puzzle in the report rested only on the DCN's "60", which is now contradicted. Treat her birth year as **unknown, c.1829–1840**.
- No parents are named anywhere. Her **1915 Wisconsin death certificate** is still the record that should name them and test "Nancy Storm".

## Lead 3 — Henry Martin's naturalization  [CONFIRMED, images viewed]

FS collection **2046887, "Wisconsin, County Naturalization Records, 1807-1992"**.

1. **Declaration of intention, Brown County, 27 Oct 1860**: FS index 6NHF-LZHL; image **3:1:3QS7-8936-DVDG** (Brown › "Declarations of intention 1860 box 4, folder 4, A-Z", image 207 of 357). Henry Martin "was born in **Ireland**, on or about the year eighteen hundred and **thirty one**; that he emigrated to the United States, and landed at the **Port of New York** on or about the month of **July**, in the year eighteen hundred and **fifty one**." He renounces Victoria, Queen of Great Britain & Ireland. Clerk John B. A. Massé, by deputy Chas. F. J. Massé.
2. **Petition and admission, Door County Circuit Court, Sturgeon Bay, 16 Feb 1863**: FS index ZNG4-JH3Z; image **3:1:3QSQ-G938-14VL** (Door › "Petitions for naturalization 1861-1879 vol 1", image 22 of 156). Born Ireland; arrived Port of New York, July 185_ (the last digit looks like "7" but probably copies the declaration's 1851); declaration filed Brown Co. 27 Oct 1860. Witnesses **Joseph Harris** and **Wm. K. Dresser**; clerk **M. E. Lyman**. Signed "Henry Martin".
- **Result:** the obituary's 1851 New York arrival is confirmed. The month (**July 1851**) and his own stated birth year (**1831**) are new. **No county, town or ship is given.** "Near Belfast" still rests only on the 1896 obituary.
- M. E. Lyman was clerk of the Door Co. circuit court. This may explain why the 1896 obituary names "Judge Lyman" as the officiant at the 1862 marriage while the contemporaneous notice names Robert Graham (SPECULATIVE).
- Passenger list, SPECULATIVE and unverified: FS 275M-SWD shows Henry Martin, 17, Ireland, ship *A H Schultz*, New York 1851 (M237 roll 108). The age is 3 years off and the month is not checked. Two Famine Irish Passenger Index entries are dated May 1851, which is the wrong month. To settle it, check NARA M237 for July 1851 arrivals.
- Bonus, CONFIRMED (index): **1860 US census, Sevastopol, p.23** (FS MW9Q-1HZ). Henry Martin, 30, born Ireland, is living in the household of **Nicholas Ambrose** (30, Germany) and **Catherine Ambrose** (30, **Ireland**), with children Lawrence 7 and Mary E 5, both born NY. This puts Henry in Sevastopol in 1860, not in the town of Washington, which settles the Genealogy Trails question in henry-martin.md. Catherine's maiden name is unknown. That she could be a sister is SPECULATIVE but worth one check.
