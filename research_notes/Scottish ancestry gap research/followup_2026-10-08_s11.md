## Headline

1. **Thomas Hutchinson's death found: Lockport, NY, 24 Aug 1854.**
   - Grace Church (Episcopal) burial register, image read: died of consumption on 24 Aug at 5 P.M.; buried 26 Aug in Cold Spring Cemetery.
   - *Niagara Mail*, 30 Aug 1854: "Mr. Thomas Hutchison, formerly of this Town, aged 48 years."
   - The identification is **PROBABLE-strong**. It explains why nobody could find him after 1850.
2. **Mary Hutchinson × Joseph Lavassor, 19 Apr 1854, Grace Church, Lockport** (image read). This is the marriage s3 searched for in Michigan and Brown Co.
   - It was held at "the dwelling of [blank] Hutchinson, on Cottage St., S. of High St.", with **"the Bride's father"** present.
   - With the 1831 baptism of "Mary Louisa, of Thomas and Sophia", this makes Mary Lavassor **Thomas's daughter and Eliza's sister, near-certain** (still no single record says "sister").
3. **Rhoda Hutchinson died at Lockport on 10 Dec 1857**, of typhus, "in her 15th year" (image read).
4. **Door Co. probate (images read).**
   - **John Walker** died at Nasewaupee ("late Town of Otumba") on **14 Apr 1859**, leaving his wife "Eliza Walker, now Eliza Peters" and five children: **Elias, Mary Jane, Charles, John and Eliza A[nn]**.

5. **Thomas × Sophia marriage: still NEGATIVE.** Three Douglas A. Robbins Niagara compilations in the FS Digital Library are OCR-searchable even though they are "protected"; none has the marriage. The OGS *St Mark's baptisms 1792–1856* is not in the Digital Library.

8. **Scottish: still NEGATIVE.** A Scottish (Berwickshire) Hutchison family is buried at St Andrew's Presbyterian, Niagara, but nothing links it to Thomas, who was Anglican and English-born in every census.

### (b) Thomas Hutchinson's death, Lockport 1854
- ***Niagara Mail*, Wednesday, 30 Aug 1854** (Burtch, image 60; image read at zoom): "c) At Lockport, N.Y. on Thursday the 24th inst., after a short illness, Mr. Thomas Hutchison, formerly of this Town, aged 48 years." The year comes from the page header ("Wednesday, August 30, 1854") and from the Hon. R. S. Jameson (d. 1 Aug 1854) notice on the same page.
- **Grace Church, Lockport, burial register, p. 226** (FS film 008273185, image 86; ark 3:1:3Q9M-CS5F-4D1H). Found by FS Full-Text with the facet `f.recordPlace3=10,New York,Niagara,Lockport`. Image read:
  > 1854 August 26 | Thomas Hutchinson | [age blank] | Consumption | [time of decease] August 24th at 5 P.M. | Cold Spring Cemetery | Chas H. Platt | Services at the house, and at the grave.
  - The "38" in the OCR belongs to the next entry (Myron Holley Davis).
- **Assessment: PROBABLE-strong.** Same name, same date, Lockport, "formerly of [Niagara]". The 1848 St Mark's roll put Thomas in the Town of Niagara, and the 1850 census has Thomas Hutcherson at Lockport.
  - **Age conflict:** 48 in 1854 (b. c.1806) against 38 in 1850 (b. c.1812). Recorded as c.1806–1812.
- Find a Grave, Thomas Hutch\*, d. 1854: NEGATIVE. NYS Historic Newspapers, Hutchison, Aug–Oct 1854: only NYC papers. NEGATIVE.

## 2. Door Co. probate (priority 2): FS film 100728701 (images read)
- **John Walker** (image 107; 3QSQ-G9G7-P34V):
  - Petition of "Eliza Peters of Nasewaupee", dated Sturgeon Bay, 6 Jan 1862, signed "her mark"; witness E. F. Battershill [?]; sworn 24 Jan 1862 before M. E. Lyman, County Judge.
  - Walker "departed this life at Nasewaupee, late Town of Otumba, on the fourteenth day of April 1859", intestate.
  - Real estate worth about **$600**.
  - "Left him surviving: Eliza Walker his wife, now Eliza Peters, also five children, viz. **Elias Walker, Mary Jane, Charles, John, Eliza A[nn]**." **CONFIRMED.**
- **Martin Peters**
  - Eliza's petition (image 65; -PKM), dated Sturgeon Bay, 2 Dec 1861, "her mark": he "died intestate October 24th [?] 1861, late of the Town of Nasewaupee". He left real estate in the town of Sturgeon Bay and personal property in Nasewaupee.
  - Henry Martin's petition (image 36; -PV9), Sturgeon Bay, 2 Mar 1863. Henry Martin of Sevastopol is "husband of [the] wife of the deceased". Peters "departed this life at **Little Sturgeon** on the **24 day of Oct 1861**". Personal property up to $250; real estate about **$1,000**. "Left him surviving: **wife and four children**." **CONFIRMED.**
  - The four children are not named. Possibly William (b. c.1861) plus Walker stepchildren, or Peters children by an earlier wife. Unresolved.
