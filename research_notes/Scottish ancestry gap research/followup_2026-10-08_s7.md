# Follow-up searches, session 7 — 2026-10-08

Continues `followup_2026-10-08_s6.md`. The earlier sessions worked through the four lines once thought to be Scottish (Laing/Miller, Meikle, Hutcheson, Martin). This session opens the one **colonial-American** branch that had never been researched: the ancestry of Mildred Amelia (Guernsey) Martin, made up of the Guernsey, "Hinson", Randall and Shaffstall/Cady families. All of its reliquary files were stubs. All work was read-only on FamilySearch (account Don8629): nothing was edited, attached, merged or commented there.
Labels: **CONFIRMED** = read from the original image; **PROBABLE** = strong but indirect; **SPECULATIVE** = a lead only; **NEGATIVE** = searched, not found.

## Headline

1. **Mildred's mother was Almeda LINSON, not "Hinson".** She was the daughter of **Asa W. Linson** (b. Feb 1814 NY, d. 20 Dec 1889 Fairplain Twp, Montcalm Co., MI; FS L7JK-338) and **Hannah Walker** (b. c.1816 NY, d. 9 Jan 1907 Ionia Twp, MI; FS L7JK-3SC). **CONFIRMED** (1881 death register image, below). "Hinson" (FS L2SJ-TYB) and the "Amelia Hinson" in Mildred's NUMIDENT entry are misreadings of an L.
2. **Almeda died 4 Feb 1881, not 1883**, at her parents' home in Fair Plain, Montcalm Co., aged 22, of "heart disease". **Three days earlier, on 1 Feb 1881, her unnamed infant daughter died aged 3 days.** The register names the infant's parents as "John Grumsey, Ionia" and "Almira Grumsey, dead". **CONFIRMED (image).** Mildred was 10 months old.
3. **Scottish result for this branch: NEGATIVE.** George W. Guernsey's FamilySearch pedigree (224 persons, 8 generations) is entirely New England colonial stock (Rehoboth, Connecticut, Massachusetts, Washington Co. NY). Its only Irish entries are the "Woodward of Dublin" gentry claims, which are typical legacy-tree myth, and it contains no Scottish birthplace. The FS tree has no ancestors for Esther Randall, Asa Linson or Hannah Walker, and only names (William Cady and Mary Ann) for Catherine Shaffstall. **Nothing Scottish is documented, but the Linson, Walker, Randall and Cady lines are open**, so Scottish ancestry is not ruled out here.

## Lead A — Almeda's identity

### (a) 1880 US census, Ionia city, Ionia Co., MI (ED 94, sheet 122D; FS MWSD-ZQH)
John L. Guernsy, 22, farm labourer, b. MI; **Almeda**, 20, wife, b. MI, **father b. Indiana, mother b. Indiana**; Mildred A., daughter, b. MI. **CONFIRMED (index).** The parents' birthplaces are wrong for Asa and Hannah, who were both born in New York. The informant may have been thinking of Almeda's elder siblings, Betsy and Catharine, who were born in Indiana. This is the one discordant record.

### (b) 1870 US census, Bushnell Twp, Montcalm Co., MI (index)
Asa W Linson 1814 NY; Hannah 1818 NY; Betsy 1850 Ind.; Catharine 1854 Ind.; Dorcas 1856 MI; Myron 1858 MI; **Almeda 1861 MI**; Almy 1865 MI; Alford 1865 MI. **CONFIRMED (index).**

### (c) 1880 US census, Ionia, Ionia Co., MI (index)
Asa W. Linson 1816 NY, Hannah 1818 NY, Alma 1865, Alfred 1865. They were living in the same town as John and Almeda Guernsey.

### (d) Michigan death register, Montcalm Co., 1881 (FS "Michigan, Deaths, 1867-1897", film 2363670, p. 338 rn 340; images 94–95 of the Ionia/Wexford/Montcalm volume; record N3JH-XFZ)
- **No. 340:** 4 Feb 1881, "Almon Grumsey" [*sic*; = Almeda Guernsey], marked M, W, married, aged 22, at Fairplain. Facing page: cause **heart disease**; born Michigan; occupation unknown; parents **"A. W. Linson / Hannah Linson", Fairplain**; recorded 21 Jun 1882.
- **No. 341:** 1 Feb 1881, "Not named", F, W, aged 3 [days], at Fairplain. Facing page: cause unknown; born Michigan; parents **"John Grumsey, Ionia" / "Almira Grumsey, dead"**.
- The clerk's "Almon … M" is an error. A *married* child of the Linsons with the Guernsey surname must be a daughter, and the next entry is her own baby, with "Almira Grumsey" marked dead. **CONFIRMED (image).** FS has wrongly made "Almon Grumsey" (GYKC-JT2) a separate male child of Asa and Hannah. That profile is Almeda, and it duplicates L2SJ-TYB.

### (e) Corroboration
- Eliphlet Martin × Mildred Guernsey, 1901 Wisconsin marriage index: mother of the bride "**Almeida Linson**" (already attached to L2SJ-TYB).
- Mildred A. Martin, NUMIDENT: b. 21 Mar 1880, Ionia Co.; parents "John H Guevnsey" and "Amelia Hinson". These are transcription errors, but they confirm the 21 Mar 1880 birth.
- **A second Guernsey–Linson marriage:** Dennis Guernsey married **Esther (Ann) Linson** (1852–1919), Almeda's elder sister. Their children married in Ionia Twp in the 1890s–1900s. So two Guernsey men married two Linson sisters.
- **A Randall link:** Almeda's sister **Catherine (Linson) Randall** (1851–1900, d. Ronald Twp, Ionia; death record names Asa W. Linson & Hannah Walker). John Loami's grandmother was **Esther Randall**. A kinship between the Randalls is SPECULATIVE.

## Lead B — Asa W. Linson and Hannah Walker
- Asa: b. Feb 1814 NY; m. 1842 NY; children b. Indiana 1843–54 (Armina 1843, Anna Elvira 1844, Betsy 1850, Catherine 1851/54), then Michigan from 1856; d. 20 Dec 1889 Fairplain. FS L7JK-338 has 16 sources and **no parents**. The "1860 New Britain, Hartford, CT" residence on his profile belongs to a different man, Asa *Linerson* (b. 1811 CT; 1850 Southington, CT).
- Hannah: obituary, *Ionia Standard*, 11 Jan 1907: "Mrs. Hannah Linson, aged 90, of Ionia township, died Wednesday … interment in Tuttle cemetery" (FS collaborate note). FS L7JK-3SC has **no parents**. Her 1907 Michigan death certificate (which should name her parents and their birthplaces) is **not indexed** under Linson/Linsen/Linsom/Lenson/Linsin/Lynson or under father "Walker". NEGATIVE (index).
- Children's later death records found (Catherine Randall 1900; Anna Baker 1888) give the parents' names only, without birthplaces, in the index.
- **1850 / 1860 censuses of the Asa Linson household: NOT FOUND** under Linson/Linsen/Lenson, or by searching for the children. NEGATIVE (index). Indiana 1850 should exist.
- **Possible sister (SPECULATIVE):** Susan A. Linson (1 Mar 1817 NY – 6/10 Nov 1900 Bangor, Van Buren Co., MI; FS LDC3-3WQ). She married Welcome L. Horton in 1837 at **Belleville, Jefferson Co., NY**, lived at Lysander, Onondaga Co. in 1850, and named a son **Welcome Asa Horton** (1845). Her 1900 death entry (Michigan Deaths and Burials, v C p 150) names no parents. If she was Asa's sister, the Linson family came from **Jefferson Co., NY**, an area settled from New England after 1800.
- On the surname, briefly: Linson is rare. It occurs in Ohio, Indiana and New York, and sometimes as a clipped form of Tomlinson. Nothing points to Scotland. SPECULATIVE only.

## Lead C — the rest of Mildred's ancestry (FS pedigree sweep)
- **George Washington Guernsey** (b. 15 Dec 1810, Washington Co., NY; FS K23V-Q48). The FS pedigree has 224 persons: Silas Guernsey → Lewis Franklin Garnsey (b. 1777, Chatham, CT) × Sarah Ann Flagg (MA) → John Lewis Garnsey (b. 1742, Rehoboth) × Judith Ormsby, and so on. The tree is internally unreliable: it gives a father "Silas Sr." born 1805 for a son born 1810. It has **no Scottish birthplace**. The Irish entries are Ralph Woodward ("b. 1600 Dublin", d. Hingham MA 1662), Rachel Woodward ("b. 1628 Dublin Castle") and "Lady Mary Malby" (b. 1553, Athlone). These are legacy-tree embellishments; they are **not evidence**.
- **Esther Randall** (1815–1845; LZ28-CL4): no parents in FS.
- **Catherine Shaffstall** (1840–1902; 9Z3D-X3X): parents in FS are "William Cady" and "Mary Ann" (temple-ordinance entries only). Shaffstall is presumably a married name. **SPECULATIVE:** she may have been born a Cady.
- The Hugunin/Fuller family is **not** a direct line (see `Hugunin/caroline-amelia-hugunin.md`). That leaves Guernsey–Linson–Walker–Randall–Cady as the only colonial-American part of Don's ancestry.
