- The 1860 Newfane Robert Hutchinson, 13, b. West Indies (David Hutchinson's son), is a different person.
- 1850 index: neither Robert nor Margaret is found under Hutch\* (they may still have been in Canada, or be garbled).

### Assessment: PROBABLE (circumstantial)
- Robert, b. c.1838 in Canada, and Margaret, b. c.1836 in Canada (reading her age as 24), fill the gap between Mary (1831) and Rhoda (1843).
- Both witnessed Mary's 1854 wedding at "the dwelling of [blank] Hutchinson", where "the Bride's father" was present.
- Margaret witnessed a wedding beside John Walker (Eliza's husband) in 1853.
- Both Margaret and Robert are tied to the Ransom household, as were the 1853 bride Jane Harris and the 1855 witness Emily J. West. That is the pattern of a poor labourer's older children placed out in service before 1850, which also explains why they are missing from Thomas's 1850 household.
- **No record names their parents.** They are kept out of the frontmatter `children` lists.

### Fify name forms

- Not found: Fify, Fifey, Fiffy, Fifie, Fyfe, Fife, Fyffe, Phifey or Feiffer at Lockport in 1860 or 1870 (the 1870 "Margaret Feeney", b. Ireland, is not her).
- Find a Grave has a "Corve Fifye" (d. 1871) and a James Fifey (1793–1849), both unplaced.

## 4. Thomas × Sophia marriage and the Niagara Hutchisons (priority 3)
- **Record index (hr), any collection:** Thomas Hutchinson, Hutchison or Hutcheson × spouse Sophia (and × Sophia Miller), marriage 1820–35. Hits: Battle, Sussex (an English census family with Frederick and Charles); Sophia Reynolds (1846); Sophia Akens (Hutchens, 1822); Sophia McClintick (1846); and later marriages. **NEGATIVE.**
- **Children:** any Hutch\* child with father Thomas and mother Sophia, b. 1824–46: only English and Hanbury families. **NEGATIVE.**
- **FS Full-Text, Lincoln and Welland Cos.:** none of "Sophia Hutchinson", "Sophia Hutchison" or "Margery Miller" appears before 1880.
  - The "Sophia Miller" of 1813–14 is a married daughter named in an Oxford Co. father's will (with Edmund, John, Catherine, Hannah Young, Rebecca Powell and Mary Willson); not ours.
  - "Thomas Hutchinson", 1848 and 1861 deeds, St Catharines: a minor heir under Armstrong Hutchinson's 1846 will, son of Edward and Jane Hutchinson, by 1861 a saddler at Lyons, Clinton Co., Iowa; not ours.
- **Lincoln Co. Hutchison records 1815–50** (41 full-text hits):
  - **Will of George Hutchison of Grantham, yeoman**, dated 2 Sep 1841 and proved in the Niagara District Surrogate Court on 24 Sep 1841 (affidavit of witness John Heaney); executors Francis Goring and James B. Ives, merchant, of Grantham, with his wife Hannah. FS film **008130596, images 875–878; read.**
    - Bequests: to his "loving wife **Hannah**", then to his children; sons **George and David** to pay £12 10s each to his "loving children **Jane, Ann and Hannah**".
    - The household goods go to his wife and then to "my children George, David, Jane, Ann and Hannah".
    - **No Thomas.**
  - This is the St Andrew's Presbyterian family (Robbins: "Hannah BARRIE wife of Geo. 1777–1841"). Robbins's "Geo. 1773–1831, a native of Berwicks." must be misaligned OCR, or a different George: this George died in Sept 1841.
  - An 1834 deed, Town of Niagara: George J. Hutchison, blacksmith, and Matthew Hutchison (same group). An 1833–37 grantee index names Jacob, Richard, Matthew and William Hutchinson. No Thomas.
  - **Conclusion: the Scottish Hutchisons of Niagara and Grantham are EXCLUDED as Thomas's family.** Thomas was an Anglican born in England, as every census says.
- **Still offline:** the OGS *St Mark's baptisms 1792–1856* (FS 734190), and Upper Canada district marriage registers not yet indexed.
