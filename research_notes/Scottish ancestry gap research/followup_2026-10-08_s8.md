# Follow-up searches, session 8 — 2026-10-08

Continues `followup_2026-10-08_s7.md`, on the Guernsey side: the Linson, Walker, Randall and Shaffstall lines behind Mildred (Guernsey) Martin. All work was read-only, on FamilySearch (account Don8629) and on the Archives of Michigan digital collections (Michiganology/Preservica). Nothing was edited on FS.
Labels: **CONFIRMED** = read from the original image; **PROBABLE** = strong but indirect; **SPECULATIVE** = a lead only; **NEGATIVE** = searched, not found.

## Headline

1. **Hannah (Walker) Linson's parents are John Walker and Catherine Finkle, all three born in New York State.** CONFIRMED from her 1907 death certificate (image).
2. **Asa W. Linson's father was born in England** and his mother in New York (1880 census). The 1889 death register gives his parents as "Not known" (image). Two of his children's death certificates give Asa himself as born in England, which probably garbles the same fact.
3. **PROBABLE:** Hannah's mother Catherine Finkle is **Catherine (Finkle) Flagg Alexander Guernsey** (c.1797–1892), who married George Washington Guernsey in 1847. If so, the Linsons were step-kin of the Guernseys. That would explain why they lived side by side in 1850 and why two Guernseys married two Linson girls. It would also make Mildred's mother and father related by marriage, though not by blood.
4. **Catherine Shaffstall was born a Shaffstall** (Pennsylvania German, Crawford Co., Ohio). After Justus Guernsey died in 1860 she married a Gordon and then Charles Boyce Jr., and lived at **Sevastopol, Door County, WI**, from 1866 to 1902. **This is why Mildred ended up in Sturgeon Bay.** John Loami Guernsey grew up in Door County.
5. **The Guernsey, Flagg, Randall and (probably) Walker/Linson families all came from Granby, Oswego Co., NY**, and moved to Fairfield Twp, DeKalb Co., Indiana, c.1844–47.
6. **Scottish result: still NEGATIVE.** The new ancestors are English-born (Asa's father), New York Palatine-German (Finkle), Pennsylvania German (Shaffstall, Peterman), and New York/New England colonial (Walker, Randall, Guernsey). No Scottish birthplace or claim appears anywhere.

## 1. Hannah (Walker) Linson's death certificate

**Where it was:** not on FamilySearch. The Archives of Michigan's *Death Certificates, 1897–1952* are **name-searchable** at michigan.access.preservica.com (reached through Michiganology → "Search the Archives of Michigan digital collections"). A simple search for "Linson" returned 35 items, with an index of parents' names and the image for each.

**Michigan death certificate, Ionia Co., Ronald Twp, 1907** (item 280526). **CONFIRMED (image):**
- Hannah Linson, female, white, born 1816, age 91, widow
- "Parent of 5 children, of whom 3 are living". This is wrong: at least 10 children are known.
- Birthplace: York State
- Father: **John Walker**, born York State
- Mother: **Catherine Finkle**, born York State
- Occupation: housewife
- Died 9 Jan 1907, of paralysis (66 days); physician Dr. W. E. Ogden(?), Ionia

The place of death is **Ronald Twp**, not Ionia Twp as the obituary has it. The reliquary file now uses Ronald.

1880 census (index, MWSD-ZW8): Hannah's father and mother were both born in New York, which agrees with the certificate.

Against: the parents' birthplaces conflict.

## 3. Catherine Finkle: probably Hannah's mother, and G. W. Guernsey's second wife

FS L6CV-QH3, "Catherine Finkle" (1804–1892):
- **Parents John Finkle and Dorcas.** Michigan Deaths 1867–1897, Ionia Co., p. 418, rn 1861 (index, N3VG-GH4): "Katherine Guernsey", 95, widowed, housekeeper, b. New York, d. **13 May 1892, Orange, Ionia Co.**; father John Finkle, mother Dorcas Finkle.
- *Ionia Standard*, 20 May 1892 (FS life sketch): "Mrs. Catharine Guernsey died Friday at the home of her daughter, Mrs. A. T. Adgate, Orange. She was probably the oldest woman in the county, her age being 95."
- Buried in Tuttle Cemetery, Ionia Twp (FS).
- Marriages (FS):
  - Welden Flagg, by whom Asenath M. Flagg (1835–1928, b. Oswego Co., NY; m. Cyrus W. E. Clark) and Harriet Flagg (1837–1914, b. Oswego Co.; m. **Alfred Thomas Adgate**, i.e. "Mrs. A. T. Adgate")
  - a Mr. Alexander
  - **George W. Guernsey**, marriage licence 1847, DeKalb Co., IN (Indiana Marriages, XXRZ-GMC: "Catherine Alexander", St. Johns, Butler Twp, p. 132)
- Censuses: 1850 Fairfield Twp, DeKalb Co. (Catharine Gurnsey, 50, NY, with the two Flagg girls); 1860 Ionia (56); 1870 Ionia Twp.

**Why she is PROBABLY Hannah's mother:**
- Same name (Catherine Finkle) and same state of birth, and her age (b. c.1797–1800) allows a daughter in 1816.
- The 1850 Linson household lived among the Guernseys in Fairfield Twp, and both families came from the Oswego/Onondaga border.
- Hannah's daughter Esther Ann married G. W.'s son Dennis, and her daughter Almeda married G. W.'s grandson John Loami.
- Hannah named a daughter **Dorcas** (b. 1857), the name of Catherine's mother.
- Hannah and Catherine are **both buried in Tuttle Cemetery**. Catherine died in Orange Twp, where Hannah's daughter Dorcas Wiggins also died.

**Against, or unproven:** no record names Hannah as Catherine's daughter. FS has no Walker marriage for Catherine. Her censused ages (50 in 1850, 56 in 1860) would make her too young; only the 1892 age of 95 fits.

**Next:** the 1892 death register image and the full obituary, for "Mrs. Linson"; and a John Walker × Catherine Finkle marriage c.1815. There is a "Jerimiah Walker" at Granby in 1830.

**Side result:** Esther Randall's FS life sketch claims her parents were "Peter Randall and Catherine Alexander". The "Catherine Alexander" is G. W.'s *second* wife, so that statement is a conflation and should not be used.

## 4. The Guernsey household, 1850 and 1860

- **1850, Fairfield Twp, DeKalb Co., IN** (index): George Gurnsey 41 NY; Catharine 50 NY; Justus 15 NY; Dennis 13 NY; Aaron 9 NY; Alvin 6 NY; Asenath Flagg 15 NY; Harriet Flagg 13 NY. So **Dennis Guernsey, husband of Esther Ann Linson, was Justus's brother, i.e. John Loami's uncle.** That resolves an open question from session 7.
- **1860, Ionia, Ionia Co., MI** (index): Geo W Guerney; Catherine 56; Aaron, Marvin, Alvin, NY; Polly, MI. Dennis, 21, was at Lyons, Ionia Co., with the **Tuttle** family (Almond and Rhoda J. Tuttle). Tuttle Cemetery is presumably theirs.
- FS: G. W.'s children were born at **Granby, Oswego Co., NY**, 1835–1848. 1840 census, Granby: G W Gurnsey, Silas Gurnsey, Asa/Daniel/David Randall, Phillip Alexander. 1830, Granby: Silas Randell, Jerimiah Walker (index). His obituary (quoted on FS) says: "In 1844 he moved to Indiana, where his wife died, and he married again".
- **Justus George Guernsey** was alive at the June 1860 census (Fairfield Twp, DeKalb Co.: Justice G. Grensay 25 NY; Catherine 20 OH; **John L. 3, b. Michigan**; George, infant, b. Indiana). Find a Grave: d. 1860, buried Hudson, Steuben Co., IN. The reliquary previously had 1859.
- Indiana marriages: **Justus G. Guernsey × Catherine Shaffstall, licence 6 Mar 1857, Steuben Co.**
- John Loami's birthplace: FS says Hudson, Steuben Co., IN; the 1860 and 1870 censuses say Michigan. Unresolved.

## 5. Catherine Shaffstall

All from FS indexes (persona records read in-page):
- FS profile 9Z3D-X3X: b. 10 Jan 1840, Liberty Twp, Crawford Co., OH; d. 28 Sep 1902, Green Bay, WI; buried Bayside Cemetery, Sturgeon Bay.
- **1850, Liberty Twp, Crawford Co., OH:** Catharine Shoffstall, 10, b. Ohio, in the household of **John Peterman, 76, b. PA** and **Catharine Peterman, 55, b. PA**. PROBABLE grandparents. A Shoffstall family (Peter, b. PA) lived at Bucyrus in the same county.
- 1857: marriage to Justus (above). 1860: DeKalb Co. (above).
- **Wisconsin marriages: Charles Boyce Jr.** (b. Delaware; parents Charles and Phebe Boyce) **× "Cathrine Gorden"** (parents **William Cady and Mary Ann Cady**), 30 Jun 1866, Sturgeon Bay. This is the source of FS's "William Cady" father. Since she was a Shoffstall at 10 and a Shaffstall at 17, **Cady is probably a stepfather** (PROBABLE). "Gorden" suggests a short second marriage c.1861–66 (SPECULATIVE).
- **1870, Sevastopol, Door Co., WI:** Charles Boyce 30, Delaware; Catherine 30, Ohio; **John Guernsey 12, Michigan; George Guernsey 10, Indiana**.
- 1880 Sevastopol (FS residence); **1900, Sebastopol town:** Charles 60 (b. May 1840, Delaware), Catherine 60 (b. Jan 1840, Ohio), Jennie B. 21, Ruth M. 17.
- Boyce sons: Thomas William (b. Sturgeon Bay; m. 1896 Milwaukee, to Vinnie Grundman) and Albert H. (FS: "Catherine Thaffstall in entry for Albert H. Boyer", WI marriages).
- **"Albert H. Boyce of Hollywood, California"** attended Gerald Martin's funeral in 1931 (mildred-martin.md). He was almost certainly Catherine's son, and so Mildred's half-uncle. PROBABLE.
- George Dennis Guernsey's Idaho death certificate names his mother as "Catherine Shaftstalk". So John Loami's brother George went west.
- **NEGATIVE:** no Shaffstall/Shoffstall household with a Catherine in the 1850 Indiana index; no clear William × Mary Ann Cady household found in 1850 or 1860 in OH, IN, MI or WI.
