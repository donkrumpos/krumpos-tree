## 1. Robert Hutchinson (priority 2)

### Where the Lockport Catholic registers are
FS catalog, Lockport, Church records (6 entries). The two Catholic films are **browsable at home** (not indexed):
- **DGS 7900179** (film 1324470; catalog koha:30511), St. John the Baptist, 1842–1917. Images 1–~110 are a typed baptismal abstract, chronological (child, date, parents, sponsors). A 1932 note on image 5 says the baptisms of **1855–63 are in St. Patrick's registers** (St. John's burned in 1855). The abstract jumps from 1855 to Nov 1864. Images ~600–948 hold the marriage register, which is continuous: July 1854 is at image 700, Oct 1862 at 755.
- **DGS 7900308** (film 1378572; catalog koha:269556), St. Patrick's, 1853–1917. "Vol. I", images 5–~200, is the handwritten baptismal register 1855–79 (St. John's, then St. Patrick's). About 5–6 images a year: Feb 1863 is at image 88, Feb 1866 at 115, May 1868 at 118, 1869 at 122–127. Images ~560–600 hold the burial register (Aug 1861 at 565; 1864–65 at 579–583).

### The marriage, 6 July 1861 — CONFIRMED (image read)
- DGS 7900179, **image 748**, left page, 1861: "Julii 6°", margin "Hutchison Robt. et Rutlege Cath."
- Text: *"Disp. obtenta a Cancellaria super impedimentum disp[aritatis] cultus, conjunxi in matrimonium Robertum filium Thomae Hutchison et Sophiae Miller; et Catharinam filiam Joannis Rutlege et Catharinae McMahon. Testibus Joanne Smith et Margarita Cunningham. G. Gleeson."*
- So the couple received a dispensation from the Chancery for **disparity of cult**: Robert was not a Catholic (the family used Grace Episcopal Church in 1854), and Catharine was. The priest was Fr. G. Gleeson. The witnesses were John Smith and Margaret Cunningham.
- This also fixes the date of the marriage: after the 1860 census (Robert single, in the Ransom household) and before the 1865 census.

### The burial, 24 April 1862 — CONFIRMED (image read)
- DGS 7900308, **image 568**, right page, 1862: "24 Infans sine Baptismo — Infantulus Roberti Hutch[inson] et Catharinae Rutledge, mortuus sine Baptismo. G. Gleeson."
- This is probably one of the two children that Kate had borne by 1865. The other is not found.

### Searches that came up NEGATIVE
- St. Patrick's baptisms, July 1861 to mid-1865 (images 79–100, margin names read on every page): no Hutchinson. The second child was perhaps also unbaptized, or baptized elsewhere.
- St. Patrick's baptisms 1866–70 (images 107–127, full pages read): **no Frank (1867) and no Mary (c.1869)**. Pages 215–216 (Oct–Nov 1867) are marked "Missing Pages" on the film. The St. John's typed abstract, Nov 1864–June 1870 (DGS 7900179 images 98–105), has no Hutchinson either. So Frank and Mary were not baptized in a Lockport Catholic church in 1866–70, or their entries are on the missing pages. That leaves a St. Catharines baptism possible after all, although the 1870 census says New York.

- Same census, Town of Lockport: **Marey Rutledge, 47, b. Ireland**, with Michael McMahan. Given Kate's mother Catharine McMahon, this could be kin. SPECULATIVE; noted only.

## Method notes
- **FS catalog, Lockport:** the subject list collapses. Click the "Church records (6)" text, then the expander button, to list the titles. Film viewer URLs: `/search/film/<DGS with leading zeros>` (e.g. 007900179).
- **Index volume first:** film 005469002 opens with a year-by-year name index (images 1–60). Read it before paging through the registers. It turns a ~400-image page-through into ~25 index images.
- **Margin strips:** for registers with the name in the margin, zooming the two margin columns (x≈60–300 and 590–830 at the 1203-wide frame) at 0.45 scale is enough to read every name, about 5 images per batch call.
- The image box sometimes lands one image short; step with the arrow to check.
