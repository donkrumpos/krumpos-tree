### The censuses: one man, not two
The 1918 obituary's children (Mary and Frank) are those of the 1875/1880 Robert and Catherine. The question was whether that man is the same as the 1900 Robert, b. 1840 Canada, with wife Rebecca, married 9 years. Each census was checked, and only one Robert of the right age is at Lockport at each date. The other Lockport Robert in the 1880 index, Robert Hutcheson b. c.1835 England, a boarder in Patrick Collin's house, is probably Robert B. (d. 1909).

- **1875 NY state census, Lockport Ward 4, p. 19, line 27 (image read; coll. 1918735, film 1577677):**
  - Robert Hutchinson, 28 M, **b. Canada**, **Engineer**; framed house worth $600.
  - Catherine, 27, b. Ireland. Francis (son), 8, and Mary (daughter), 6, both b. Niagara.
  - Both adult ages are about 10 years low against 1880. The index's "b. 1847" for Robert comes from this error. In s13 this household was set aside as "another man"; that is now withdrawn.
- **1880 US census, Lockport, ED 203, sheet 569B, line 67 (image read; coll. 1417683):**
  - "Hutchison, Robert", 39, works in gas house, b. **N.Y.**, **father b. Eng., mother b. Eng.**
  - Catharine, 40, b. Ireland (parents Ireland). Frank, 13, and Mary, 11, both b. NY (father's birthplace given as NY).
  - A boarder, "…ander" Feltheus [?], 40, b. Ireland, labourer.
- **1891 NYS Marriage Index (archive.org `New_York_State_Marriage_Index_1891`, image p. 389 "Hutchinson", p. 6 "Aikens", p. 126 "Carr"; read):**
  - Hutchinson, Robert — **14 May — Lockport — 8117**; Aikens, Rebecca — 14 May — Lockport — 8117.
  - Hutchinson, Mary — **12 May — Lockport — 7957**; Carr, Henry W. — 12 May — Lockport — 7957.
- **1892, 1900 and 1910:** as in s13. The 1900 entry reads b. Mar 1840 Canada, immigrated 1850, naturalized, married 9 years, father b. England, mother b. Canada. Wife Rebecca had borne 1 child, 1 living (her own, by an earlier marriage?).
- **Not found:** 1865 (index). Frank Hutchinson's and Mary Carr's deaths are not in the NYS Death Index under those names at Lockport.

### Assessment
- **Identity of the 1860–1918 Robert: CONFIRMED as one man** (consistent age c.1838–41, Canada birth in 1875, 1892, 1900 and 1910, residence, and the children/wife sequence).
- **As a son of Thomas and Sophia: still PROBABLE.**
  - For: Canada-born c.1838–40; came to Lockport c.1850 (the family's move); witness at Mary's 1854 wedding with Margaret; naturalized 1862; father b. England in both 1880 and 1900.
  - Against or uncertain: the mother's birthplace is Canada in 1900 but England in 1880 (and the 1880 census also has Robert himself b. NY, so that enumerator's information is weak). The Catholic burial is probably through his Irish wife Catharine; Thomas's family were Grace Church Episcopalians.
- **Order:** NYS cert. 8117 (1891 marriage; should name his parents and his mother's maiden name). Second: cert. 15561 (1918 death; informant probably Frank). Lockport city clerk or NYS DOH genealogy unit. → Offline batch, "Archives and libraries".

## Method notes (new)
- **The NYS Death Index CSV** (1880–1956 in 15 files, about 5 million rows; columns CertificateNumber, Given, Surname, Soundex, DeathDay, DeathMonth, DeathYear, DeathCity) is the fastest route to any upstate NY death. The age column is blank before 1941.
- **The NYS Marriage Index scans** have rough OCR and are laid out in columns. Use djvu.xml to find pages by nearby surnames (the volumes are alphabetical, about 885 pages a year), then read the page images. Each spouse appears under their own surname, so search the certificate number to find the other spouse.
- **Door archive:** the "Search by date" panel's open state persists. Check `#rc-start-date-input`.offsetParent before clicking the toggle, or the click hides the panel and the dates are dropped. The page select is the third `<select>`; zoom is the second.
