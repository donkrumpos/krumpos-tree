## Method notes
- **The FS film viewer** ignores the image box until the grid has loaded. Open any thumbnail first (double-click), close the "Image Index" panel (× at about 1148, 978), and then type the number in the box. In the single-image view the box works every time.
- **1855 NY census:** the "Image Index" table (JS read of `tr` cells) gives the row order. Rows on a page are in census line order, which tells you where to zoom.
- **The St. Patrick's film (DGS 7900308)**, in full: baptisms vol. I images 5–~200 (1855–79), vol. II ~200–~370 (1879–1917; 1887 at 230, 1903 at 300); **confirmations 374–~410 (1871–)**; **marriages ~470–~530 (1879–; 1890–91 at 492–494, 1900 at 510)**; burials ~560–772 (1861–Jan 1918).
