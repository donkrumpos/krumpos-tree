## Headline

1. **Fred Meikle's Swiss family is found: Conrad Mägli (b. c.1808) and Elisabeth Schorer of Oberbipp, Canton Bern.** The 1860 census of Humboldt, Brown Co., WI shows Conrad Sr. with five of their Oberbipp-baptised children *and* "Fredrick". Fred's own obituary names his surviving brother as **Godfried**, who was baptised at Oberbipp in 1842 as a son of Conrad and Elisabeth. **Fred = Samuel Mägli (b. 19 Mar, bapt. 9 Apr 1848, Oberbipp) is now PROBABLE-strong.** Fred's gravestone gives his birth as "Apr. 9, 1848", which is Samuel's *baptism* date.
2. **Fred's death is CONFIRMED.** He drowned at the Goodrich dock, Sturgeon Bay, on Saturday 9 Aug 1924, aged 76. Two front-page obituaries survive. He is buried at **Shiloh Moravian cemetery**, and his stone reads "FRED MAGGLE".
3. **Mary Johnson is CONFIRMED born in Sweden, 4 May 1859, to John and Mary Johnson.** She came to Door Co. "early … with her mother" (arrived 1873 per the 1920 census) and died 14 Nov 1938. Her 1938 obituary and gravestone both survive. The FS tree's 1935 Cook County death belongs to a different woman.
4. **Mary (Hutchinson) Lavassor's death was reported in the "NIAGARA, Ont." column** of the *Niagara County News* (Youngstown NY), 22 Feb 1884: "born in Niagara in the year 1831." This ties her to Niagara-on-the-Lake specifically. The Eliza-sister link stays PROBABLE but is stronger.
5. Leads 5–6 (Henry/Owen Martin; the 1854 Lavassor marriage) gave nothing decisive.

- **Mother's burial: CONFIRMED (index).** Elisabeth Mägli-Schorer, wife of Conrad, died 18 Apr 1850 and was buried 22 Apr 1850 at Oberbipp, aged 44 (b. c.1806). ### Assessment — Fred = Samuel Mägli of Oberbipp: **PROBABLE (strong)**
- The 1860 household (below) is this family: Conrad Sr., then Elizabeth, Conrad, Charles (Hans Ulrich?), Godfried and Godleib in birth order, with Fredrick youngest.
- Fred's 1924 obituary names his brother **Godfried**.
- The parents' given names match the 1874 marriage index (Conrad and Elizabeth).
- Fred's stone gives **Apr. 9, 1848**, which is Samuel's baptism date. The 1900 census and 1924 Advocate give April 1848.
- Age 6 at arrival (1924 obituary) fits October 1854 at Milwaukee (naturalization). Johannes's burial at Oberbipp in April 1854 fixes the departure as after that.
- Against: the given name differs (Samuel vs Frederick/Friedrich), and the 1860 census gives Fredrick as 8 rather than 12 (image read; it does read "8"). A second given name at baptism, or a change of name in America, would explain it. Only the church-book image could show a second name, and that needs a FamilySearch center visit.

## Lead 3 — Fred's death — CONFIRMED

- **Gravestone (photo read):** Find a Grave **#208337203** ("Fred Maegele"), Shiloh Moravian Church Cemetery, Sturgeon Bay. The stone reads "FATHER / FRED MAGGLE / Apr. 9, 1848 / Aug. 9, 1924". Photo by Teresa Young, 2020.
- ***Door County News*, Thu 14 Aug 1924, p. 1, "AGED MAN MEETS TRAGIC DEATH — Fred Magli of Sawyer Falls Into Bay at Goodrich Dock Early Saturday Morning and Drowns, Body Recovered"** (doc `88b0dabc-478c-4621-a4a1-0ae7125b6234/wsbd0000/20120718/00001858`, read from the image). He worked at the canning factory and rowed across the bay. Saturday morning he fell from the Goodrich dock while loading a crate of cherries. His son **Oscar** searched with a pike pole, and the body was found with his watch stopped at 3:10. "Mr. Magli was 76 years old, having been born in Switzerland. He came to this country when he was only 6 years old and during the past half a century has made his home in this county and for the past eight years made his home in the Fourth Ward." Survivors: wife; children **Emil, Carl, Ernest and Oscar, and Mrs. Verny Olson**, this city, and **Mrs. Gordon Lindquist** of Chicago; "also a brother **Conrad** Magli". The funeral was Tuesday from his home on State Street, Rev. E. F. Helmich officiating, "interred at Bayside". He farmed until about ten years earlier.
- ***Door County Advocate*, Fri 15 Aug 1924, p. 1, "DROWNS SATURDAY — Fred Magle, 76, Walks Off Dock in Dark … Was Early Settler in Nasewaupee From Switzerland"** (doc `1e8fc801-90a4-4104-8e86-19a1ea0947dc/wsbd0000/20130111/00001494`, read from the image). He was night watchman at the Fruit Growers Canning company. The funeral was Tuesday afternoon **from the Shiloh church, with interment in the Shiloh cemetery**, Rev. E. F. Helmich. Survivors: wife and six children, **Emil, Carl, Oscar and Mrs. Vernon Olson of Sawyer; Mrs. Gordon Lindquist, Chicago; Ernest, of Green Bay**; and "a brother, **Godfried Magle**, of the Town of Sturgeon Bay". "**Mr. Magle was born in Switzerland in April, 1848, and came to this country with his parents when he was six years of age.** Fifty years ago in March, he was married to Miss Mary Johnson. For five years after the wedding Mr. and Mrs. Magle lived on a farm in **Nasewaupee**. After that for thirty-seven years, they resided on a farm at **Shiloh** and then retired to live in **Sawyer** eight years ago."
- **The two papers disagree:** the News says "brother Conrad" and burial "at Bayside"; the Advocate says "brother Godfried" and the Shiloh cemetery. Conrad Jr. died in 1899 and the stone is at Shiloh, so **the Advocate is right on both**.
- Children's graves at Shiloh (Find a Grave): **Emma F. Maegele, b. 31 Jan 1874, d. 22 Oct 1892, "child of F & M"** (#208336979); **Sophia, 13 May 1877–6 Jan 1878** (#208337073); **Julia, 4 Jul 1885–6 Mar 1886** (#208337033); Josephine (#208337115, no dates). Emma was born seven weeks before the 23 Mar 1874 marriage.

## Lead 6 — Lavassor

### Niagara County News, 22 Feb 1884 — CONFIRMED (page image read)
- **Niagara Falls Public Library card index** (FS "Vital record card index of Niagara County, New York", catalog koha:182741; film 1411835 = DGS 8125277, image 38; ark 3:1:3Q9M-CSVV-D9PH-7). The card reads "Lavassor, Mrs (nee Mary Hutchinson) / Death notice p 1 Feb 22, 1884 / NC News". The catalog's newspaper list shows that **"NC News" = *Niagara County News*, Youngstown, NY**.
- The page itself, NYS Historic Newspapers, *Niagara County News* (Youngstown), **22 Feb 1884, p. 1, under the heading "NIAGARA, Ont."**: "A short time since the death was announced in the 'Advocate,' a paper published at Sturgeon Bay, Wis., of Mrs. Lavassor, in the fifty-third year of her age. The maiden name of Mrs. Lavassor was Mary Hutchinson. **She was born in Niagara in the year 1831.** She was married to Joseph Lavassor in 1854, and with him moved to Sturgeon Bay in May of that year. Nearly sixty vehicles composed the funeral cortege. …" (https://www.nyshistoricnewspapers.org/?a=d&d=ncn18840222-01.1.1)
- **Significance:** the Niagara-on-the-Lake correspondent thought her death was local news, a month after the Advocate printed it. That implies she was still remembered, or had family, in the town of Niagara (Ontario). "Niagara West" in the Advocate therefore means the Niagara area of Canada West, i.e. Niagara-on-the-Lake or Niagara Township. **Her Niagara origin: CONFIRMED. Sister of Eliza: still PROBABLE (strengthened).** It now matches the FS tree's Niagara-on-the-Lake for Eliza, though nothing yet names a parent.
- **Other Hutchinson/Hutchison cards** in the same index (film 1411834 = DGS 8326635, images ~2427–2450): no Thomas, Sophia or Eliza. Possibly relevant (SPECULATIVE): **"Hutchison, Mrs John", death notice, NC News 2 Jun 1882, p. 3, more p. 5** (and Suspension Bridge Journal, 10 Jun 1882); **John Hutchinson**, death notices, Niagara Courier 20 Apr and 24 Aug 1889, Suspension Bridge Journal 27 Apr 1889; "Hutchison, Mr and Mrs J", birth of twins, NC News 3 Jun 1887. A John Hutchison family in the Youngstown area could be kin (a brother of Mary and Eliza?), but this is unproven.
